## KCR College Radio — San Diego State University, California

KCR's official Music Submissions page states that all musicians are encouraged to submit, with special interest in San Diego artists. It authorizes digital delivery through:

`kcr@sdsu.edu`

The station requires:

- FCC-clean songs;
- filenames formatted as `Artist – Song Title`;

Accepted tracks may be added to KCR's automated playlist between live shows and made available to the music department for DJ use.
