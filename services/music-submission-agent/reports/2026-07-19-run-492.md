## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact label and subgenre fit;
- private-link access, exclusivity and download permissions;
- release status and track selection;
- composition, master, sample, feature and contributor rights;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- AI-assisted or AI-generated eligibility;
- privacy, retention and personal-data handling;
- post-selection contract, licence, royalty and termination terms;
- international eligibility where not explicit;
- the final email or form action.

MelodicLab additionally requires the private link to be downloadable and exclusive. Another Life Music requires emotional or melancholic progressive-house fit. Plazma additionally requires human CAPTCHA completion, the honeypot to remain untouched, verification of the invitation, pricing, payment/refund conditions and a distinction between a conventional label agreement and the paid release-service package.
