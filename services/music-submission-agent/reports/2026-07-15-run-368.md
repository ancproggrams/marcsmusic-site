## 2. 101.5 UMFM / CJUM — Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada

### Authorized routes

UMFM's official Music Department FAQ accepts digital submissions through a secure downloadable link. Accepted examples include:

- WeTransfer;
- Dropbox;
- Google Drive;
- Bandcamp;
- DISCO;
- embedded links.

UMFM explicitly rejects individual MP3 or other audio-file attachments by email.

Physical CDs may be mailed or dropped off to the published Music Director address. Physical singles are not accepted. EPs must contain at least four songs.

The FAQ states that digital submissions must be sent through its official Program Director email action. It identifies Michael Elves as Program Director, but the email destination is Cloudflare-protected in passive rendering. The address was not decoded, inferred, guessed or stored.
