## Verification notes

- KEXP was verified from its official Submission Guidelines page. The route asks artists to digitally submit music to the Music Director/Music Department path with streaming and WAV download links, metadata, clean edits for FCC issues, lyrics, credits, bio/one-sheet and no attachments or physical submissions.
- WFMU was verified from its active July 2026 site and official Send Music page. The route accepts vinyl, CD, CD-R and cassette review materials addressed to the Music Director by postal or courier address.
- NTS was verified from its active July 2026 homepage, support articles and Radio Show Proposal form. NTS is open to show submissions through a public form, while the separate Supporter Radio mix route requires becoming an NTS Supporter.
- WXPN was verified from its active July 2026 site and official How to Reach Us page. The route asks for a digital EPK and download link for airplay consideration, plus a separate Local Show path with WAV/high-quality MP3, short bio and upcoming show information.
- Atwood Magazine was verified from its active July 2026 site and official Pitching Us page. The page welcomes music from around the world and gives editorial pitch requirements, direct music-link guidance, one-follow-up limits and premiere timing requirements.
