## Verification

MOCRadio publishes a dedicated first-party submission page for its R&B, Hip-Hop and unsigned/indie programming. The page instructs artists to send music to `mp3@mocradio.com` and requires a radio or clean version, MP3 delivery, `Artist - Song Title` filename/tag formatting and Twitter information.

The standard editorial-review route has no published fee. The same page mentions separate promotional packages offering guaranteed exposure and live interviews. Those paid services were treated as an adjacent payment boundary and excluded from the free submission queue entry.

Current activity was verified from MOCRadio's first-party home page and program/music-director page, which publish an active weekly schedule and current recurring shows. Supporting external podcast evidence lists a MOCRadio episode aired on June 20, 2026.

The first-party advertising page lists Adult Contemporary R&B, House, EDM, Hip-Hop, Indie Music, Reggae and Slow Jams. This creates a plausible fit for selected clean MarcsMusic electronic, house, reggae and crossover tracks after human track-level review.

## Manual-review boundaries

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because:

- the final action is a human-composed email with an MP3 attachment;
- the track must be a radio or clean version;
- filename and metadata must follow `Artist - Song Title`;
- master, composition, sample and contributor rights require confirmation;
- AI-assisted and fully generated music eligibility is not published;
- international artist eligibility is not explicitly published;
- attachment-size, track-count, response-time and airplay-rights terms are not published;
- optional paid promotional packages must remain outside the free editorial-review route;
- the final email and attachment require explicit owner approval.

No login, CAPTCHA or payment is required for the standard public email route.
