It requests artist biography, official website, social, audio and purchase links where available.

## Manual-review boundaries

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because:

- the submission page grants OurTownRadio rights to use submitted music for airplay and promotion;
- the Terms of Service describe a non-exclusive, non-transferable hosting right for public airplay and advertising promotions;
- territory, duration, revocability and promotion outside the station require human review;
- international artist eligibility is not explicit;
- AI-assisted and fully generated music eligibility is not published;
- ownership, samples, contributors and cover-song rights must be confirmed;
- the final email or file-transfer action requires explicit owner approval.

No login, CAPTCHA or payment requirement is published for the email route. No form was identified. Optional physical CD delivery is retained as an alternative channel inside the same canonical opportunity rather than as a duplicate.
