## Resurge Audio verification

Resurge Audio publishes a first-party electronic-music submission form for house, tech house and techno. The form asks for the artist's legal name, alias, country/location, label and email; channel and consideration choices; streamable or downloadable music links; release status; social and press assets; and explicit rights, submission-agreement and GDPR confirmations.

The published submission terms reject music generated wholly or partly using artificial-intelligence tools. They require the submitter to control the music and promotional assets and grant Resurge Audio a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free promotional licence across its websites, YouTube, playlists, editorial and social channels. Ownership remains with the rights holder, but the licence scope, Content ID interaction, withdrawal and rights clearance require legal review.

`music@resurgeaudio.com` is first-party published, syntactically valid, domain-aligned and expressly designated for music submissions. The official privacy page says email submissions must contain streamable links and that attachments or download-only email submissions will not be reviewed. The canonical web form and official music mailbox were consolidated into one opportunity.

Current activity was verified through 2026 editorial posts, current house/tech-house and Irish electronic playlists, and submission, contact, terms and privacy pages revised in February 2026.

## Joshua Tree Live verification

Joshua Tree Live publishes a free first-party media-submission form for independent artists worldwide. The visible form asks for the artist or band name, song title, contribution type, description, email, optional social tag and website/QR destination. It accepts MP3 or WAV audio and MP4 video with a published maximum of 500 MB. Selected music may enter the morning or afternoon audio programming, and selected artists may be invited to a free live show or interview.

The platform states that artists keep their ownership and revenue and that it does not take a cut or sell submitted data. Its general terms and privacy policy similarly state that creators retain ownership. The music page, however, does not publish a detailed broadcast, promotional, archival, retention or withdrawal licence, so those permissions require manual review.

The official contact, terms and privacy pages publish `media@joshuatreelive.tv`. An About page separately shows `media@joshuatreelive.io`; the cross-domain address was treated as inconsistent and was not used or verified. The form remains the canonical submission route.

Activity was verified through the current 2026 streaming schedule, artist and video listings, live web/Roku distribution and the active media-submission page. The submission page and current schedule show differing morning-program times, which is retained as a manual-review discrepancy.

## Global Entry Radio verification

Global Entry Radio is an international syndicated melodic-electronic programme broadcasting through more than 40 FM, DAB, television and digital outlets in at least 15 countries. Its official site says that only a limited number of submissions are reviewed each month and links submissions exclusively to a specific Groover curator route.

The official programme archive showed episode 100 dated 3 July 2026 and episodes 97–99 from April through June 2026. The suitable styles include melodic house, progressive house, deep house and melodic techno.

The linked route is an external Groover campaign. Groover requires an artist account/login and paid Grooviz credits. Official Groover documentation currently describes one Grooviz as €1 and a normal curator contact as two Grooviz, while selected top curators may cost more. The exact price for the Global Entry Radio curator must therefore be checked within the authenticated campaign flow. Payment guarantees listening and feedback, not airplay or publication.

Global Entry Radio does not publish a direct submission email. Its general contact form is for syndication and partnership enquiries and was not substituted for the linked Groover route. `support@groover.co` is platform support rather than a submission mailbox.
