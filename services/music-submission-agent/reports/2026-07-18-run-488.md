Official route: the first-party demo page and `demos@subculture050.com`.

- a downloadable private SoundCloud playlist with downloads enabled;
- track titles in `Artist – Track Title (Demo/WIP)` format;

- finished tracks or works in progress.

## 2. Pangea Recordings

Official route: `pangea@pangearecordings.com`.

Pangea accepts demos year-round from artists and labels worldwide for:

- progressive house;
- melodic techno;
- deep techno and related underground electronic music.

The official guidance recommends 1–3 finished or near-finished tracks, a private streaming or download link, a brief introduction and no unsolicited large attachment. Its 2026 editorial guidance says the label is not looking for AI-generated music, so origin must be reviewed before contact.

Current activity is confirmed by the January 16, 2026 demo guide, podcast episode 131 from April 2026 and editorial updates through May 2026.

## 3. Symmetry Recordings

Official route: `demos@symmetryrecordings.co.uk`.

The drum-and-bass label accepts:

- 320kbps MP3 or WAV;
- email attachments or a storage/cloud link;
- files labelled with producer and track name;
- SoundCloud links only when downloading is enabled.

The official Bandcamp profile also links a LabelWorx DemoBox. Its complete fields, JavaScript behavior, upload limits, consent, CAPTCHA and login state were not passively inspected, so it remains an adjacent manual boundary rather than an automated route.

Current activity is confirmed by releases in January, March, May and June 2026, including *SYMM074 – So Right* on June 5, 2026.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact label and genre fit;
- track or playlist selection;
- private-link access, download permissions and expiry;
- file quality, naming, attachment security and upload limits;
- composition, master, sample and contributor rights;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- AI-assisted or AI-generated eligibility;
- privacy, retention, consent and hidden controls;
- international eligibility where not explicit;
- the final email, form or portal action.

Pangea additionally has a hard AI-origin check. SubCulture requires a 2027 release-window and preferred multi-track-playlist check. Symmetry requires inspection of the external LabelWorx portal if that alternative is used.
