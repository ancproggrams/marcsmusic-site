## SoundChat Radio verification

SoundChat Radio publishes a dedicated first-party submission form for Caribbean artists and describes the standard route as completely free. The page advertises global exposure in more than 100 countries and a 24/7 broadcast.

The published guidelines request high-quality audio, with 320 kbps MP3 or WAV preferred; a radio edit or clean version alongside explicit material where applicable; correct artist, track and genre metadata; a short biography; and social links. The visible form asks for artist name, email address, track title, genre, a SoundCloud, Spotify, YouTube or direct-download track link, and information about the artist or track.

The page states that submission confirms ownership of the music rights and grants SoundChat Radio permission to broadcast the track. This is a rights-bearing consent and requires human approval before submission.

Current activity was verified from the live first-party weekly schedule, 24/7 radio pages, current rotation and featured artists, and a dated SoundChat story from March 16, 2026.

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because a human must select a suitable clean Caribbean-format track, verify metadata, biography and external-link permissions, confirm composition, master, sample and contributor rights, review the broadcast grant, confirm the unpublished AI-music policy, inspect hidden form controls and approve the final submission.

## All'It Radio verification

All'It Radio publishes an official first-party submission process for Hip Hop, R&B, Reggae and Urban music and links to an external Google Form. The route requests one downloadable high-quality MP3 or WAV file at 44.1 kHz and 16-bit stereo plus cover artwork. The station says that email submissions are not accepted.

A Gmail account is explicitly required. The linked `forms.gle` route returned HTTP 401 Unauthorized in this runtime. The authentication boundary was not bypassed, so the complete form fields, privacy text, upload limits, consent wording and any Google anti-abuse controls remain unverified.

Selected tracks enter the station's “All It or Not at All” voting page for one week. The published process says a track needs 100 “All It” votes from valid email addresses to reach regular rotation and that the artist must follow the station on Instagram or Facebook. Submission also authorizes All'It Radio to broadcast the music if selected.

Activity was verified from the official 24-hour station description and current weekly shows, including Friday Night Warm Up and multiple Saturday programmes with named hosts.

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because Gmail authentication, direct audio and cover-art uploads, broadcast permission, audience voting and a social-follow condition require human decisions. International eligibility, AI-music eligibility, complete form terms, media rights, hidden controls and every final upload and submit action remain unresolved.
