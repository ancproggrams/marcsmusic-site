## Verification notes

- CJSR: official Submit Music page lists Music Department physical delivery and music@cjsr.com for digital albums/EPs; it rejects single tracks, paid streaming links and expiring transfer links.
- CFUV: official Music Submissions page lists music@cfuv.ca, requires at least three unique tracks, requests digital download links, prefers 320 kbps MP3s and limits physical submissions to local/special cases.
- CJAM: official Submit page says CJAM has moved to digital-only submissions, lists cjammd@gmail.com, rejects Spotify links and direct MP3 attachments, and requires four or more original tracks plus ID3/file-quality checks.
- CFRU: official Music page lists music@cfru.ca, requires EPs/albums with at least three songs, high-quality MP3 download links and metadata, and exposes a separate local-musician form.
- Radio Western: official temporary page states the main site is impaired but the station stream remains available, warns not to send submissions to the general address, and routes new music/promo to music@radiowestern.ca.

## Manual-review reasons

All five routes remain manual-review only. This batch includes official public music/business email routes, physical mail/drop-off routes, one local upload/download-link form, a programmer-only login resource that must not be used, and a local paid-advertising side-route that must remain separate from free editorial/radio submission. Manual review is required for email copy, metadata, album/EP and track-count eligibility, ID3 tags, language warnings, download-link permissions, no-expiry link checks, physical package preparation, local eligibility, paid-route avoidance and station/library fit.
