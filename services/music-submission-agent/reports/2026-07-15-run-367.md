## 1. CJSR FM 88.5 — Edmonton, Alberta, Canada

### Authorized routes

CJSR's official Submit Music page authorizes digital albums and EPs through:

- `music@cjsr.com`

Digital delivery must provide files rather than paid-streaming-only links. The page accepts:

- Bandcamp download codes;
- Dropbox;
- Google Drive;
- comparable stable cloud-storage links.

CJSR advises against expiring links such as WeTransfer because the release may not be reviewed before the link expires.

Physical submissions are also authorized and may include CDs, vinyl, cassettes or other playable media sent to the published Music Department address. The station gives customs guidance for CDs sent from outside Canada, confirming an international physical route.

CJSR does not accept single tracks. Only albums or EPs should be submitted.

A complete submission should include:

- artist or band name;
- album or EP title;
- complete track names;
- track lengths;
- artist origin;
- identified focus tracks;
- a concise one-sheet;
- warnings for explicit language;
- tagged MP3 metadata containing track title, track number, artist and album.

Official evidence:

- Website: `https://www.cjsr.com/`
- Submission instructions: `https://www.cjsr.com/submit-music/`
- AI policy: `https://www.cjsr.com/about-cjsr/ai-policy/`
- Contact page: `https://www.cjsr.com/contact/`
- Current chart: `https://www.cjsr.com/2026/07/chart07072026/`

### AI-origin policy

CJSR's AI policy was updated in January 2026. It permits AI as a tool with human review and oversight but states that the station will not use or air music wholly generated by AI.

Before outreach, a human must determine whether the selected MarcsMusic release satisfies this policy. No unsupported production-origin declaration should be made.

Selected MarcsMusic electronic, experimental, ambient, global, reggae, dub and hip-hop-adjacent releases therefore have a plausible catalogue fit without implying acceptance or airplay.

Official evidence:
