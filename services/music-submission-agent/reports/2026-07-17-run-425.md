## Brum Radio — Birmingham, United Kingdom

Brum Radio's official Submit Music page accepts one track as MP3 at 192 kbps or higher or WAV. The station prefers a radio edit and asks artists to keep the track under 4 minutes 30 seconds where possible.

The main playlist is restricted to artists based in Birmingham or the West Midlands. Music from elsewhere is expressly accepted for specialist-show consideration, although international eligibility is not stated in unambiguous terms.

The submission page rejects ordinary Spotify and YouTube links. Bandcamp or SoundCloud press links are acceptable only when downloads are enabled.

## Submission form

The dedicated first-party form requests:

- name;
- email address;
- band or artist name;
- base postcode;
- track title;
- release date;
- confirmation that the track is radio friendly and does not contain explicit lyrics;
- one uploaded song;
- an optional press-track link;
- notes for the Music Team.

Passive inspection exposed no visible CAPTCHA, account login or payment field. A human must recheck the live form immediately before submission and stop if any dynamic anti-spam, browser-verification, authentication or payment control appears.

## Manual-review boundary

A human must confirm:

- one clean, radio-ready MarcsMusic track;
- a currently active specialist show with a credible genre match;
- whether an artist based in the Netherlands is eligible;
- MP3 encoding at 192 kbps or higher or the station's preferred WAV specification;
- maximum upload size;
- runtime, filename, embedded metadata and master quality;
- that any Bandcamp or SoundCloud link has downloads enabled;
- composition, master, sample and contributor rights;
- release-recency expectations;
- the current AI-assisted and AI-generated music policy;
- whether to use the form or email route.

Only one route should be used for a release. The form and mailbox must not be used for duplicate servicing.
