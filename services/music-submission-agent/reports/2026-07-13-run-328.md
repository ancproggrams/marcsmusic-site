- Authorized route: the official contact page says demo submissions are always welcome and instructs artists to select **Demo Submission** in the first-party contact form.
- Delivery guidance: 320 kbps is preferred; the passive page does not reveal the complete live delivery fields, upload limits, link rules, consent text or rights declarations.
- Human-verification boundary: the official page states that the site is protected by Google reCAPTCHA.
- Cost and access: no submission fee, account, login or payment requirement is stated.
- Label and genre fit: Critical Music is an underground drum-and-bass/electronic label with strong relevance to MarcsMusic jungle, drum-and-bass, bass and experimental electronic material.
- Activity: the official releases catalogue is live and currently exposes entries including CRIT298, CRIT273, CRIT275, CRIT279 and other active catalogue releases.
- Business-email verification: `info@criticalmusic.com` is published for press/general enquiries and `badger@criticalmusic.com` for label management and operations. Neither is authorized as a demo mailbox, so neither was queued for submission.
- Manual boundary: a human must select the Demo Submission subject, inspect the current fields and terms, complete reCAPTCHA, choose the track, verify rights and approve the pitch and metadata.

### Shogun Audio

- Authorized current route: the official site navigation labels a Shogun-specific LabelRadar portal as **Demos**.
- Authorized legacy route: the official contact page also instructs artists to submit through `shogunaudio.label-engine.com/demos`.
- Legacy form details: the Label Engine form requests artist name, real name and email, accepts up to five MP3 tracks, offers optional social-profile fields and permits a message of up to 500 characters.
- Reliability concern: the passive legacy form rendered unresolved `{{demo.NAME}}` template placeholders, so its operational state cannot be trusted without a manual browser check.
- Account/pricing boundary: the current LabelRadar path is a third-party portal and must be reviewed for account/login requirements, current submission allowances, optional paid features, terms and consent before use.
- Activity: Shogun's live 2026 site presents current releases and a Shogun Sessions event scheduled for 26 September; the site footer is current to 2026.
- Business-email verification: `shop@shogunaudio.co.uk`, `info@shogunaudio.co.uk` and `licensing@shogunaudio.co.uk` are publicly assigned to shop, general-enquiry and licensing purposes. None is authorized for demo delivery.
- Manual boundary: a human must determine which official portal is current, review all login, pricing, upload, rights and consent terms, select a suitable drum-and-bass track and perform the submission manually.
