## Verification and enrichment

- Gravitas Recordings: the official demo page says every submission is listened to, accepts unreleased music and strongly prefers a mastered private SoundCloud playlist share link. The form asks for artist and sender details, email, demo links, whether the music has been sent to other labels, artist context and optional social profiles. Its contact page explicitly redirects demo senders to this dedicated form. Final-master status, rights, exclusivity disclosure, private-link permissions and the live form must remain manual.
- Quality Goods Records: the official homepage links its Demos destination to a public Airtable form. The label describes its scope as electronic trap, future bass, future beats and experimental bass and says artist size or location is not the main criterion. The static form view does not expose the complete fields, link or file rules, rights declarations, consents, limits or runtime controls, so the Airtable workflow requires human inspection before any data or audio is supplied.
- Soma Records: the official contact page exposes a dedicated demo mailbox through a protected/non-plaintext link. Soma says it is not currently looking to sign new artists but will listen, requests a SoundCloud link containing the best two tracks plus a short message, and rejects download links, MP3s and all attachments. The official site also shows current 35th-anniversary and 700th-release activity. The protected address was not decoded and must be opened and verified manually.
- Loci Records: the official contact page provides name, email, phone and message fields but no explicit current unsolicited-demo policy, audio field or delivery format. Only a short asset-free routing inquiry is queued. The official site carries a 2026 copyright notice and displays active releases across downtempo, chill and organic electronic music.
- CloudKid: the official legal imprint identifies CloudKid GmbH in Berlin and publishes `hello@cldkid.com` as a general business contact. The homepage describes CloudKid as a label discovering and developing new artists and displays featured releases, but no complete unsolicited-demo procedure is published. Only a short asset-free inquiry asking for the current authorized demo route is queued.
