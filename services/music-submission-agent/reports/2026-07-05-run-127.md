## Verification and enrichment notes

- WPRB: official public research confirmed a music-submission route for artists, labels and promoters using CDs, CD-Rs, LPs, 12-inch, 10-inch and 7-inch records. The page lists a Music Director postal route and states the public music email route can also be used, while physical submissions are prioritized.
- WMBR: official public research confirmed an active MIT freeform station with a Music Director physical mailing route, unsigned-band acceptance, no-MP3-review guidance, one-sheet guidance and genre contacts including RPM/Techno, World and Hiphop. Protected image-based contacts were not decoded.
- CFUV: official public research confirmed a Music Director route for all formats including electronic and world, with music@cfuv.ca, at least three unique tracks, 320 kbps MP3 digital download links, proper track listing and a one-page biography. Physical routes are limited side routes.
- CIUT: official public research confirmed an active University of Toronto campus/community station with public new-music submission routing to newmusic@ciut.fm and relevant 2026 programming including electronic/ragga-jungle and geographically diverse contemporary music.
- KTRU: official public research confirmed active Rice University freeform radio, a public department-selector contact form with Music Director as an option, and physical-only music submission instructions for CD, LP and 7-inch formats through USPS or UPS.

## Deduplication
