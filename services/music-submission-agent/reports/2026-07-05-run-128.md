## Verification and enrichment notes

- KALX: official public research confirmed Music Director contact routes and strict physical-only submission requirements for professionally pressed CDs or LPs. Digital downloads, file transfers, Bandcamp, Spotify, YouTube, CD-R and other digital-only routes are not accepted.
- Radio K / KUOM: official public research confirmed current station activity and a Submitting Music route supporting physical submissions plus digital music-department links. The page prefers lossless WAV or FLAC files, accepts Bandcamp download code/free-link options and asks for simple one-sheet and FCC-friendly track guidance.
- WXYC: official public research confirmed an active UNC-Chapel Hill freeform station with current 2026 programming/news, a public station contact route and a Music Department mailing address.
- WREK: official public research confirmed active Georgia Tech radio programming and a public How To Submit Music route allowing snail mail or Music Director email submissions, with CDs and LPs having a better chance of airplay.
- WRAS Album 88: official public research confirmed active schedule/current-rotation routes and a Contact page listing Regular Rotation, RPM and Urban music contacts. WRAS asks for local Atlanta or generally underground music released in the past four months.

## Deduplication

Repository searches for KALX, Radio K / KUOM, WXYC, WREK and WRAS Album 88 returned no existing matching music-submission-agent run entries before creation.
