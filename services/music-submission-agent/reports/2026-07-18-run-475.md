## The Relay Station verification

The Relay Station publishes a dedicated first-party artist form for independent and signed ambient, atmospheric and neoclassical artists from around the globe. It is a weekly 59-minute programme distributed by email, Substack, PRX and evergreen direct-download syndication.

The route accepts unused Bandcamp codes or non-expiring direct-download links that do not require an account. Spotify links are prohibited. High-quality 320 kbps or variable-bitrate MP3 files with complete metadata are preferred. Submitters must own or control distribution rights and prevent automated Content ID or distributor takedowns.

Genre fit is narrow: prominent drums, vocals, electronica, techno, house, noise, solo piano and deeply experimental material are generally rejected. The statement of consent grants Michael Tangen and Stolace Productions free-and-clear permission for The Relay Station, Ambient Discourses, syndicated programming, digital shorts and reels, archives, monetized platforms, promotional materials, advertisements and other produced content. The programme states that it does not pay mechanical royalties, although participating stations may report public performances through standard agencies.

Current activity was verified through Season 7 Episode 07.22, broadcast May 31, 2026, and weekly archive entries dated May 24 and May 17, 2026.

## rBeatz verification

rBeatz publishes an official free four-step submission form for singles and albums. The form collects contact identity, phone, social links, postal address, country, release data, genre, label, optional ISRC, AI-generation status and a chosen rBeatz network station. It then requires a direct audio upload and digital signature.

The station options include rBeatz Radio, ROQ Charlotte, Oui Global, 25/8/366 Global, USA Heroes, Bonfire, Oui Talk Raw and The Vessel. The network publishes active 24/7 global streams across Hip-Hop, R&B, electronic, house, global music, pop, rock and gospel-oriented formats. Submission is free and airplay is not guaranteed.

The mandatory Durable Licensing Agreement is a major legal boundary. It grants a non-exclusive, transferable, sublicensable, royalty-free, perpetual—or in some jurisdictions maximum-term-plus-twenty-years—irrevocable and fully paid universal license. Published uses include hosting, streaming, copying, public performance, synchronization, promotion, advertising, archiving, critique, ranking, translation, modification, derivative works, distribution and use of the submitter's name, voice, image, signature, performance and likeness. It also contains warranties, waiver and indemnity provisions and incorporates linked terms.

Activity was verified through the current rBeatz Radio Live page, which describes a no-login 24/7 global stream, independent-artist programming, multiple network stations and a 2026 copyright notice.

## PDX Radio verification

PDX Radio publishes a dedicated no-cost song-submission page for independent Hip-Hop, R&B and related subgenres. The station streams worldwide and its content policy explicitly permits international submissions from artists, producers, labels, managers and other authorized rights holders.

The published requirements include an adult authorized submitter, a final master rather than a rough draft, accurate official title, primary and featured artist names, and either an ISRC or album and label data. Submitters are responsible for song splits, metadata, PRO and SoundExchange registration where relevant, samples, featured performers and all underlying rights.

The page states that submission is not pay-to-play and that accepted music may become eligible for rotation, listener requests, chart movement and related artist statistics. If selected, the artist grants a non-exclusive, revocable license for broadcast, livestream, affiliated-platform and promotional use. The FAQ states that AI-generated music is not accepted and submissions are not used for AI training. The broader policy says AI-assisted material may require disclosure and additional review.

The embedded intake form rendered only as an iframe/loading boundary during passive retrieval, so its complete fields, provider, audio limits and live anti-abuse controls were not inspected. `info@getpdxradio.com` is first-party published and domain-aligned, but it is a general contact rather than a replacement submission mailbox. The separate livestream-only New Music Monday/Nero route and paid interview or media services were excluded.

Activity was verified through the official about page, which states that PDX Radio has streamed independent Hip-Hop and R&B worldwide 24/7 since 2019, and the active weekly New Music Monday page listing live reviews on Mondays from 6–8 PM Pacific. The content policy was updated April 1, 2026.
