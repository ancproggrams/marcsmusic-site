## Verification and enrichment notes

- KFAI was verified through its official homepage and contact page. The contact page invites artists to share music with the KFAI Music Department and Music Library through an official public email route, while the site shows active programming and 2026 footer signals.
- WNCW was verified through its official homepage and Submit Music page. The page says WNCW prefers CD submissions but accepts digital WAV/MP3 files via Dropbox or Google Drive links, asks for one-sheet details and notes that DJs review music without an airplay guarantee.
- Radio Boise was verified through its official homepage/navigation and Submit Your Music page. The homepage links to Submit Your Music and shows current 2026 activity; the submission page was a public route but only exposed a loading boundary in static fetch, so it remains manual.
- KGNU was verified through its official homepage and Contact Us page. The contact page includes a Music department contact-form selector and reCaptcha v3, while department email routes were protected/redacted and were not decoded or guessed.
- KBCS was verified through its official homepage and staff/contact pages. The site showed current 2026 activity and public music-related staff/contact routes including Music Director, Music Librarian and music-request contacts; because no complete static auto-submit policy was found, outreach remains manual.
