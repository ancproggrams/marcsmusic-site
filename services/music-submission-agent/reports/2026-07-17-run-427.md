## Camaradio 101.3 FM — Camarillo and Ventura County, California

Camaradio's official music-submission page accepts one song for possible airplay consideration. The form allows either a downloadable music-file link or a direct audio upload.

The station requires WAV or MP3 audio in 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz, 16-bit stereo. Direct uploads must be under 10 MB; larger files must be supplied through a link.

The page states that submission does not guarantee airplay and that Camaradio does not provide reviews or critiques.
