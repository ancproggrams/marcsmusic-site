## Verification notes

- PBS 106.7FM: official Submit Your Music page invites local and international acts, prefers the official digital form, requires stream and high-quality download links, shows a CAPTCHA control, and also lists physical CD/vinyl mail or reception handover options.
- 2SER: official contact page lists submitmusic@2ser.com for Music Director airplay consideration and says not to send physical copies unless requested; the How to Submit Music page exposes a public airplay-consideration form.
- Triple R: official Submit Music page says the station is not playlisted, gives direct presenter servicing and station-level servicing routes, lists music@rrr.org.au, and provides physical Music Department mailing details.
- FBi Radio: official Music Submissions page asks musicians, bands, producers and DJs to submit digitally to music@fbiradio.com with stream and high-quality download links; it also separates paid promotional campaigns from editorial/radio submission.
- 4ZZZ: official Music Submissions page routes music to an external Music Department form, states submissions are considered for processing and digital-library inclusion, and rejects non-downloadable stream-only submissions.

All five routes remain manual-review only. This batch includes digital forms, official music/business email routes, physical-mail routes, an external form route, one CAPTCHA case and one paid-promotion side-route that must remain separate from free editorial/radio submission.
