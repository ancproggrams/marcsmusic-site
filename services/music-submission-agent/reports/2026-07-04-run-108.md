## Verification notes

- Music Connection was verified from an active 2026 site and its official New Music Critiques page. The page offers a free review consideration form, requires JavaScript, artist/contact details, music style, website and at least three streamable songs.
- SoundClick was verified from its official active site. The route invites artists to join free, upload unlimited songs or beats, sell beats or songs, configure artist pages and use built-in promotional tools, while requiring account/session handling.
- BandLab was verified from its public site, current reference material and trade coverage of its distribution rollout. The route is useful for creator publishing, hosting and distribution, but distribution is tied to a paid Membership boundary and the public app is JavaScript/session based.
- Radiooooo was verified from public reference sources that describe user uploads curated by DJs/producers before appearing on the global map. Its free-trial/subscription boundary and app workflow make this manual-only.
- Audius was verified from the Open Audio Foundation/Audius public pages and independent reporting. The Audius Music route is JavaScript app based, and artist posting/upload context requires account, rights, metadata and token/monetization review.

## Deduplication

Repository code search for Music Connection New Music Critiques, SoundClick, BandLab, Radiooooo and Audius returned no existing matching run entries before creation. The new rows were therefore treated as unique for this batch.
