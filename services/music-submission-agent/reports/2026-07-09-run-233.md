# Music Submission Agent — Run 233

Date: 2026-07-09

## Summary

Run 233 adds five public-authorized global music-promotion, curator-submission, DJ/radio promo-delivery and independent-blog submission routes for MarcsMusic.

## New opportunities

1. Groover Paid Curator Radio Label Playlist Submission Route
   - Route type: paid music-promotion platform for curators, playlists, press, radio, labels, bookers, managers and DJs.
   - Manual boundaries: account/login, campaign creation, song/demo upload or link, curator targeting, credit purchase, genre fit and paid-feedback/refund mechanics.

2. Playlist Push Spotify TikTok Campaign Curator Submission Route
   - Route type: paid Spotify playlist and TikTok creator promotion campaign route.
   - Manual boundaries: app account, campaign budget, track/snippet upload or link, creative brief, curator/creator network terms, cookie-consent and dashboard reporting.

3. DropTrack Artist Label DJ Radio Blog Promo Campaign Route
   - Route type: free-first/freemium promo-campaign platform for DJs, labels, playlist curators, blogs, radio and fans.
   - Manual boundaries: account/signup, track upload, supplied-contact and email-campaign permissions, playlist placement, AI press materials, analytics and paid Pro/ad-budget workflow.

4. Inflyte Promo Delivery DJ Radio Airplay Monitoring Route
   - Route type: paid promo-delivery, pre-save, DJ/radio/media delivery and airplay-monitoring route.
   - Manual boundaries: account/login, paid subscription or pay-as-you-go promo pack, audio/asset upload, contact targeting/import, RADAR monitoring, anti-piracy and Stripe/VAT payment terms.

5. Obscure Sound Independent Music SubmitHub Direct Submission Route
   - Route type: independent music blog submission route for indie, electronic, hip-hop, ambient, alt-R&B and related genres.
   - Manual boundaries: choose free direct email versus SubmitHub/MusoSoup/paid PR route, protected/non-plaintext email-link handling, subject-line compliance, streaming-link readiness, press photo rights and release-bio context.
