## 1. NOS Recordings

Official route: the first-party JavaScript demo form at `https://nosrecordings.com/demo-submissions`.

Published requirements:

- House music only;
- no more than three tracks;
- private SoundCloud links with downloads enabled;
- Box or Dropbox links are also accepted;
- do not attach files;
- include a brief professional introduction;
- submit finished, polished tracks;
- identify tracks that are still unmastered.

NOS also offers an optional opt-in for livestream A&R feedback on YouTube or Twitch. Because that may expose the demo publicly, the choice requires explicit human approval.

The form requires JavaScript and its complete fields, anti-spam controls and consent wording were not available in passive extraction. No public submission mailbox was identified or substituted.

Current activity is confirmed by the official catalogue, which lists 62 releases and 2026 titles including NOS 052, *NOS in Miami 2026*, and NOS 053, *Whatchu Need*, as out now.

## 2. Diepgraven Records

Official route: the first-party demo form at `https://www.diepgravenrecords.com/demo-submission`.

The Netherlands-based label seeks Chill, Tropical and Deep House. Its form requires:

- original content;
- no duplicate submission of the same track;
- a private streaming link;
- no WeTransfer or other download links;
- confirmation of complete copyright in the submitted music and artwork;
- disclosure of whether the track is a cover remix;
- disclosure of whether it is mastered.

Visible fields include name, email, private SoundCloud URL or Dropbox link, copyright confirmation, cover-remix status and mastering status.

`contact@diepgravenrecords.com` is first-party published, syntactically valid and domain-aligned, but it is described for questions and business inquiries. It was not substituted for the canonical form.

The official news page confirms current activity with 2026 releases dated 22 May, 5 June, 12 June and 3 July 2026.

## 3. COOL Recordings

Official route: the first-party form at `https://www.coolrecordings.com/`.

The Austrian label accepts:

- House;
- Tech House;
- Afro House;
- private SoundCloud or Dropbox links only;
- finished full tracks, not snippets;
- links with downloads enabled.

Visible fields are Artist Name, Direct Email, Track Link, Social Media Portfolio and A&R Note. The page says the label generally responds within two weeks when a track fits.

No public submission mailbox was identified or inferred.

Beatport confirms current label activity through 5 June 2026, including House, Tech House, Afro House and Melodic House releases.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact label and subgenre fit;
- private-link access, expiry and download permissions;
- composition, master, sample, remix, cover, artwork and contributor rights as applicable;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- AI-assisted or AI-generated music eligibility;
- privacy, retention and hidden anti-spam or JavaScript controls;
- prospective label-contract terms;
- the final form action.

NOS additionally requires review of the three-track maximum, finished-track status and optional public livestream-feedback consent. Diepgraven requires explicit copyright, cover/remix and mastering confirmations. COOL requires a finished full track and download-enabled private link.
