## Verification and enrichment

- KFAI: the official contact page explicitly invites artists to share music with its Music Department. The address is presented through a protected link and was not decoded. The page warns that receipt may not be confirmed because of submission volume.
- WMNF: the official submission page provides a public upload form and publishes `musicrelations@wmnf.org`. The form accepts MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, AIFF, WMA and M4A, with a maximum of five files and 50 MB per file.
- WPKN: the official contact page labels a dedicated Music Submission route to the Music Director and permits physical submissions for library or airplay consideration. The digital address is protected/non-plaintext.
- WERU: the official staff page publishes Denis Howard as Music Director at `denis@weru.org`. Because no current delivery policy is published, the queue item is limited to a pre-submission inquiry without music assets.
- KRCL: the official policy accepts professionally pressed physical CDs, rejects demos and CD-Rs, states that submitted copies become station property and limits its digital upload form to Utah-based artists. MarcsMusic is therefore queued only for the physical route.

Official activity was verified from current station pages.
