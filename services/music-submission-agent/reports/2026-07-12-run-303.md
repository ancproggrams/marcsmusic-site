## Verification and enrichment

- Indiemono: the official submission page provides a free editorial system organized by genre boxes and says one submission is enough because the team routes accepted music internally. It also provides a separate route for unreleased demos. Indiemono explicitly rejects direct email or social-media submissions, discourages resubmitting the same rejected track and states that it does not charge for playlisting. The site shows 2026 editorial activity and weekly social updates. A cookie-consent boundary and live form details require human review.
- alexrainbirdMusic: the official site links to a free District submission portal, says every submission is listened to and promises an Approval or Decline email after review. SubmitHub is listed as an alternative. The portal is dynamically rendered and exposed only a loading shell in the static check, so fields, account requirements, asset rules and runtime protections must be inspected manually. The official site publishes `alexrainbirdmusic@gmail.com` as a business contact, but it is not classified as an alternative music-submission route.
- BIRP!: the official About page says music submissions moved to Groover because of submission volume. The dedicated BIRP Groover route requires signup or login, lets artists choose music professionals, starts at approximately EUR 2 or USD 2 per contact and provides feedback or credit return when no response is received within the stated period. BIRP's homepage also displays a SubmitHub link, which must be confirmed live as a secondary option. The published `submit@birp.fm` address is specifically for contributor inquiries and is not a music-submission mailbox.
