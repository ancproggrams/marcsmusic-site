## 1. UKF / Pilot

Official route: `https://form.jotform.com/241682584698373`, reached from `https://ukf.com/submit-your-demo/`.

UKF's official demo page invites music for a UKF or Pilot label release. The public Jotform also supports consideration for an authorized upload to UKF's channels.

The form requests:

- a private SoundCloud link;
- upload versus unsigned label release;
- premiere/exclusive versus regular upload;
- Bass/Electro House, Chillout, Drum & Bass, Dubstep, Trap or another style;
- artist name and social profiles;
- free or paid release status;
- the intended UKF channel;
- self-release or label context;
- release and requested go-live dates;
- a press release or artist/track explanation;
- contact name and email.

For channel uploads, the form states that the submitter must have the label's permission and all rights, including samples, cleared. A UKF label-release submission must be unsigned.

The Jotform contains a visible `Should be Empty` honeypot field. It also offers an optional newsletter opt-in. No visible CAPTCHA or mandatory fee was found.

UKF's general website terms grant UKF and affiliated companies a non-exclusive, worldwide and perpetual right to use, reproduce, modify, adapt, publish, translate, distribute and display user submissions. Because those terms may apply broadly to submitted audio and materials, legal review is required before using the form.

`orders@ukf.com` is first-party store support and `dpo@aei.co.uk` is the official privacy contact. Neither is a music-submission route, and UKF explicitly says not to send demos to support.

Activity is current: UKF lists **T & Sugah — The Rush** dated **15 July 2026** and Pilot lists **YAANO — 4NOW4EVER** dated **9 July 2026**.

## 2. V Recordings / Liquid V / Chronic / Philly Blunt

Official route: `https://www.vrecordings.com/content/demos`.

The first-party page accepts demos for release consideration across:

- V Recordings;
- Liquid V;
- Chronic;
- Philly Blunt.

V Recordings says every submission is listened to, although it cannot reply individually to every unsuccessful submission.

The embedded demo box is not exposed in passive page extraction. A Databeats login and account-creation interface is present on the page, requesting first name, surname, email, password and acceptance of the Databeats terms and privacy policy. Whether the embedded demo box always requires an account could not be verified without interaction. The opportunity is therefore conservatively queued behind a login/session and incomplete-form-inspection boundary.

No mandatory demo fee or visible CAPTCHA was found. `support@databeats.com` is first-party published for platform, privacy and customer support, not demos. No public demo mailbox was found and no address was inferred.

Activity is current through the official Planet V feed, which published **V Podcast 178** on **8 May 2026** and several V Recordings tracks on **14 April 2026**. The first-party store also shows an active current catalogue.

## 3. Future House Music

Official route: `https://tstack.app/futurehousemusic`, linked from the official Future House Music website.

The Trackstack landing page lists House, Progressive House, Melodic House & Techno and additional related genres. It accepts:

- up to five tracks;
- MP3 or WAV files;
- direct drag-and-drop or file selection.

The page displays a one-week indication. Future House Music also links to its official Discord community and promotes monthly demo drops, live feedback sessions and related producer activities.

The Trackstack and Discord routes create account/session, availability, third-party terms, privacy and possible optional-feature or pricing boundaries. None was exercised. Current inbox capacity, the meaning of the one-week indicator and any downstream review or contract terms must be checked manually before upload.

`info@futurehousemusic.net` is published in the label's official privacy policy as a general/privacy contact. It is not a demo mailbox and does not replace Trackstack.

Activity is current: the official site promotes **22Bullets & Bruno Martini — Cobra** as FHM439, and Beatport dates the release to **10 July 2026**.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact label, imprint, genre and catalogue fit;
- link, upload, account or session requirements;
- master, composition, sample and contributor rights;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- generative-AI origin and disclosure;
- privacy, retention and third-party platform terms;
- post-selection contracts;
- every final form, upload, account, consent and submission action.

UKF additionally requires upload-versus-release selection, permission for labeled releases, premiere/exclusivity review, press material and legal review of broad user-content terms. V Recordings additionally has an unexposed embedded form and unresolved Databeats login/session boundary. Future House Music additionally has Trackstack/Discord account, availability and possible optional pricing boundaries.
