## Verification notes

- KRCL was verified from its active July 2026 site and official How to Submit Music page. The route supports physical CD submissions and a Utah-based local digital Google form, while making clear that DJs review music and that no reply or airplay is guaranteed.
- KXT was verified from its active July 2026 site and official Music Submissions page. The route uses an external Wufoo online form, asks for one song, requires a streaming link and downloadable WAV link, and includes authorization language for possible airplay.
- Radio K was verified from its active 2026 site, current rotation/new-addition pages and official Submit Music page. The route accepts physical and digital music packages with high-quality downloads, one-sheet/bio/contact details, FCC notes and suggested tracks.
- WREK was verified from the active WREK site and its official How To Submit Music page. The route lists snail mail and Music Director email, accepts common media formats, and says CDs and LPs have a better chance of airplay.
- WPRB was verified from active July 2026 schedules/playlists/news and its official Music Submissions page. The route lists a Music Director physical mailing address, accepted physical formats and a public music email while stating physical submissions are prioritized.
