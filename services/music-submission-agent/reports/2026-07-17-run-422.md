## Rights and agreement boundary

The form requires the submitter to confirm that the music is original and that they own or have the rights to distribute it.

Variety Vibes' official Terms of Service, last updated February 1, 2026, describes a legally binding agreement. It grants Variety Vibes a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual license to broadcast and stream submitted content, display it on the website and app, use the artist's name, likeness and biography for promotion, and distribute snippets or full versions through social media and marketing. The terms also contain a no-compensation acknowledgement unless a separate written agreement applies.

The form separately requires acceptance of a **Radio Airplay & Promotion Agreement** hosted on HiDrive. Passive access reached a JavaScript-dependent page, so the complete document was not read, accepted or bypassed.
