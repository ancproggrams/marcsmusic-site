## PBS 106.7FM verification

PBS FM's official form expressly welcomes music from local and international acts. It asks for contact and location details, artist name, biography, social links, optional gigs, a music stream and a high-quality downloadable 320 kbps AAC, MP3 or WAV equivalent. ## Brum Radio verification

Brum Radio's official form accepts one MP3 at 192 kbps or higher or WAV. A radio edit under 4:30 is preferred. Spotify and YouTube links are rejected; Bandcamp or SoundCloud links are acceptable only when downloads are enabled. The form asks for name, email, artist name, base postcode, track title, release date, a clean/radio-friendly confirmation, a direct audio upload, optional press link and notes.

The main playlist is limited to Birmingham and the West Midlands, but the page expressly states that artists from elsewhere are considered for specialist shows. The official show catalogue includes electronic, reggae, world, chill, bass and DJ-mix programming. The station exposes a current schedule and live player and recently advertised music-team and production roles.

## Radio Nano verification

Radio Nano's official submission page expressly invites local and international acts. It prefers its first-party form but also publishes `music@radionano.com` as an alternative. The form asks for name, email, role, a message containing the song URL and optional uploads. The email route may include an attached song or accessible online link, plus a one-page press release or biography.

The station requires permission from all rightsholders and contributors, including composers, lyricists, vocalists and musicians. The same requirement applies to cover songs and sampled music. Its privacy policy states that Contact Form 7 is used for forms and was updated in May 2026. Current activity was verified through May 2026 news and charts and a 2026 event calendar listing festivals through August.
