### Soundplate

- Authorized route: Soundplate's official playlist directory instructs artists to select a relevant Spotify or Deezer playlist and submit through that playlist's first-party flow.
- Cost: the standard playlist-submission service is explicitly described as free. Soundplate's advertising, smart-link, mastering and other marketing products are separate optional services.
- Scale and activity: the official directory says artists can submit to hundreds of Spotify curators, says new playlists are added every day and currently exposes live playlist pages, current label releases and 2026 editorial content.
- Genre fit: the active directory includes house/electronic/dance, techno, drum-and-bass, UK garage, bass, reggae/dancehall, chill/ambient, trip-hop/electronica/downtempo and synthwave categories.
- Fit control: playlist pages ask the artist to assess whether the track matches the playlist before submitting and may publish tempo, energy, danceability and genre indicators.
- Authentication boundary: the final Submit control routes to Spotify Accounts or Deezer Connect.
- Consent boundary: the inspected Garage & Bass route states that submission will automatically follow Soundplate and designated playlists. These actions must be reviewed and consciously authorized by the user.
- CAPTCHA/payment: no CAPTCHA or mandatory payment was visible on the inspected public flow, but OAuth, consent and any runtime controls remain fully manual.
- Email verification: no public music-submission mailbox was identified or inferred; the authorized route is the platform workflow.
- MarcsMusic fit: strong for garage, bass, drum-and-bass, electronic, house, techno, reggae/dancehall, ambient and downtempo material when matched to a specific playlist.

### DailyPlaylists

- Authorized route: the official homepage lets artists search for or paste a Spotify song and submit it to independent playlist curators.
- Cost: the free tier currently provides 10 Standard Submissions each week and access to a marketplace advertised as containing more than 18,000 playlists.
- Optional payment boundaries: extra Standard Submissions are listed at $7.99 per 10; Premium Credits at $19.99 per 10; the Professional tier is listed at $19.99 per month. None is required for the free Standard route.
- Account boundary: new users must create an account using email, Google or Facebook and then connect a Spotify account. Direct Spotify login has been removed, but Spotify linkage remains mandatory for submissions.
- Track restrictions: music must already be released on Spotify, cannot be submitted before release day and must be submitted as an individual song rather than an album or EP link.
- Editorial process: Standard Submissions enter the selected curator's queue. Free submissions do not guarantee a response, and the curator controls acceptance or rejection.
- Platform conduct: the FAQ states that curator promotion, payola or follow requirements can result in a ban.
- CAPTCHA/payment: no CAPTCHA was observed on the public pages. Account creation, Spotify API authorization, curator selection and optional payment boundaries remain manual.
- Business email: `info@dailyplaylists.com` is published on the official homepage, pricing page and FAQ. It was verified as a first-party support address and is not classified as a music-delivery mailbox.
- Current activity: the official pages display live marketplace statistics, current plans, an active FAQ and a 2026 site footer.
- MarcsMusic fit: broad; suitability depends on choosing individual curators whose playlists match the specific electronic, bass, reggae, pop or world-fusion release.
