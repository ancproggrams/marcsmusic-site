## 1. WHCR 90.3 FM

Official route: the first-party instructions at `https://whcr.org/music-submissions/`, which direct artists to select a matching music show and email an MP3 to that show's published address.

Relevant MarcsMusic-facing programmes include:

- DNB NYC Radio — Jungle/DNB/Breaks, `dnbnycradio@gmail.com`, explicitly featuring local and international artists;
- House in Harlem Radio — House, `tinad903@gmail.com`, featuring DJs from around the world;
- Mystic Vybes — deep, funky, jazzy and soulful house, `mysticvybes@gmail.com`.

WHCR also permits a show-addressed physical CD. Walk-in submissions are expressly prohibited.

Manual review is required for:

- exact show and recipient selection;
- current show acceptance and international eligibility;
- MP3 quality, metadata, attachment size and safe packaging;
- composition, master, sample, remix and contributor rights;
- AI-origin policy;
- broadcast, promotional, privacy and retention terms;
- follow-up timing and the final email or postal action.

The three show addresses are first-party published and syntactically valid but use Gmail, so WHCR-domain alignment cannot be established. No mailbox-level or deliverability probe was performed.

## 2. VANTA Recordings

Official route: the first-party demo form and `demo@vantarecordings.com`.

The German label seeks original Melodic House & Techno, Ethereal Techno, Progressive House and Deep House. The form requests:

- name;
- email;
- private SoundCloud link;
- social-media link.

The form is protected by Google reCAPTCHA. This control was identified but not completed or bypassed.

Manual review is required for label and subgenre fit, originality and release status, private-link access, all music and contributor rights, Content ID, AI eligibility, privacy/retention, possible contract terms and human completion of reCAPTCHA.

`demo@vantarecordings.com` is first-party published, syntactically valid, domain-aligned and explicitly designated for demos/promos. `paul@vantarecordings.com` is general contact only. Beatport lists current VANTA Recordings releases through 19 June 2026.

## 3. YHV Music Group / YHV Records

Official route: `demos@yhvmusic.group` and the embedded first-party form at `https://www.yhvmusic.group/demo-submission`.

The current YHV guidance covers Afro House, Afro Tech, Afro Tribal, Organic House, Melodic House, Progressive House, Indie Dance and Amapiano and states:

- unreleased demos only;
- WAV preferred;
- extended version required;
- selected demos are contacted by A&R.

An optional paid Groover curated-review route exists separately. It is not the canonical free direct route and requires separate account, price and payment review.

Manual review is required for unreleased status, extended-version availability, WAV/link delivery, exact sublabel fit, rights, Content ID, AI policy, embedded-form controls, privacy/retention, Groover separation, possible contract terms and the final submission.

`demos@yhvmusic.group` is first-party published, syntactically valid, domain-aligned and explicitly designated for demos. `info@yhvmusic.group` is general contact only. Beatport lists YHV Records releases through 17 July 2026, with further releases scheduled for 24 July 2026.
