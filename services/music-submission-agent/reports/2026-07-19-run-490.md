## 1. Get Known Radio

Official route: `https://getknownradio.com/#/artist-promotion`.

Get Known Radio accepts one MP3 at 320kbps minimum or WAV, a short note about the record, a genre tag, contact email and a rights-confirmation checkbox. It explicitly accepts non-US artists and covers club-leaning electronic music including drum and bass, house, UK garage, footwork, baile funk, amapiano and techno-adjacent records, alongside Hip-Hop and R&B.

The route is free and the station states that it has no pay-for-play, review-fee, follower-exchange or premium lane. Its terms grant a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence covering broadcast, streaming, promotion, distribution and reproduction across the station's website, live stream, social media, newsletter and marketing materials. The licence and indemnity language require legal review.

The privacy policy discloses Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise, possible public display of accepted track metadata, international processing and typical retention of submission records for the rotation period plus about two years. The first-party email fallback is published for accessibility or upload failures, but its protected address was not decoded. `support@getknownradio.com` is a first-party, syntactically valid and domain-aligned general address.

Activity is exceptionally current: the official home page is dated July 18, 2026, shows a 24/7 stream and recently submitted tracks; editorial posts run through July 17, 2026.

## 2. All'it Radio

Official route: `https://forms.gle/efv6J4nT4gAWtac68`, linked from `https://allitradio.com/`.

All'it Radio accepts one latest or strongest Hip-Hop, R&B, Reggae or Urban track as a downloadable high-quality MP3 or WAV at 44.1kHz/16-bit stereo, plus cover art. The official page states that a Gmail account is required and that only the form is accepted.

If selected, a track is placed on the `All It or Not at All` voting page for one week. It needs 100 positive votes to enter regular rotation, and the artist must follow the station on Instagram or Facebook. Submission authorizes broadcast if selected.

The external Google Form could not be passively loaded, so its exact fields, upload limits, storage, consent and CAPTCHA controls remain unresolved. `info@allitradio.com` and `hitus@allitradio.com` are first-party, syntactically valid and domain-aligned general contacts but are not submission substitutes.

Activity is supported by the live current-track area, 24/7-broadcast positioning and a current weekly schedule with Friday and Saturday shows.

## 3. Indie Global 365

Official route: `https://indieglobal365.com/music-submission/`.

The free first-party form accepts up to two Hip-Hop, R&B or Caribbean Reggae/Soca songs. Music must be positive, uplifting, motivational, conscious or feel-good. Files must be MP3, should be 192kbps, no more than 10MB each and named with the artist and song title. The visible form requests artist name, email, song titles, up to two audio files and one image per song, shows a 25MB aggregate upload indicator and requires acceptance of Terms & Conditions.

The linked full Terms & Conditions page could not be passively fetched. A legacy first-party page still says submissions are made through `indieglobal365@gmail.com`, while the current page says its form is the only route. The current form was kept as canonical and the Gmail address was retained only as historical context.

The separate membership page confirms that airplay submission is free and lists optional paid community plans. These plans are not required for the canonical review route.

Activity is supported by a live-radio/current-song area and active submission, membership, artist-directory and music-catalog pages.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact genre and station fit;
- track selection and audio specifications;
- audio and artwork upload handling;
- composition, master, sample, feature and contributor rights;
- broadcast and promotional-use permissions;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- AI-assisted or AI-generated eligibility;
- privacy, retention and international processing;
- terms, consent and hidden anti-spam controls;
- international eligibility where not explicit;
- the final form or email action.

Get Known Radio additionally requires legal review of its worldwide royalty-free licence and indemnity wording plus human completion of reCAPTCHA. All'it Radio requires Gmail authentication, complete external-form inspection, social following and fan-voting participation. Indie Global 365 requires full terms inspection, resolution of the legacy-email/current-form conflict and separation from optional paid memberships.
