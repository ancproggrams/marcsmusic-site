## 1. CJSW 90.9 FM — Calgary, Alberta, Canada

### Authorized route

CJSW's official Music Submissions page links directly to its current 2026 Jotform:

- Official guidelines: `https://cjsw.com/music/submit/`
- Authorized form: `https://form.jotform.com/260625704327253`

The station accepts digital music submissions only. Eligible releases must:

- be an EP or full-length release;
- contain at least three unique tracks;
- have been released within the previous three months;
- use MP3 files only;
- be uploaded as a complete ZIP archive.

Singles are expressly rejected.

The form requests:

- submitter email;
- artist or band name;
- release title;
- label name or self-released status;
- EP or full-length classification;
- official release date;
- general genre and optional subgenre;
- artist website or streaming URL;
- artist location;
- short biography or release description;
- RIYL information;
- full-release ZIP upload containing MP3 files;
- optional one-sheet upload, preferably PDF;
- album artwork upload;
- permission to share the release with programmers and permanently add it to the station's digital library.

The form includes a field labelled **Should be Empty**. This is an anti-spam control and was not populated, tested or bypassed.

## 2. CKUT 90.3 FM — Montreal, Quebec, Canada

### Authorized routes

CKUT states that its DJs choose recordings sent by artists and record companies from around the world.

Accepted physical formats include:

- CDs;
- cassettes;
- 12-inch vinyl;
- 7-inch vinyl.

CKUT also accepts digital submissions in 320-kbps MP3 format at:

- `music@ckut.ca`

Singles are not accepted. Except for 7-inch vinyl, submissions must contain at least three distinct tracks. Remixes do not count toward the three-track minimum.

Physical releases may be mailed or dropped off at the published CKUT Music Department address. The station notes that physical releases may receive more attention from programmers, but digital submissions are still evaluated under the same library criteria.

Official evidence:

- Website: `https://ckut.ca/`
- Submission instructions: `https://ckut.ca/submit-music-to-ckut/`
- Contact page: `https://ckut.ca/about/contact/`

### Activity and catalogue fit

CKUT's official site displayed a 2026 funding-drive notice, July 2026 station posts and archives, active schedule navigation and current programming. Its catalogue covers electronics, house, techno, ambient, experimental, avant-garde, global, reggae, dancehall and hip-hop, which creates potential fit for selected MarcsMusic releases.

### Business-email verification

The role mailbox `music@ckut.ca` is published both on CKUT's official submission instructions and in the Music Department section of the station's first-party contact page.

Verification covered:
