### Published delivery requirements

The official Music Submissions page requests:

- a clear subject such as `Artist name – track OR album/EP name`;
- a short biography;
- a press image and relevant upcoming-gig details when available;
- a stream link, which may be public or private;
- a separate free high-quality download link;
- 320 kbps MP3 or WAV audio;
- a reliable Dropbox, Google Drive, non-expiring WeTransfer or comparable link;
- download permission when SoundCloud is used for file delivery.

All music must be serviced digitally. FBi.radio no longer accepts CDs.

Music is auditioned on Wednesdays. The page recommends submission before midnight Tuesday for consideration in the following week's playlisting and states that Music Director submissions are normally listened to within two to three weeks.
