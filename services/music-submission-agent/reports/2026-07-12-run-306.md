## Verification and enrichment

- Protocol Recordings: the official demo page accepts unsigned original productions only through the embedded LabelRadar widget or its linked official portal. Bootlegs, mash-ups and promos are excluded. Protocol requires the demo to be offered exclusively to the label and states that, if interested, it will contact the artist within 30 days. The official contact page publishes separate press, licensing/sync and events contacts; the licensing address explicitly rejects unrelated inquiries, so none were treated as demo routes. The official site displays an active 2026 release catalogue, including `Like Home (Remixes 2026)`.
- Revealed Recordings: the public Demo navigation redirects to `login?return=%2Fdemo`, requiring an email/password account or optional Spotify connection before the actual demo workflow can be inspected. No demo fee is disclosed before sign-in. The official homepage shows current releases and pre-releases with availability windows extending into August 2026. The separate Pro subscription and paid store are adjacent commercial features and are not assumed to be required for demo submission.
- Monstercat: the official Contact Us page states that the label listens to every submitted demo and links dedicated LabelRadar routes for Uncaged/Instinct and Silk. A human must select the appropriate brand and inspect the live LabelRadar account, declarations and upload requirements. The homepage lists releases dated 2026-07-10 and 2026-07-03. General support, press and event emails are Cloudflare-protected on the official page; they were not decoded and are not authorized alternatives to the demo portals.
