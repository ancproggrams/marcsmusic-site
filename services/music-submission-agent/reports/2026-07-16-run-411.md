### Published submission requirements

KWDC requires:

- clean lyrics;
- no vulgar, discriminatory or explicit content;
- a completed music consent form;
- delivery through `kwdc@deltacollege.edu`;
- the subject `Music Submission`;
- sender first and last name;
- artist or performer name;
- song title or titles;
- a professional message describing the music;
- social-media handles and/or website;
- a signed consent attachment or manually completed digital-sign consent workflow;
- an MP3 file only;
- a stereo master at -3 dB.

KWDC states that a high volume of submissions may prevent a reply when music is not selected, and it reserves the right to disregard unprofessional emails.

### Consent-form and protected-workflow boundary

The consent is a legal and rights-bearing prerequisite. It must not be completed, signed or accepted automatically.

KWDC provides two consent paths:

- download, review, sign and attach the current consent document;
- use the linked digital-signature workflow and mention completion in the email.

The digital-signature workflow was identified but not executed. Its live fields, terms, session behaviour and any human-verification controls remain unknown. A human must stop if CAPTCHA, login, payment, session, identity, terms or other protected controls appear.

No CAPTCHA, account login or mandatory payment was confirmed on the canonical public email route.
