- Destination boundary: the first-party `Email Music Director` action was visible, but its destination was not exposed in passive plaintext. It was not decoded, guessed or stored.

- Cost and access: the main airplay route does not state a submission fee, mandatory payment, login or CAPTCHA requirement.
- Manual boundary: a human must reopen the official page, use only the current Music Director action, confirm international and catalogue fit, prepare the EPK and reliable download link, and send manually.
