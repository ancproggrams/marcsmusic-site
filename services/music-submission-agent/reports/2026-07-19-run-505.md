## 1. SoundChat Radio

Official route: `https://soundchatradio.com/submit-music`.

SoundChat Radio publishes a first-party music-submission form for potential airplay, online features, artist spotlights and possible interviews. It presents itself as a Caribbean station with 24/7 broadcasting and reach in more than 100 countries.

The route requests:

- artist name and email;
- track title and genre;
- a SoundCloud, Spotify, YouTube or direct-download link;
- artist or track context;
- high-quality MP3 at 320kbps or WAV where available;
- a clean radio edit alongside an explicit version when applicable;
- complete artist, title and genre metadata;
- a short biography and social links.

The page states that standard submission is free and normally reviewed within one to two weeks. The submitter must confirm ownership of the music and grant SoundChat Radio permission to broadcast it. That authorization, along with rights, version control, Content ID, privacy and the final form, requires manual review.

A separate Stripe donation page exists, but donating is not a submission condition and was not treated as part of the route.

`info@soundchatradio.com` is published as support for the official SoundChat app. It is syntactically valid and domain-aligned, but is not authorized as a music-submission mailbox; the web form remains canonical.

Activity is current: first-party live and schedule pages display active on-air and upcoming programming, and the official homepage published a dated dancehall discussion on **16 March 2026**.
