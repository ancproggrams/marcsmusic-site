## Verification notes

- KZSU official contact page lists KZSU Music postal delivery, a Music Directors section marked "send us music!" and the public route music@kzsu.stanford.edu. The linked guideline page presented a temporary-cookie/session requirement, so this route is manual.

- WHUS official New Music Committee page says the committee reviews brand-new releases that promoters send to WHUS, discusses them with peers, shapes station sound and lists musicdirector@whus.org.
- WNYU official About page says WNYU is NYU's student-run radio station, invites artists to send CDs, CD-Rs, LPs, 12-inch, 10-inch or 7-inch releases by mail and also accepts digital submissions at music@wnyu.org while physical submissions are prioritized.
- WCBN official contact page lists the station mailing address, Music Directors section and says WCBN accepts music from any and all musicians willing to send a hard copy album, preferably CD or vinyl, and listens to everything sent.
