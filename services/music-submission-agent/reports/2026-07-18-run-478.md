YardHype Radio publishes a first-party song-submission form requesting artist name, song title, email, optional comments or links, an MP3/M4A/WAV upload and optional JPG/PNG artwork. The free-first route is separate from optional paid airplay-promotion packages. Activity was verified through recently played content, current music posts through July 13, 2026 and multiple 2026 artist-spotlight voting rounds.

## UnityXM Radio verification

UnityXM describes a Jamaican 24/7 online station serving listeners worldwide. Its official guidance requires professional-quality music that is already released and searchable on major streaming platforms. It rejects explicit lyrics and listed themes, prefers Reggae, Afro-Caribe, Fusions and Contemporary Dancehall, and requires at least 1000x1000 cover art plus a high-quality MP3 or WAV.

The official page links to an external DISCO upload inbox. Passive retrieval of that provider route returned a cache miss, so its live fields, account requirements, CAPTCHA, privacy terms, upload limits and retention rules were not inspected or bypassed.

Sources:

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because release availability, strict clean-content compliance, genre and production fit, artwork/audio requirements, all rights, AI eligibility, external-provider boundaries and final upload require human review.

## M3 Radio verification

M3 Radio publishes `m3newmusic@yahoo.com` as its official music-director submission mailbox. It prefers a download link rather than attachments, accepts AIFF, WAV or MP3 at 128 kbps or higher, requires music to be no more than one year old and no longer accepts CDs. The station says it broadcasts new independent music 24/7 and reports NACC Top 200 plus Hip Hop, Heavy, Electronic, World and Jazz specialty charts.

The official page links to an active Live365 station profile showing a now-playing area and genres including Electro, Underground Hip-Hop, Classic Jazz, Heavy Metal and Rock & Roll.

Sources:

- https://www.m3radio.com/submission-info/
- https://live365.com/station/M3-Radio-a50800

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because release age, fit, audio specifications, download-link access, metadata, rights, international and AI eligibility, optional photograph rights and final outbound email require human approval.
