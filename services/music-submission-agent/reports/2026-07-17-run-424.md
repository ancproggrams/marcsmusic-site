## KAOS 89.3FM Olympia — Washington, United States

KAOS's official Submit Music page states that the station accepts complete releases but does **not** accept singles. Physical submissions are preferred and may be sent as CD, vinyl or cassette. Digital submissions are accepted when staff time allows through:

`kaosmusicdirector@gmail.com`

Digital messages must use the subject `Music Submission` and contain both streaming and download links. KAOS explicitly says not to send attachments or files directly.

The release should include a one-sheet or press release containing a short biography, album description, focus tracks and every FCC violation or clean radio edit. KAOS states that it has no safe-harbor period and never airs material considered profane or indecent.

## Manual-review boundary

A human must confirm:

- which coherent upcoming MarcsMusic EP or album should be submitted;
- that the release is not a standalone single;
- whether an EP qualifies as a full release;
- that every track complies with KAOS's no-safe-harbor standard or has an appropriate clean edit;
- the one-sheet, focus-track notes and release description;
- streaming and download-link permissions, accessibility and expiry;
- digital format, package-size and lossless-file expectations;
- international artist eligibility;
- composition, master, sample, contributor, artwork and packaging rights;
- release-recency expectations;
- the current AI-assisted and AI-generated music policy;
- whether digital or physical delivery is the better route.

Only one delivery method should be used by default. The same release should not be serviced digitally and physically without a clear operational reason.
