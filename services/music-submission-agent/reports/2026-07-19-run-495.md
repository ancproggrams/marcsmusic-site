## 2. Beat Intelligence Network

Official route: `https://www.dubstepfbi.com/beat-intelligence-network`.

## 3. Us & Machines

Official route: `https://usandmachines.com/demos`.

Us & Machines accepts artists worldwide and focuses on house, tech house, progressive house, deep house, bass house, techno and related electronic dance music.

The first-party form accepts either:

- a direct track upload; or
- a private SoundCloud link.

It also visibly requests artist name, track title, email, city/country and optional Facebook, Instagram and SoundCloud profiles. A notable field requires the submitter to identify vocal origin as **Original**, **Sample Pack** or **Suno/AI**. This is an explicit provenance disclosure, not a published guarantee that every AI-assisted track is eligible.

`info@usandmachines.com` is first-party published, syntactically valid and domain-aligned, but it is general contact and not a replacement for the demo form.

Activity is current: Beatport lists **Wolf Story — AMT High** on **July 3, 2026**, and **Inertia Vol. 1** was scheduled for **July 17, 2026**.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact label and genre fit;
- private-link or upload access;
- track and release status;
- composition, master, sample, vocal and contributor rights;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- AI-assisted or AI-generated eligibility;
- privacy, retention and personal-data handling;
- hidden or submit-time form controls;
- post-selection contract terms;
- final form or upload action.

Rhythm House additionally requires confirmation of international eligibility and link privacy. Beat Intelligence Network requires confirmation that the URL is accessible and suitable for signing review. Us & Machines requires file-format and size verification plus explicit vocal and AI provenance review.
