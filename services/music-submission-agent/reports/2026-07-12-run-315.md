## Verification and enrichment

### WMBR

- Authorized route: WMBR's first-party Your Music page instructs artists and labels to send tangible physical music to the Music Director.
- Physical address: Attn: Music Director, WMBR Radio, 3 Ames Street, Cambridge, MA 02142.
- Eligibility: unsigned artists are treated the same as major-label artists.
- Format boundary: WMBR says it does not have capacity to review MP3s and urges tangible physical delivery.
- Supporting material: a concise one-sheet is recommended; glossy photos and oversized press kits are not needed.
- Conduct boundary: the station discourages request campaigns, unsolicited mailing-list updates and aggressive follow-up.
- Contact boundary: the Music Department email is shown through a non-plaintext image link. It was not decoded or substituted for the official physical route.
- Pricing: no submission fee is stated, but physical media, packaging and international postage are sender-funded.
- Activity: the first-party homepage displayed an active live stream, current schedule and a current special event on 2026-07-12, with a broadcast reference dated 2026-06-09.

### KVRX

- Authorized route: KVRX publishes `music@kvrx.org` for all music submissions considered for airplay and states that both digital and physical submissions are currently accepted.
- Inquiry route: `operations@kvrx.org` is published for other questions about music submissions. Programming explicitly says submissions sent there will not be reviewed.
- AI boundary: AI-assisted or AI-generated music and submissions are explicitly excluded from consideration.
- Physical address: KVRX, 2504 C Whitis Avenue - HSM 3.200, Mail Stop E4100, Austin, TX 78712.
- File boundary: the public page does not state complete attachment, hosted-link or metadata requirements, so final digital packaging remains manual.
- Pricing: no submission fee is stated; physical delivery costs remain sender-funded.
- Activity: the first-party site showed a Summer 2026 schedule, active live signal and blog posts through 2026-06-30.
- Business-email verification: both addresses were confirmed as plaintext and purpose-specific on the official Contact page. No SMTP, MX, catch-all or mailbox-level probe was performed.

### Radio K / KUOM

- Authorized physical route: Radio K - KUOM, University of Minnesota, 610 Rarig Center, 330 21st Ave. S, Minneapolis, MN 55455.
- Authorized digital route: send the official Music Department a link to a ZIP file hosted on Google Drive, Dropbox or a similar service.
- Preferred files: highest-quality digital files, preferably lossless WAV or FLAC. Bandcamp download codes or free-release links are accepted.
- Pitch details: the station asks for a short bio, contact information, similar artists, a list of non-FCC tracks and 2-3 suggested focus tracks.
- Genre fit: Radio K plays eclectic independent music but says it generally does not play classical or Top 40.
- Follow-up boundary: the station does not accept tracking-status requests because of submission volume.
- Protected-contact boundary: the Music Department address is exposed through a protected non-plaintext first-party link and was not decoded, guessed or stored.
- Account/session boundary: any Google Drive, Dropbox, Bandcamp or similar file-hosting session must remain human-controlled.
- Pricing: no submission fee is stated; optional hosting, physical media and postage may carry sender costs.
- Activity: the first-party homepage showed current additions, events dated 2026-07-12 and editorial posts through 2026-07-02.
