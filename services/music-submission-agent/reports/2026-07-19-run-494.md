Official route: `https://www.coldharbourrecordings.com/send-demo/`.

Coldharbour's official **May 1, 2026** submission announcement explicitly opens the route to producers worldwide. The label seeks trance, progressive and techno, including vocal-led electronic material.

The first-party form visibly requests:

- artist or act name;
- email address;
- a private or unlisted demo link;
- optional additional notes.

SoundCloud, Dropbox, WeTransfer and Google Drive are accepted. Email attachments are prohibited, and mastered tracks or well-mixed stems are preferred.

`promos@coldharbourrecordings.com` is first-party published, syntactically valid, domain-aligned and explicitly authorized for demo submissions. `info@coldharbourrecordings.com` and `licensing@coldharbourrecordings.com` were retained only as adjacent business contacts.

## 2. Palmlands Records

Official route: `https://palmlands.de/demo-submit/`.

Palmlands seeks Tech House and Bass House and describes its current catalogue as modern club-focused Tech House. Its demo page authorizes two routes:

- the website submission form;
- the optional Trackstack app.

The label says every submission is reviewed by its A&R team and that feedback follows within the next days by email or in the app. The complete embedded website-form fields, CAPTCHA status, account requirements and consent wording were not passively exposed. The Trackstack alternative may require an account or session and remains entirely manual.

`info@palmlands.de` is first-party published, syntactically valid and domain-aligned. It is general business contact, not a confirmed substitute for the canonical demo form.

Palmlands separately sells mixing and mastering services. Those services are not stated as a condition of demo review and were recorded as an optional commercial boundary only.

Activity is current: the official site promotes 2026 events, and Beatport lists **Vinylsurfer — The Frequency** and its instrumental version on **July 10, 2026**.

## 3. Get The Sound Records

Official route: `https://www.getthesoundrecords.com/demo-drop`.

The first-party form asks for:

- name;
- artist name;
- email address;
- social profile;
- a selection field;
- a private SoundCloud or Dropbox track link.

The route accepts only original content for which the submitter owns the rights. It prohibits duplicate submissions of the same track, WeTransfer links and other download links.

This opportunity has a material legal-review boundary. Get The Sound's site-wide terms characterize submissions as non-confidential, permit use without an attribution obligation, state that no compensation is owed for use or exploitation, and include moral-rights waiver, further-assurances and indemnity language. No terms were accepted.

`contact@getthesound.fr` is first-party published in the official privacy policy and terms and is syntactically valid. It is organizationally aligned but uses a different first-party domain and is general/privacy contact, not a replacement for the form.

Activity is current: the official catalogue remains populated, Beatport exposes July 2026 label releases, and **Chapter 3** is dated **June 19, 2026**.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact label and genre fit;
- private-link access and track selection;
- release status;
- composition, master, sample and contributor rights;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- AI-assisted or AI-generated eligibility;
- privacy, retention and personal-data handling;
- hidden or submit-time form controls;
- post-selection contract terms;
- final form, email or app action.

Coldharbour additionally requires final verification of the chosen link's stream/download permissions. Palmlands requires inspection of the embedded form or an authenticated Trackstack flow and separation from optional production services. Get The Sound requires explicit legal approval of the site-wide submission terms before any final action.
