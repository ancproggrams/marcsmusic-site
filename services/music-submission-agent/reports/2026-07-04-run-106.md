## Verification notes

- EQ Music was verified from its official active site and Contact page. It asks artists to send a streaming link, compelling photo and coverage rationale, and also lists a physical music-promos address. The fetched email was redacted, so it was not decoded or guessed.
- Nialler9 was verified from its official active site and Contact page. The page gives music-specific routing for promos, track streams, premieres, album links, releases, Irish artists and gig listings, with advice on release timing, streaming links, download options, bios, social links, photos and no attachments.
- LOUD WOMEN was verified from its official Contact and Radio pages. It lists public editorial and general email routes, directs radio-play consideration to an external form and separates live-review and festival-application routes.
- Get In Her Ears was verified from its official Contact Info & About Us page. It requires at least one female-identifying, non-binary or queer person in the band for coverage, and asks for a short bio, music link, promo picture and social links.
- musicOMH was verified from its official active site and Contact Us page. It accepts feature/interview ideas and album-review alerts by email, prefers album download URLs, can discuss CDs by postal address request, and states it is not currently covering tracks, EPs, videos or singles.
