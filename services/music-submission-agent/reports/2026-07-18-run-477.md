## Electric Sunshine Cult verification

Electric Sunshine Cult publishes `artistmgmt@electricsunshinecult.com` as its official A&R submission mailbox and expressly accepts independent artists worldwide. Its FAQ requests artist name, track title, genre, a private streaming link or WAV file, artwork, release information, social links, a biography and promotional information. Reviews normally take 7–14 business days and do not guarantee release.

ESC accepts a broad electronic and crossover catalog including Dubstep, Bass Music, Drum & Bass, Jungle, Moombahton, Reggaeton, Dancehall, Afrobeat and World Fusion. It recommends a final 24-bit WAV at 44.1 kHz or higher, original or licensed 3000x3000 artwork and complete contributor, sample and remix clearances. Its published policy says artists retain master ownership unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, and lists a 70% artist / 30% ESC net-royalty split for accepted releases. Significant AI involvement should be disclosed and primarily AI-generated projects receive additional review.

An adjacent official roster form requests legal identity, WAV, artwork, promo images, optional videos, social links, newsletter choice and terms acceptance. It was identified as a boundary, not used automatically.

Activity was verified through official January-June 2026 release reporting, including a May-June cycle of 19 releases from 12 countries.

Sources:

- https://www.electricsunshinecult.com/faq
- https://www.electricsunshinecult.com/label-roster
- https://www.electricsunshinecult.com/blog/categories/label

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because track fit, WAV or private-link delivery, rights and splits, artwork and publicity rights, AI disclosure, the 70/30 model, any later distribution or label agreement, newsletter and terms choices, hidden Wix controls and final outbound email or form action require human and legal approval.
