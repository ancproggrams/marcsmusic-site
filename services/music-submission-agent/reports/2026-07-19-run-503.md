## 2. SubCulture Recordings

Official route: `https://www.subculture050.com/demos`.

SubCulture Recordings is a Groningen bass-music label currently accepting demos for 2027. The official page explicitly permits artists to submit from anywhere and seeks deeper, darker, minimal or experimental music including:

- Drum & Bass;
- Dubstep and 140;
- Halftime;
- Jungle;
- Leftfield bass;
- cross-genre bass music.

The published preparation rules request a downloadable private SoundCloud playlist with downloads enabled, track naming in `Artist – Track Title (Demo/WIP)` format, and preferably **6 to 10 tracks**. Finished tracks and works in progress are accepted. WAV is preferred, while high-quality MP3 is also accepted.

The official page offers an embedded first-party form and the authorized mailbox `demos@subculture050.com`. `info@subculture050.com` is first-party published for privacy and general contact. The embedded form fields were not exposed in passive extraction, so any hidden anti-spam or submit-time control remains manual.

The privacy policy contains uncompleted placeholders for automated systems and exact retention periods. This data-handling defect requires review before sharing artist data or private playlists.

Activity is current. The official site promotes **Physis: Deuterion Stasimon**, released on **26 June 2026**, and documented its same-date release event.

## 3. NexGen Music Group

Official route: `https://nexgen.music/demos/submission`.

NexGen Music accepts unreleased music across Bass Music, House/UK Garage, Downtempo, Future Jazz, Chill-out, Leftfield, Trip-Hop, Pop, R&B, Indie, Rock and other genres. It also considers music for licensing-library use.

The first-party form requests:

- full name;
- email;
- project information;
- a required file upload capped at **300 MB**.

The accompanying guidelines request at least four of the artist's best tracks, minimum 320 kbps, with 16/24-bit or higher WAV/AIFF preferred. The authorized email alternative is `demos@nexgenmusicgroup.com`, using the subject `Demo Submission: (artist name)` and including real name, artist name, links, background and files or FTP-style download links. Streaming-only SoundCloud profiles are rejected.

The terms require the submitter to be at least 18 and to own or control all necessary rights. Submission grants NexGen a non-exclusive, fully paid and royalty-free, sublicensable worldwide licence to use, modify, display, perform, reproduce, communicate and distribute the file on or through the website. Warranties and indemnity provisions also apply. These terms require human and legal review before upload or email delivery.

`info@nexgen.music` is the current general/licensing address. The official submission terms also publish `info@nexgenmusicgroup.com`. No mandatory fee, login or visible CAPTCHA was found, but upload validation, privacy, retention and submit-time controls remain manual.

Activity is current: the official release feed lists **Rob Sparx & Afua — Immunity EP** dated **3 July 2026** and **Kachina — Zurcon's Prayer EP** dated **19 June 2026**.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact label, genre and catalogue fit;
- private-link, playlist or upload access;
- master, composition, sample and contributor rights;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- generative-AI origin and disclosure;
- privacy, retention and post-selection deal terms;
- every final form, email, acknowledgement and terms action.

GoodBrother additionally requires a finished, unreleased track and explicit label-signing acknowledgement. SubCulture additionally prefers a 6-to-10-track downloadable playlist and has incomplete privacy-retention documentation. NexGen additionally requires at least four tracks, a 300MB upload or file-transfer route, an age-18 check and legal review of its broad licence, warranties and indemnity.
