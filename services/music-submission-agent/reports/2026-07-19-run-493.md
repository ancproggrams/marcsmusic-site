## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact platform and genre fit;
- private-link access and download permissions;

- composition, master, sample, artwork, image, video and contributor rights where applicable;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;

- privacy, retention and personal-data handling;

- international eligibility where not explicit;
- final form, account or campaign action.

OVERCLOCKIN additionally requires inspection of hidden form controls. Magnetic requires separation of editorial consideration from any optional paid promotional package. Frequency State requires an authenticated SubmitHub account and a deliberate standard-free-versus-premium-paid credit choice.
