- KUPS: the official contact page explicitly invites artists to submit music through its public form. The current staff page publishes role addresses for Electronic, Specialty, Alternative, Hip-Hop and other Music Directors, and the contact page also publishes a postal route. The station is preparing for its 2026-27 season and is summer streaming.
- WUTK: the official Music Department page accepts MP3 tracks through `wutkmusic@gmail.com` or music by mail. It requires a track list, artist name and band contact information, reviews music on Sundays and Mondays, and does not guarantee airplay.
- KBVR: the official music-request page explicitly directs performers and promoters to the Music Director and publishes a station address. A separate listener-request form on the same page contains a hidden validation field and CAPTCHA and is not an artist-submission route.
- KCPR: the official contact page publishes dedicated Music and Programming and Programming and Music Director contacts plus a mailing address. Because current delivery requirements are not published, this route is limited to a pre-submission inquiry without assets.
- WRFL: the official contact page instructs visitors to contact the appropriate director and provides `contact@wrfl.fm` when the correct recipient is unknown. Because no current music-delivery policy is published, this route is limited to a routing inquiry without assets.

Official activity was verified from current station pages.
