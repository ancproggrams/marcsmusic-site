## Verification and enrichment

### KUTX

- Authorized route: `music@kutx.org`, published for on-air rotation by the KUTX Music Director.
- Subject format: `Music Submission - [Artist Name]`, with `ATX` or `TX` added for relevant local releases.
- Required assets: a streaming link, a downloadable WAV link through Dropbox or Google Drive, a short biography and release context, and a highlighted priority track when several are supplied.
- Format and eligibility: final mastered, new or upcoming, FCC-clean radio edits; rough demos and MP3 attachments are not requested.
- Relevant specialist route: Soundfounder covers electronic and experimental music. The official page also lists hip-hop, R&B, Latin, jazz, indie and general new-music programming.
- Excluded adjacent routes: event-promotion Qualtrics forms, paid underwriting and invitation-only Studio 1A workflows were not classified as general music-submission routes.
- Business-email verification: `music@kutx.org` is plaintext, purpose-specific, first-party and domain-matched. No mailbox-level probe was performed.
- Pricing: no music-submission fee is stated.
- Activity: the official site displayed dated articles and episodes from 2026-07-08 through 2026-07-11, including a Soundfounder episode dated 2026-07-08 featuring electronic and beat music from Austin and around the world.

### Fresh On The Net

- Authorized route: the official `Send Us A Track` first-party submission page.
- Window: opens Monday morning and closes after 200 tracks or by Wednesday evening, whichever comes first.
- Status during verification: the official page displayed the inbox as closed.
- Volume and review: one track per artist per week; the team states that every accepted track is heard.
- Availability: the track must be publicly available in full worldwide. Private tracks, SoundCloud Go-only tracks and country-restricted tracks are excluded.
- Repeat and version rules: tracks submitted in an earlier week and remixes or alternative versions of previously submitted tracks are excluded.
- AI eligibility: AI-generated tracks are explicitly not accepted.
- Integrity boundary: artists selected for the Listening Post must not publicize the selection or solicit votes.
- Conditional adjacent radio route: the official page publishes `del.owusu@gmail.com` for clean-radio-edit consideration on Exile FM, but only after first submitting to Fresh On The Net. It is not treated as a replacement for the primary form.
- Pricing: no submission fee is stated.
- Activity: the official site displayed a Listening Post batch dated 2026-07-10 and other current July 2026 posts.
