## Verification and enrichment

### RTRFM

- Authorized digital route: the official first-party Submit Your Music form, which includes an `Overseas` location option.
- Protected email alternative: the page publishes Music Team and Music Director email links, but passive extraction masked the addresses. They were not decoded, guessed or stored.
- Form fields: first name, surname, email, optional phone, city, state or overseas status, artist name, biography upload, social links, upcoming gigs, music stream and a free music-download link.
- Biography upload: PDF, DOC or DOCX.
- Audio delivery: downloadable WAV or high-quality MP3 through services such as Google Drive, Dropbox, SoundCloud or Bandcamp. Audio should not be attached directly to email.
- Anti-spam boundary: the form includes a `Comments` validation field that must remain unchanged. This is treated as a honeypot and prevents automated form completion.
- Physical option: CDs are accepted, but the station says they must be accompanied by a free digital submission. Physical production and postage remain sender costs.
- Relevant programming: current shows include ambient, electronic, experimental and other independent formats.
- Activity: the official site displayed live programming, weekly featured music, current events and stories dated through 10 July 2026.
- Pricing: no submission fee is stated.

### FBi Radio

- Authorized route: `music@fbiradio.com`, published specifically for music submissions on the official station site.
- Required email content: a clear artist and track or album/EP subject, short biography, press image and/or gig details, a stream link and a free high-quality download link.
- File quality: 320 kbps MP3 or WAV. Dropbox, Google Drive and non-expiring WeTransfer are listed as suitable delivery methods; SoundCloud downloads must be enabled.
- Timing: general music auditioning occurs on Wednesdays. Submissions intended for the following week should arrive before midnight Tuesday.
- Specialist servicing: artists may contact an appropriate specialist program through presenter details on official program pages.
- Physical boundary: FBi no longer accepts CD submissions.
- Geographic priority: FBi champions emerging Sydney music and has a strong Australian and Sydney programming mandate. The submission page does not state an international exclusion, but a human should assess fit before pitching.
- Business-email verification: the address is plaintext and purpose-specific on the official site. The station publishes it on its current `fbi.radio` website while using the organization’s established `fbiradio.com` mail domain. No mailbox-level probe was performed.
- Activity: the site displayed current scheduling, episodes through 12 July 2026 and music coverage through 10 July 2026.
- Commercial boundary: separate optional on-air promotional campaigns are not part of the free editorial submission route.

### PBS 106.7FM

- Authorized digital route: the official first-party Submit Your Music form.
- Geographic scope: PBS explicitly says it welcomes new music from local and international acts.
- Form fields: email, first name, surname, optional phone, location, country, city or suburb, state, artist name, biography, social links, upcoming gigs, stream link and a required high-quality download link.
- Stream restrictions: SoundCloud, YouTube and Bandcamp are accepted examples. Spotify and Deezer links are not accepted.
- Download quality: 320 kbps AAC, MP3, WAV or equivalent.
- Human-verification boundary: the form explicitly contains a CAPTCHA for testing whether the visitor is human.
- Anti-spam boundary: the form also includes a field marked `Leave this field blank`.
- Consent boundary: PBS eNews signup is optional and must remain separate from the music submission decision.
- Physical option: CDs and vinyl are accepted; PBS recommends four to five copies with a one-page press release or biography.
- Business contact: the official contact form contains a Music Department category and its own CAPTCHA. No public plaintext submission email was required or inferred.
- Relevant programming: the active program list contains extensive electronic, house, bass, hip-hop, ambient, experimental, dub, reggae and global shows.
- Activity: the official homepage displayed a feature album for 13-19 July 2026, current events and scheduled interviews.
- Pricing: no submission fee is stated.
