## Verification and enrichment

- RTRFM: the official form accepts overseas artists and requests contact/location details, artist biography, social links, upcoming gigs, a streaming link and a freely downloadable WAV or high-quality MP3 link. The station recommends submitting at least two weeks before release, says not to attach audio to email and permits optional physical copies only alongside a free digital submission. The form contains a hidden validation/honeypot field.
- CFRU: the official Music Department accepts digital EPs or albums with at least three different tracks. Submissions must include artist/album information, track listing, contact information and a downloadable package, with 320 kbps MP3 preferred. The local upload form is restricted to artists within 50 km of Guelph, so MarcsMusic is queued through the general official music-email route.
- CJAM: the official digital-only route requires four or more original tracks, a reliable Dropbox/WeTransfer/Google Drive download link, correctly named and tagged audio, artist/release information and an EPK or equivalent copy. Spotify links and direct MP3 attachments are explicitly rejected. MP3/AAC must be at least 192 kbps; FLAC is accepted.
- CFCR: the official Music Department welcomes properly labelled Album/EP MP3s, preferably 192 kbps, via file-sharing delivery rather than attachments. Offensive language must be disclosed, the department works exclusively by email and the station responds only when a release is added.
- CFRO: the official Music Library form accepts local and non-local artists. It requires a ZIP upload up to 250 MB containing correctly named/tagged 320 kbps MP3s, plus artist/release data, ordered track list, CanCon status, CRTC category, genre and suggested shows. Optional artwork may be JPG or PNG up to 4 MB.

Public business contacts were recorded only where visibly published on official station pages. CFRU, CJAM and CFCR publish direct plaintext submission addresses. RTRFM exposes protected/non-plaintext email links, which were not decoded. CFRO uses its official upload form rather than a public submission mailbox.
