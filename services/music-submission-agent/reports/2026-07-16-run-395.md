## 1. Radio K (KUOM) — Minneapolis, Minnesota, United States

### Public first-party submission route

Reviewed official Radio K surfaces:

- Homepage: `https://radiok.org/`

- About page: `https://radiok.org/about`
- Playlist: `https://radiok.org/playlist`
- Schedule: `https://radiok.org/schedule`

The official submission page asks whether an artist wants to submit a release for airplay consideration and provides separate digital and physical methods. Independent artists are expressly included.

### Authorized digital method

For digital delivery, Radio K instructs artists to email its Music Department and:

- link to a downloadable `.zip` file on Google Drive, Dropbox or a similar service;
- provide the highest-quality files available;
- preferably supply lossless WAV or FLAC files;
- alternatively provide a Bandcamp download code or a link to a release that is free to download.

The digital route is the queued free-first method. No account, platform fee, CAPTCHA or web-form workflow is stated.

### Published metadata and package guidance

Radio K asks independent artists to keep supporting information concise. The submission should include:

- a short biography;
- contact information;
- genuinely similar artists;
- a list of tracks that are not FCC-friendly;
- two or three tracks recommended for radio.

Local artists are asked to mark `LOCAL` in the email subject or on a physical package. MarcsMusic must not use that label.

Radio K says it receives a very high volume of releases and cannot accept tracking-information requests. No tracking or status-chasing message is queued.

### Protected business-email boundary

The official Contact and Submitting Music pages both link the Music Department for digital delivery. The destination is presented through first-party Cloudflare email protection and is not visible in plaintext in the passive representation.

The protected address was not decoded, guessed, inferred from staff naming patterns, copied from third-party directories or stored in the database. No SMTP, MX, catch-all, mailbox-level or deliverability probing was performed.

A human reviewer must open the official submission page in a normal browser and use only the designated Music Department contact. If the live route introduces login, CAPTCHA, payment, permission-gated storage or another protected workflow, the submission must stop and remain `needs_manual_review`.

This physical method is retained as an authorized alternative but is not a separate opportunity and is not queued before the free-first digital route because it involves international postage and package preparation.

### Catalogue fit

Radio K describes its programming as an eclectic variety of independent music, old and new. Its submission guidance says the station plays many genres but generally does not play classical or Top 40. It also maintains specialty shows for records that may not enter general rotation.

Current homepage additions included electronic, sound-collage and rap releases. Selected MarcsMusic electronic, bass, hip-hop-adjacent, reggae-adjacent and world-fusion releases therefore have plausible fit, subject to human track selection and the station's final editorial judgment.

### Manual-review procedure

A human reviewer must:

- select one MarcsMusic release that fits Radio K's independent programming;
- open the protected Music Department route only from the official submission page;
- confirm that international artists are accepted;
- confirm Radio K's current AI-assisted or AI-generated music policy;
- use a downloadable `.zip` link that works without login, permission request or expiry;
- prefer lossless WAV or FLAC files;
- include a concise bio, contact details, similar-artist references, FCC-content notes and two or three recommended tracks;
- confirm that all supplied audio, artwork and metadata are authorized for review and airplay;
- avoid Spotify-only, streaming-only, permission-gated or non-downloadable links;
- avoid general contacts, individual staff, specialty shows and the separate Off The Record performance-booking route;
- not request tracking or follow-up status after submission;
- retain `needs_manual_review` until the final asset and policy checks are complete.
