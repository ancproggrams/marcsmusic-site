## 1. 4ZZZ 102.1FM and Zed Digital — Brisbane, Australia

### Authorized route

4ZZZ’s official Music Submissions page directs artists to the canonical form:

`https://jeff.4zzz.org.au/form/submit-your-music-to-4zzz`

Official evidence:

- Station homepage: `https://4zzz.org.au/`
- Music Submissions page: `https://4zzz.org.au/music-submissions`
- Canonical form: `https://jeff.4zzz.org.au/form/submit-your-music-to-4zzz`
- Contact page: `https://4zzz.org.au/contacts`
- Current schedule: `https://4zzz.org.au/grid`
- Reviews and New Releases: `https://4zzz.org.au/reviews`
- Events: `https://4zzz.org.au/events`

The Music Submissions page says submissions are considered by the Music Department for possible processing and inclusion in 4ZZZ’s digital library, where announcers may access them for programming. It also states that non-downloadable streams are not accepted.

### Published submission requirements

The canonical form publishes the following requirements and fields:

- one artist contact email;
- subscriber-band or solo-artist status and subscriber number when applicable;
- artist name and location;
- release name and release date;
- permission or refusal for pre-release airplay;
- release genre;
- label or distributor;
- member names and pronouns;
- optional Aboriginal, Torres Strait Islander, gender-diverse, transgender, agender, non-binary or gender-nonconforming identification fields;
- artist biography, release information or a press-pack link;
- language or content warnings;
- a downloadable release link;
- a download code when required;
- cover-art link;
- social-media links;
- Brisbane or surrounding-area show information;
- final acknowledgement giving 4ZZZ permission to play the release on 102.1FM and Zed Digital.

Accepted audio and delivery examples:

- WAV;
- MP3 at 320 kbps;
- download-enabled public or private SoundCloud link;
- Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive link;
- Bandcamp or Apple Music with a download code.

Explicitly unsuitable delivery:

- Spotify-only links;
- non-downloadable streams.

The form requires JavaScript. No textual CAPTCHA, account-login or payment requirement was visible in the passive first-party representation, but live validation, anti-spam controls and session state must be rechecked manually when the form is opened.

### Business-email verification and route consolidation

4ZZZ’s official Contact page publishes:

`music-department@4zzz.org.au`

Verification level:

- published in plaintext on 4ZZZ’s official Contact page;
- valid email syntax;
- exact official `4zzz.org.au` domain alignment;
- explicitly associated with the Music Department;
- designated for following up an existing submission;
- no guessed address pattern;
- no SMTP, MX, catch-all, mailbox-level or deliverability probing.

The mailbox is not treated as an alternative initial submission route because the same official page instructs artists to complete the online Music Submission form. General, sponsorship, community, reception, interview, giveaway, news and volunteer mailboxes were excluded.

The official postal address was not interpreted as authorization for physical music delivery.

### Separate Album of the Week form

4ZZZ also links a separate Album of the Week recommendation form. That route is not queued for MarcsMusic because the page limits eligibility to Australian artists and says the release must already have demonstrated support across multiple 4ZZZ programmes, subject to specialist-programme exceptions.

### Missing or unresolved public requirements

The reviewed first-party pages do not clearly publish:

- whether non-Australian artists are eligible for the general Music Department form;
- single, EP and album eligibility boundaries for the general form;
- maximum audio-link or file-package size;
- required metadata embedded in the audio files;
- response times or follow-up timing;
- an AI-assisted or AI-generated music policy;
- a complete privacy explanation for the optional identity fields;
- whether a CAPTCHA or extra consent step appears only after JavaScript executes.

### Manual-review controls

Before any submission, a human must:

- re-open the official Music Submissions page and canonical form;
- confirm the form remains authorized and free;
- verify that no CAPTCHA, login, payment, session or additional consent boundary has appeared;
- confirm that international artists are eligible;
- choose a suitable MarcsMusic release and confirm that it has not already been submitted;
- verify ownership, distribution rights and airplay permission;
- provide a functioning downloadable WAV or 320 kbps MP3 link rather than Spotify;
- confirm that private-link and download permissions work without account access;
- review artist, member, pronoun and optional identity fields and disclose only approved information;
- provide accurate release metadata, biography or press-pack information, cover art and social links;
- disclose any required language or content warning;
- review the pre-release-airplay and final airplay-permission choices;
- review AI-origin eligibility;
- submit manually and retain the confirmation before using the follow-up mailbox.
