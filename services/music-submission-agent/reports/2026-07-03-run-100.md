## Verification and enrichment notes

- WUOG was verified through its official homepage and contact page. The station describes itself as UGA's student-run alternative media outlet broadcasting 24 hours a day, committed to uplifting smaller artists, and its contact page gives an official album-mailing process plus Music Director and Local Music Director email routes.
- WRAS/Album 88 was verified through its official site, contact page and current-rotation page. The site says WRAS is student-operated and Atlanta-focused, its contact page lists Regular Rotation music-department routes for Atlanta or underground music released in the past four months, and its Spinitron current-rotation page shows March 2026 rotation activity.
- Amazing Radio was verified through its official JavaScript application shell and public reference material describing the platform as an international radio station for new and emerging artists where artists can submit music via the Amazing Radio website.
- TIDAL was verified through the official TIDAL Artist Home login/sign-up page and recent public reporting about TIDAL Upload as a free direct-upload feature for independent artists. The route remains manual because account, rights, AI/copyright, territory/eligibility and no-royalty caveats need review.
- SoundOn was verified through its official homepage and public reference material describing it as a TikTok-owned music marketing/distribution platform where artists upload music, retain copyright and receive royalty-share mechanics. The route remains manual because account, distribution, rights, payout and platform choices need review.
