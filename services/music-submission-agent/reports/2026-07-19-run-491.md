## 2. Bass Darkness

Official route: `https://www.bassdarkness.com/submissions`.

Bass Darkness accepts finished tracks only and prefers private SoundCloud or Dropbox links. Its focus is Phonk, Bass and dark, heavy-driven electronic music. The visible form asks for artist name, email, track title, demo link, notes, Discord and Instagram.

If the form confirmation does not arrive, the page authorizes `demo@bassdarkness.com` as a fallback. The mailbox is first-party published, syntactically valid, domain-aligned and explicitly demo-related. It was not used.

The site says artists retain ownership and that agreements are intended to be transparent. Exact royalty split, licence, term, termination and release-contract wording is not published on the submission page and remains a manual legal boundary.

Activity is current: the official site lists releases through **July 3, 2026** and an upcoming release dated **July 24, 2026**.

## 3. BASSWAV

Official route: `https://www.basswav.com/submit`.

BASSWAV is a Dutch independent label for drum and bass, hard techno, hard bass, dubstep and UK bass. Its two-step form exposes email and one private track-link field on step one; the FAQ also requests a short fit note. Accepted link methods include private SoundCloud, Drive, Dropbox and WeTransfer.

The label states that artists keep masters and options, no exclusivity is required and there is no upfront artist cost. It allows unfinished tracks when the direction is clear, while discouraging tracks already released publicly everywhere.

The page contains a visible `Leave this field empty` honeypot. It was not touched. Step two and the full privacy and terms text were not passively inspected, so final fields, consent and data handling require human review.

Activity is confirmed by form metrics verified **May 23, 2026** and the BW002 release dated **June 5, 2026**.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact label and subgenre fit;
- track selection, release status and private-link permissions;
- composition, master, sample, feature and contributor rights;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- AI-assisted or AI-generated eligibility;
- privacy, retention, form storage and later-step controls;
- post-selection contract, licence, royalty and termination terms;
- international eligibility where not explicit;
- the final form or authorized fallback-email action.

Boomslang additionally requires complete external-Google-Form inspection. Bass Darkness requires finished-track validation, social-contact-field review and strict fallback-email use only when confirmation fails. BASSWAV requires the honeypot to remain untouched and human inspection of step two.
