## RekHeard Breakerz verification

RekHeard Breakerz publishes a first-party artist form for one independent Soca, Reggae or Dancehall single. The track must be clean, radio-friendly, club-ready, in English, not a riddim track and already available on Spotify.

The form requests artist and contact data, city, country, Instagram, optional TikTok, genre, a clean Spotify link, a non-expiring tagged-MP3 download link and a non-expiring high-resolution solo artist-photo link. YouTube and streaming links are not accepted for the MP3 field. When Google Drive is used, the page instructs submitters to grant access to `info@rekheardbreakerz.com`.

The required confirmation grants permission to review, discuss, play excerpts, feature, publish, share and promote the track, artist name, image, likeness and submitted materials across podcast, video, website, playlists, social media, email and promotional platforms. The same form also presents consent to receive submission, episode, playlist, opportunity and platform-update emails, with an unsubscribe statement.

Activity was verified through the official site, which reports more than 440 submissions from more than 25 countries and lists four Season 1 episodes. Apple Podcasts identifies the show as active in 2026 and dates Episodes 1 and 2 to June 1 and June 15, 2026.
