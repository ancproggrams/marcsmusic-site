The label is open to new talent in trance, progressive house, melodic techno and related styles, and lists active events for August 29 and October 21, 2026. - KEXP: the official submission policy publishes `md@kexp.org`, requires streaming and WAV download links with no attachments, and asks for artist details, release metadata, focus tracks, clean edits, lyrics, credits and a bio or one-sheet. KEXP states that airplay cannot be purchased. Package preparation and editorial-fit review remain manual.
- Soundplate: the official platform provides free submissions to independent Spotify and Deezer playlists. The inspected Garage & Bass route requires Spotify or Deezer authorization and automatically follows specified Soundplate accounts and playlists. OAuth scopes, automatic-follow consent, genre fit and track selection remain manual. A separate request-verification interstitial was observed and not bypassed.
- CKCU: the official contact page publishes `programs@ckcufm.com` for the Program Director, while the active station homepage shows current July 2026 programming across indie, electronic, reggae and global music. Because no complete unsolicited-music policy is published, the queue item is restricted to a short inquiry without audio, attachments or links.
