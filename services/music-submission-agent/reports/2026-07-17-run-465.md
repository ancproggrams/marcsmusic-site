## Verification

KDBR Dain Bramage Radio publishes a dedicated free submission form for independent artists worldwide. The first-party page accepts clean, broadcast-ready MP3/WAV files and artwork uploads, or private SoundCloud/YouTube links. Published fields include Band/Artist Name, Email, Genres, Desired Link and track/artwork attachments.

The page states that submissions are normally reviewed within 48 hours and that selected artists receive a separate permission form before rotation and a featured-artist profile. It also states that artists retain ownership. Standard submission is described as completely free, with no payola or label fee.

Current activity was verified from the official KDBR page, its current featured-artists page and the linked Caster.fm station page. The featured-artists page identifies artists currently in heavy rotation. The Caster page publishes daily broadcasts from 7 AM to at least 5 PM AKST. This differs from the main page's 24/7 language and is retained as a manual-review note rather than silently reconciled.

The general mailbox `contact@dainbramageentertainment.com` is first-party published, syntactically valid, domain aligned and context aligned. It is not designated as the music-submission route and was therefore excluded from the submission queue. No SMTP, MX, catch-all, mailbox-level or deliverability probing was performed.

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because:

- the embedded form is explicitly protected by Google reCAPTCHA;
- the route permits direct audio and artwork uploads;
- selected artists receive a later permission form that was not available for passive review;
- clean-audio, ownership, sample, contributor and artwork rights must be confirmed;
- AI-assisted and fully generated music eligibility is not published;
- upload limits, MIME restrictions and the permission grant's territory, duration and revocation mechanism are not published;
- the final upload and submit action requires explicit owner approval.

No login requirement was observed for the music form. Optional account links elsewhere in the site and the optional support/donation route were excluded from the submission workflow. No payment requirement is published for the standard music-submission route.
