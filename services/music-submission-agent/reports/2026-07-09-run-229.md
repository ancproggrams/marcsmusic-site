## Verification notes

- KVRX official Contact page lists music@kvrx.org for all music submissions for airplay consideration, says both physical and digital submissions are currently accepted, and excludes AI-assisted/generated submissions from consideration. The official homepage shows current student-run underground/local-discovery programming.
- KCSB official Contact page identifies the music director, says the music director reviews music sent to the station, corresponds with music industry representatives, labels and artists, and asks submitters to send new releases and demos in all formats to the attention of the music director. The official site shows current 2026 programming and blog/review activity.
- WKDU official Contact page exposes a public contact form with Music Submission as a topic option and required name, email, subject and body fields. The official homepage shows active July 2026 playlists, events and on-air schedule data.
- KSPC official Music Submissions page welcomes submissions from artists in all genres, accepts mailed CDs/records and digital submissions by email attachment/cloud-download link, rejects singles, demos, cassettes, Spotify/iTunes-only links and substantially AI-generated work, states no pay-for-play, and notes a review process before airplay.
- KXLU official Contact page lists FCC-clean submissions only and physical attention routes for General Submissions, Demolisten and A Fistful of Vinyl. The official homepage shows current playlist, DJ, blog and 2026 station activity.
