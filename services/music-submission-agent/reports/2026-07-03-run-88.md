## Manual-review reasons

All five routes remain manual-review only. The batch includes external SubmitHub/Groover curator-platform workflows, a public multi-step editorial submission form with free and paid package choices, a JavaScript-enabled free playlist track submission form, and public magazine news/review-material email routes. Manual review is required for account/session handling, possible payment/credit choices, pitch copy, curator/editorial targeting, track-link and metadata selection, release-status and EPK/artwork context, mood/emotion tagging, rights/originality assertions, marketing consent, email copy and human platform fit review. No route in this batch exposed a safe complete public auto-submit flow.
