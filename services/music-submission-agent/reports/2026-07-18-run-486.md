## 1. GoodBrother

Official route: the first-party demo form at `https://www.goodbrother.io/demos`, with `demo@goodbrother.io` published as the label's demo mailbox.

The Dubai independent electronic label accepts:

- Techno;
- House;
- Garage;
- Drum & Bass;
- Dubstep;
- finished, unreleased tracks only;
- a private SoundCloud link with downloads enabled.

Visible fields are Artist Name, Email, Track Title, Genre, Private Link and Additional Notes / Social Media Links. The form also requires acknowledgement that the submission is for possible label release signing, not playlist placement.

`demo@goodbrother.io` and `info@goodbrother.io` are first-party published, syntactically valid and domain-aligned. The first is explicitly for demos; the second is for other inquiries.

Current activity is confirmed by the official 2026 site, current genre playlists and releases dated through 3 July 2026.

## 2. NexGen Music Group

Official routes: the first-party demo page and uploader at `https://nexgen.music/demos`, plus the designated mailbox `demos@nexgenmusicgroup.com`.

NexGen seeks unreleased music in styles including:

- Bass Music;
- Drum & Bass;
- Dubstep;
- EDM;
- House and UK Garage;
- Downtempo;
- Chill-out;
- Trip-Hop.

Published requirements include:

- at least four of the artist's best tracks;
- minimum 320 kbps audio;
- preferably 16/24-bit or better WAV or AIFF;
- real name, artist name, profile and production/release background;
- attachments or Dropbox/WeTransfer-style download links for email;
- no streaming-only links;
- no material previously offered as a free download;
- no remixes or mashups of commercial artists;
- an uploader limit of 300 MB;
- a minimum age of 18.

The demo terms require the submitter to own or license all rights and prohibit uncleared third-party samples. They also grant NexGen a non-exclusive, royalty-free, sublicensable worldwide licence to use, modify, display, perform, reproduce, communicate and distribute the submitted file on or through the website. Warranties, indemnity, privacy and jurisdiction provisions require legal review.

Current activity is confirmed by official releases dated 19 June and 3 July 2026.

## 3. KPEO World Community Radio

Official route: the first-party form at `https://kpeo.org/contact-us/music-submissions`.

KPEO states that it accepts all genres but no explicit lyrics. The form requires:

- name;
- email;
- phone;
- an embedded MP3;
- cover art;
- audio no larger than 50 MB;
- artwork no larger than 20 MB;
- completion of a visible human `I'm not a robot` verification.

The page asks artists to like KPEO's Facebook and Instagram pages so the station may tag them, but it is unclear whether that is a strict eligibility condition.

Current programming includes independent reggae covering roots, dub and dancehall, world music, Afrobeat, electronic dance and broader independent programming. KPEO lists worldwide coverage and current on-air and recently-played surfaces.

`info@kpeo.org` is first-party published, syntactically valid and domain-aligned as a general station contact. It was not substituted for the official music-submission form.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact genre, programme or label fit;
- release status, clean-content or multi-track requirements as applicable;
- private-link, attachment, upload and file-size requirements;
- composition, master, sample, collaborator, artwork and contributor rights;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- AI-assisted or AI-generated music eligibility;
- privacy, retention and hidden form controls;
- possible recording, licensing or broadcast terms;
- the final submission action.

GoodBrother additionally requires review of the release-signing acknowledgement and finished-unreleased status. NexGen requires legal approval of its broad website licence, warranties and indemnity and confirmation of at least four eligible tracks. KPEO requires clean-content review, direct audio/artwork uploads, contact-data handling, clarification of the social-follow request and manual CAPTCHA completion.
