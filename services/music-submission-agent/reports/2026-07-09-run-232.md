# Music Submission Agent — Run 232

Date: 2026-07-09

## Summary

Run 232 adds five public-authorized global music distribution, artist-upload, royalty-split, playlist/radio-support and royalty-free discovery routes for MarcsMusic.

## New opportunities

1. Amuse Self Service Distribution Artist Upload Licensing Route
   - Route type: global digital distribution / self-service artist upload / licensing support.
   - Manual boundaries: account/login, subscription tier, master rights, audio/artwork upload, DSP delivery metadata, licensing and royalty-advance/AI-mastering options.

2. Labelcaster Independent Label Artist Royalty Split Distribution Route
   - Route type: worldwide independent label/artist music distribution with royalty-split tooling.
   - Manual boundaries: label/artist account, roster ownership, contract/pricing terms, splits, metadata and DSP delivery.

3. GYROstream Independent Artist Distribution Radio Playlisting Services Route
   - Route type: Australia-origin global independent music distribution plus publicity, radio playlisting, marketing and royalty collection.
   - Manual boundaries: account/session, release ownership, service pricing, artist fit, radio/playlisting scope and upload workflow.

4. ONErpm OFFstep Emerging Artist Fixed Fee Distribution Upload Route
   - Route type: emerging-artist fixed-fee distribution and video-upload route through ONErpm/OFFstep.
   - Manual boundaries: OFFstep account, fixed-fee payment, artist/release eligibility, distribution/video delivery and publishing/label-service boundaries.

5. Chartwin Royalty Free Artist Upload Contest Discovery Route
   - Route type: worldwide royalty-free music upload/discovery/contest platform with free and paid tiers.
   - Manual boundaries: account/login, royalty-free licensing, PRO/collecting-society restrictions, AI/traditional-work disclosure, contest terms and catalogue fit.
