## Verification and enrichment

- KXCI: the official Music Department guide states that it accepts music from around the world as CDs, vinyl with a digital download, and MP3/WAV download links. It requires artist, release, label, date, genre, comparable-artist, explicit-content, biography, social, performance and contact metadata. The dedicated mailbox is presented through a protected/non-plaintext link and was not decoded.
- KZSC: the official 2026 staff page lists dedicated RPM & Electronic and International Music Directors. Their email links are protected/non-plaintext, and the page does not publish package or file requirements, so this route is restricted to a manual pre-submission inquiry.
- KALX: the official contact page accepts only physical professionally pressed CDs or LPs and explicitly rejects downloads, file transfers, streaming links, YouTube, CD-Rs and other digital forms. A March 13, 2026 chart confirms recent music-library activity.
- KDVS: the official staff page states that all new music must be sent physically. The FAQ accepts CDs, singles, records and tapes, publishes separate USPS and courier addresses, and provides department attention lines including Electronic and Experimental. A March 10, 2026 chart confirms recent activity.
- KCSB: the official contact page invites new releases and demos, says all formats are welcome, and publishes separate USPS and courier addresses addressed to the Music Director. The site shows live now-playing data and Music Department content from May 2026.

Public business contacts were recorded only where visibly published on official station pages. KALX, KDVS and KCSB publish plaintext music-department business addresses, but their authorized submission instructions direct music through physical delivery rather than unsolicited digital attachments. KXCI and KZSC contact links were not decoded, guessed or technically probed.
