## 1. Stonx Music

Official route: `https://stonxmusic.co.uk/about/`.

Stonx Music publishes `Demo@StonxMusic.co.uk` as its authorized demo mailbox. Its current process asks for:

- a private SoundCloud link;
- downloads enabled;
- MP3 at 320 kbps preferred;
- information about the artist and notable achievements;
- track and style context;
- an explanation of why the track suits the label.

The label describes itself as a global neurofunk and heavy drum-and-bass community. `Hello@StonxMusic.co.uk` and `Mgmt@StonxMusic.co.uk` are first-party, syntactically valid and domain-aligned, but are not substitutes for the demo mailbox.

Activity is current: the official site published **STX070 — Croo, Toy Soldiers / Plastic Man** on **16 July 2026** and Stonxcast episode 196 on **4 July 2026**.

This route remains manual because exact neurofunk fit, private-link permissions, audio quality, artist context, rights, Content ID, AI provenance, privacy/retention, future contract terms and the final email must be reviewed by a human.

## 2. Red Devil Radio

Official route: `https://reddevilradio.com/submit-music.html`.

The current artist workflow requires:

- an artist account and email verification;
- one submission credit;
- one original, rights-cleared MP3;
- cover art, metadata and track context;
- acceptance of the current submission agreement.

The active submission cycle focuses on rock, country, hip-hop, indie rock, folk and alternative. MarcsMusic should only consider a clearly matching hip-hop or crossover release.

Each submission costs one credit. New accounts receive free starter credits; extra credit packs and monthly/yearly Unlimited plans are optional paid boundaries.

The submission agreement is versioned **11 June 2026**. It preserves artist ownership but grants a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free and removal-request-based licence for technical processing, review, station streaming, public performance, metadata display and related promotion. It does not promise direct payment and records agreement/audit data including legal-account details, a hashed IP value and user agent.

The official contact page publishes protected exact-domain mailboxes for general support, artist-review follow-up, press/partnerships and DMCA. Their local parts were not decoded or inferred. New artists are explicitly directed to the account queue.

Activity is current through 24/7 streaming and public artist airplay records through **29 June 2026**.

## 3. WCOM 103.5 FM

Official route: `https://wcomfm.org/submit-music/`.

WCOM is an all-volunteer community station without a music director or centralized library. Hosts select their own music. The station therefore advises artists to:

- choose a relevant host or programme;
- address the electronic form to that host;
- use a named DJ when sending physical music;
- avoid relying on the general new-release box, which receives only sporadic airplay.

The current programme schedule includes electronic, world, hip-hop, indie, jazz, Latin, R&B, reggae, rock and other potentially relevant formats. The station broadcasts locally in Carrboro/Chapel Hill and streams worldwide, but its submission language emphasizes independent local music and regional performances. Non-local eligibility is not guaranteed.

During passive inspection, the form area rendered `sorry, we can only accept one submission`. The form fields, file mechanism, CAPTCHA and session controls were therefore not exercised or bypassed.

`stationmanager@wcomfm.org` is first-party, syntactically valid and domain-aligned, but is a general station contact rather than a substitute for the host-specific submission route.

Activity is current: WCOM posted its 2026 listener survey on **9 July 2026**, showed current June music/news posts and published a programme schedule for **12–18 July 2026**.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact label, genre, station and programme fit;
- private-link, MP3, artwork, metadata or physical-delivery preparation;
- master, composition, sample and contributor rights;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- generative-AI origin and disclosure;
- privacy, retention and post-selection terms;
- every final email, account, form or delivery action.

Red Devil Radio additionally requires account creation, email verification, a credit and agreement acceptance, with optional paid products. WCOM additionally requires a specific host and has a current form/session gate. Stonx Music requires a manually prepared demo email and private downloadable SoundCloud link.
