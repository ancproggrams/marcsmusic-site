## Verification and enrichment

### Radio One 91FM

- Authorized route: the official contact page publishes `music@r1.co.nz` specifically under MUSIC SUBMISSIONS and provides a separate physical-submission address at PO Box 1436, Dunedin 9016, New Zealand.
- Named role: the contact directory identifies Maddy Barnes as Music Director and also publishes a direct staff address; the role address remains the queued route because it is explicitly designated for submissions.
- Current activity: the official 2026 website shows a live stream, weekly programme, podcasts, a current Top 11 countdown and active station contacts.
- Format guidance: Radio One links to the official one-page Getting Airplay on Alt Radio guide. It recommends a personal introduction, contact details including a phone number, a correctly named and tagged download at minimum 320 kbps MP3 quality, focus songs and a one-page information sheet with website and social links.
- Locality boundary: the guide's suggested subject line begins with `NZ Artist`, and the document is oriented toward servicing New Zealand alternative radio. International eligibility for MarcsMusic is therefore not assumed and must be confirmed manually.
- Cost: no submission fee is stated. Physical postage is optional and sender-funded.
- Email verification: `music@r1.co.nz` was validated through first-party publication, syntax, domain alignment and explicit purpose. No SMTP, MX, catch-all or mailbox probe was performed.
- MarcsMusic fit: potentially suitable for alternative, electronic, experimental, hip-hop, reggae or world-fusion releases after a human confirms international eligibility and identifies the most relevant programme or playlist context.

### Radio Control 99.4FM

- Authorized route: the official contact page publishes `music@radiocontrol.org.nz` specifically for Music Submissions and a snail-mail address at Massey University.
- Current activity: the official homepage states that Radio Control broadcasts 24/7 from Palmerston North and streams worldwide. It promotes live sessions, a weekly NZ Top Ten and 100% NZ Music Week dated 25-31 May 2026.
- Geographic scope: the station describes its programming as alternative music from Aotearoa and the rest of the world, so international music is not excluded on the public page.
- Format boundary: the station does not publish definitive audio-format, attachment, download-link, release-age, explicit-content, rights, AI-origin or response-time rules on the reviewed contact page. These must be confirmed before sending.
- Cost: no submission fee is stated. Physical postage is optional and sender-funded.
- Email verification: `music@radiocontrol.org.nz` was validated through first-party publication, syntax, domain alignment and explicit purpose. Manager, programme, production, breakfast and volunteer addresses were not classified as alternate submission routes.
- MarcsMusic fit: potentially suitable for alternative, independent and electronic releases, but a human should avoid large unsolicited attachments and verify the station's current preferred delivery method.

- MarcsMusic fit: potentially relevant for alternative and electronic programming, subject to the live submission page's eligibility and delivery rules.
