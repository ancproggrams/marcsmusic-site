## Verification notes

- KUTX official submission guidance asks artists to email the music director at music@kutx.org with a streaming link, WAV download link, bio/location/release notes and a highlighted top track; it asks for final mastered, new/upcoming and FCC-clean radio edits, and exposes specialty-show/event-form boundaries.
- WXPN official contact guidance says music submissions for airplay should send an EPK including a digital download link by email, cannot guarantee receipt/airplay confirmation, and separately routes Local Show submissions to a WAV or high-quality MP3 download link plus short bio/show info.
- WFMU official Send Music page welcomes review materials by physical delivery, including vinyl, CD, CD-R and cassette, lists postal and UPS/FedEx addresses for the music director, and points contact to a Music Department Mail Page. The active WFMU homepage shows current 2026 archives/playlists and a Send Us Music navigation link.
- WNXP official Music Submissions page asks for email to submissions@wnxp.org with a download link to a clean/no-cursing, mastered, 44.1k/16-bit stereo WAV file and basic artist info, says no MP3s, asks submitters to check fit, and notes Nashville emphasis with limited reply capacity.
- WREK official submission guidance says music can be submitted by snail mail or emailed to music.director@wrek.org, says WREK can handle common music media formats while CDs and LPs have a better chance of airplay, and explains music directors randomly pull submissions for review. The active WREK site shows current 2026 programming and playlists.
