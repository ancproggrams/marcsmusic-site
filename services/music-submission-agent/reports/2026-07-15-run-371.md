## 1. CFRU 93.3 FM — Guelph, Canada

### Authorized route

CFRU's official Music page directs artists to send download links to `music@cfru.ca`. A valid submission must:

- be an EP or album containing at least three different songs;
- include artist and album information;
- include a track listing;
- include contact information;
- provide a download link;
- preferably provide high-quality MP3 files at 320 kbps.

CFRU states that it is accepting digital submissions except in rare circumstances.

Official evidence:

- Current station site: `https://www.cfru.ca/`
- Music Department and submission guidance: `https://www.cfru.ca/music/`

- `music@cfru.ca`

Verification covered:

### Local-form boundary

CFRU also links a Local Music Submissions Form that permits file upload or a download link. The official page expressly limits that form to musicians in Guelph or the surrounding area within 50 km. MarcsMusic is not eligible for that local route, so it was classified as an adjacent form and excluded rather than used as a workaround.

The general Music Director email route remains the only queued route.

CFRU's official site remains active with live programming, 2026 broadcasts and performance archives, and charts dated July 7, 2026. Its published programming and archive catalogue include electronic, experimental, drum-and-bass, reggae, hip-hop, international and independent music.

## 2. CJAM 99.1 FM — Windsor, Canada

### Authorized route

CJAM's official Submit page states that the station has moved to digital-only submissions and directs artists to `cjammd@gmail.com`.

The published requirements are:

- do not send Spotify links as the submission route;
- do not attach MP3 files directly;
- provide a reliable download link, with Dropbox, WeTransfer or Google Drive preferred;
- submit at least four original tracks;
- encode the music at a minimum of 192 kbps in MP3 or AAC, with FLAC also accepted;
- embed ID tags containing artist name, song title, album, track number, composer name and genre;
- include information about the artist and release, either in the email body or as an EPK PDF.

Official evidence:

- Current station site and activity: `https://www.cjam.ca/`
- Digital submission guidance: `https://www.cjam.ca/submit/`

### Business-email verification

The official Submit page directly and repeatedly publishes:

- `cjammd@gmail.com`

Verification covered:

- direct first-party publication;
- valid email syntax;
- explicit Music Director submission purpose;
- detailed release, download-link, quality, metadata and artist-information requirements.

The mailbox uses Gmail, so official-domain alignment is absent. No SMTP probe, MX probe, catch-all test, mailbox-level check or deliverability test was performed.

### Activity and catalogue fit

CJAM's official homepage published Singles Club material for July 2026 and charts covering June 29 through July 5, 2026. Recent specialty reporting includes an electronic chart, and the station's Music navigation continues to link the submission page.

Selected MarcsMusic electronic, experimental, ambient, dub, reggae, hip-hop and global-fusion multi-track releases therefore have a plausible catalogue fit without implying review or airplay.

### Manual-review controls

- Select a release containing at least four original tracks; do not submit a standalone single.

- Send manually only to `cjammd@gmail.com`.
