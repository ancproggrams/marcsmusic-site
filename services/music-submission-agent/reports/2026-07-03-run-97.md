## Verification and enrichment notes

- Soundplate Play was verified as an official free service for submitting music to Spotify and Deezer playlists. It exposes a playlist grid/selector workflow and explicitly asks artists to submit only to relevant playlists and avoid spam.
- KXCI was verified through its official Music Department page. It accepts music from all over the world, supports digital MP3/WAV download links plus CDs/vinyl, lists metadata requirements and requires volunteer/staff review for genre, quality and explicit-language/content checks.
- KUTX was verified through its official Submit Your Music page. It lists an authorized Music Director email route, asks for a streaming link, WAV download link, bio/release notes, final mastered radio edits and separates specialty-show, event and paid sponsorship routes.
- Audiomack was verified through its official About/creator page as an artist-first platform that allows free music upload, free creator dashboard/stats and trending consideration, but the route requires sign-up/sign-in and rights/metadata review before any upload.
- Groover was verified through its official site as an account-based paid-credit platform for reaching curators, playlists and music professionals. It requires picking pros, uploading a song/demo, writing a pitch and purchasing Grooviz credits before sending.
