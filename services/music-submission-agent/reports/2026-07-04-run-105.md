## Verification notes

- Radio Milwaukee was verified from its official 88Nine Music Submission page. It asks for uncompressed audio, WAV preferred, mixed/mastered radio edits, no physical media, lyrics, bio, song notes, album/live-show information and artist links.
- WFUV was verified from its official Contact & Staff page. It routes music to the Music Department by snail mail at the Fordham University address and limits follow-up calls to a stated Wednesday window.
- WNRN was verified from its official Contact page. It identifies itself as listener-supported independent music radio and tells artists to email an album for airplay consideration, with a separate Commonwealth artist route.
- WERS was verified from its official site and Wicked Local Wednesday page. It exposes a local-artist submission form with artist/contact/location fields, MP3 upload and site-level reCAPTCHA protection.
- WYEP was verified from its official Pittsburgh Area Music Submissions page. It asks Pittsburgh-area artists to email links or mail hard copies, include contact and origin details, suggest 2 to 3 album tracks, check specialty-show fit and provide FCC-clean material.
