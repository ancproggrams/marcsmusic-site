## 1. PBS 106.7FM — Melbourne, Australia

### Authorized routes

PBS's official Submit Your Music page says it welcomes new music from local and international acts and prefers digital submission through its first-party form. The country selector includes the Netherlands and a broad international country list.

The digital form requests:

- email, first name, surname, phone number and location details;
- artist or band name and a short biography;
- social-media links and optional upcoming-gig information;
- a music-stream link from services such as SoundCloud, YouTube or Bandcamp;
- a free high-quality download link for 320-kbps AAC, MP3 or WAV-equivalent audio.

Spotify- and Deezer-only stream links are explicitly rejected.

PBS also authorizes CDs or vinyl by post or in person. It recommends four to five copies of each release and asks for a one-page press release or biography with each CD. Presenters retain editorial control over airplay.

Official evidence:

- Website: `https://www.pbsfm.org.au/`
- Submission form and physical-delivery instructions: `https://www.pbsfm.org.au/submitmusic`
- Program list: `https://www.pbsfm.org.au/programs/list`

Current programs include electronic, bass, beats, house, dub, reggae, dancehall, experimental ambience, global music, world music and hip-hop. Relevant examples include Android Discotheque, Audio Vitamins, Babylon Burning, Dry Run, Electric Sunset, Fresh Produce, Global Village, Riddim Yard, The Blend and The Sound Barrier. ### Form verification

The first-party form explicitly shows:

No login or mandatory payment requirement was observed. The CAPTCHA and anti-spam field make automated submission prohibited.

## 2. fbi.radio — Sydney, Australia

### Authorized route

fbi.radio's official Music Submissions page directs digital submissions to `music@fbiradio.com`. The same purpose-bound address appears on the official Contact page under Music Submissions.

A submission email should include:

- a clear subject in the form `Artist name – track or album/EP name`;
- a short biography, press image and/or upcoming-gig details;
- a stream link, public or private;
- a free high-quality 320-kbps MP3 or WAV download link.

Dropbox, Google Drive and non-expiring WeTransfer links are recommended. SoundCloud downloads must be enabled. All general servicing is digital, and fbi.radio no longer accepts CDs. Music auditioning occurs on Wednesdays; submissions should arrive before midnight Tuesday for consideration in the following week's playlisting.

Official evidence:

- Website: `https://www.fbi.radio/`
- Music-submission instructions: `https://www.fbi.radio/pages/music-submissions`
- Contact page: `https://www.fbi.radio/contact`
- Genre index: `https://www.fbi.radio/genres`

### Activity and catalogue fit

The official homepage displayed featured episodes and articles dated July 2026, including material dated July 14, 2026. Its genre index includes electronic, ambient, experimental, dub, reggae, dubstep, drum and bass, grime, house, techno, EDM, global styles and hip-hop-adjacent genres.

The station describes itself as championing emerging Sydney music. The reviewed submission page does not explicitly limit submissions to Sydney or Australia, but it also does not explicitly confirm international eligibility. This ambiguity requires human review before MarcsMusic outreach.

### Business-email verification

`music@fbiradio.com` was verified through:

- repeated first-party publication on the official Contact and Music Submissions pages;
- valid email syntax;
- explicit Music Submissions purpose;
- use of the `fbiradio.com` domain across the station's official role addresses.

No SMTP, MX, catch-all, mailbox-level or deliverability probing was performed.

### Route separation

fbi.radio also publishes routes for specialist presenters, interviews, gig listings and paid promotional campaigns. Those routes were not queued as duplicate airplay opportunities. The queued route is the general editorial Music Submissions mailbox only.

### Manual-review controls

- Confirm that international artists are currently eligible before sending.
- Choose a suitable MarcsMusic release and prepare a release-specific subject and concise pitch.
- Verify rights, metadata, clean-content requirements where relevant and free download access.
- Use a non-expiring download link and submit before the stated Tuesday-night cut-off where practical.
- Keep specialist-presenter, interview, gig-guide and paid-promotion contacts separate.
- Send manually; no email was dispatched during research.
