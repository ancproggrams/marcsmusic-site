## Added opportunities

1. WPRK 91.5FM Music Submissions Email Route
   - Official public route: https://wprk.org/music-submissions/
   - Status: needs_manual_review
   - Pricing model: free
   - Verification notes: official WPRK site is active with current schedule/show listings; the Music Submissions page asks artists to send submissions to a public Rollins-hosted WPRK music address. Manual review is required for pitch copy, metadata, clean edits, link permissions and fit.

2. KSPC 88.7FM Album and EP Airplay Submission Route
   - Official public route: https://kspc.org/contact/music-submissions/
   - Status: needs_manual_review
   - Pricing model: free
   - Verification notes: official KSPC page accepts albums/EPs by physical mail and digital downloadable package, rejects singles, demos, cassette tapes, non-downloadable streaming-only routes, substantially AI-generated work and pay-for-play. Manual review is required for album/EP eligibility, digital package prep, protected/redacted contact handling and physical mail prep.

3. WTJU 91.1FM Genre Music Director Submission Route
   - Official public route: https://www.wtju.net/submit-music/
   - Status: needs_manual_review
   - Pricing model: free
   - Verification notes: official WTJU page accepts CD, LP, 45 rpm and digital MP3/WAV/FLAC routes, gives U.S. mail and private-carrier addresses, and lists genre contacts. Manual review is required for genre routing, link/file permissions, metadata, follow-up timing and broadcast-library requirements.

4. WMNF 88.5FM Music Department Upload Form and Email Route
   - Official public route: https://www.wmnf.org/submit-your-music-to-wmnf/
   - Status: needs_manual_review
   - Pricing model: free
   - Verification notes: official WMNF page exposes a Music Department upload form and public music email route, with accepted audio formats and file-size/file-count constraints. Manual review is required for upload/file selection, rights, local context, release metadata, clean edits and pitch copy.

5. KZSU Stanford Music Director Email, Mail and Protected Submission Route
   - Official public route: https://kzsu.stanford.edu/contact/
   - Status: needs_manual_review
   - Pricing model: free
   - Verification notes: official KZSU contact page lists Music Directors, music email, physical music mail addresses and a send-us-music route. The send-us-music route produced a cookie/session verification boundary, so it was not used or bypassed.
