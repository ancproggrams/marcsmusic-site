## Verification and enrichment

- CJSR: accepts albums or EPs by purpose-specific email or physical delivery, but not singles. Digital servicing needs actual downloadable files or a Bandcamp code, complete track metadata, track lengths, focus tracks and language warnings; paid-streaming and expiring-transfer links are unsuitable. Its January 2026 AI policy says wholly AI-generated music will not be aired, so MarcsMusic production provenance requires human verification. A separate CAD 200 local advertising offer is optional and not part of free music consideration.
- CFUV: directs all general submissions to `music@cfuv.ca`. A release needs at least three unique tracks, minimum 320-kbps MP3 downloads, a one-page biography and a complete album title and track listing. Physical servicing is generally limited to local artists.
- CFRU: accepts general digital EPs or albums with at least three tracks through `music@cfru.ca`, preferably as 320-kbps MP3 downloads with artist, album, track-list and contact information. Its separate upload form is restricted to musicians within 50 km of Guelph and was excluded for MarcsMusic.
- CJAM: is digital-only and requires at least four original tracks, minimum 192-kbps MP3 or AAC or optional FLAC, complete ID tags and a reliable download link. Spotify links and direct MP3 attachments are rejected.
- CJSF: offers an official National Music Director form and lists `cjsfmusc@sfu.ca` for music. The form grants broadcast rights to CJSF and partner stations and offers identity/discoverability labels; full live controls require human inspection. Singles may be distributed through !earshot but are not considered for CJSF's local library, and non-local physical releases are excluded.
