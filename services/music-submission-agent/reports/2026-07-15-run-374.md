KEXP's official Contact and Submission Guidelines pages authorize digital submissions to:

- `md@kexp.org`

The current first-party requirements are:

- send streaming and downloadable WAV links;
- do not attach audio files;
- include artist name and member pronouns;
- include album or EP title and label when applicable;
- provide phonetic spellings where useful;
