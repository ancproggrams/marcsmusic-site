## Verification and enrichment

- High Tea Music: the official contact page explicitly directs demos to its external Fillout form. Public business contacts include `releases@highteamusic.nl` for label matters and `contact@highteamusic.nl` for other enquiries, but neither is used as a substitute demo route. The label's official releases page lists multiple releases in 2026.
- Liquicity: the official demo page directs all music submissions to Label Engine. The rendered form requests artist and real names, confirmed email, up to five MP3 tracks with titles, optional social profiles and a message. Liquicity says every track is listened to, although a response is not guaranteed.
- Circus Records: the official site states that demos are accepted through LabelRadar only and publishes `info@circus-records.co.uk` for questions, not demo delivery. The route covers Circus Records and Circus Electric; the correct imprint, account/session and any current credit boundary require human review.
- Monstercat: the official contact page links separate LabelRadar portals for Uncaged, Instinct and Silk and says every submitted demo is listened to. Support, press and event contacts are protected/non-plaintext and are not used as submission routes.
- NCS: the official contact page identifies LabelRadar as the demo route and keeps claims reports, commercial licensing and general enquiries separate. The music library shows active recent releases across electronic genres.

Activity was verified from current official pages: High Tea Music lists 2026 releases; Liquicity lists 2026 events and active releases; Circus displays current latest releases; Monstercat lists releases dated July 3 and July 10, 2026; and NCS lists releases published from one day to several weeks before verification.
