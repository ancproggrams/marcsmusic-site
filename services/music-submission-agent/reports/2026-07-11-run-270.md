## Verification and enrichment

- 95bFM: official submission guide verified. It requires both a streaming link and a lossless download link, rejects services requiring the station to create an account or use password-restricted access, accepts FLAC/WAV, and asks for concise artist, track, origin and release-date context. Tracks older than one month are not eligible for high-rotation A/B playlisting.
- Radio One 91FM: official contact page verified. It publishes the current Music Director, `music@r1.co.nz`, a physical postal route and active 2026 programming, but does not publish complete asset/package requirements on that page.
- Radio Control 99.4FM: official contact page verified. It publishes `music@radiocontrol.org.nz`, current station/programme leadership and a postal address; detailed file and metadata requirements are not published.
- RadioActive.FM: official contact page verified. It publishes `music@radioactive.fm`, staff contacts and physical/postal delivery details. Current June-July 2026 interviews, guest mixes and events confirm activity.
- RDU 98.5FM: official 2026 site verified. It publishes `admin@rdu.org.nz`, phone and postal details, but no explicit public music-submission instructions. It is queued only as a manual pre-submission inquiry; no music or links should be sent until RDU confirms the current process.

No email address was guessed, SMTP-probed, MX-probed or contacted. The 95bFM dedicated music mailbox is published through a protected/non-plaintext link and was not decoded.
