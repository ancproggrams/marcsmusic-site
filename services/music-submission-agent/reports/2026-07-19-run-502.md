## 1. NOS Recordings

Official route: `https://nosrecordings.com/demo-submissions`.

NOS Recordings accepts house music only through its first-party demo form. Its published preparation rules require:

- no more than three tracks per submission;
- private SoundCloud links with downloads enabled, or Box/Dropbox links;
- no file attachments;
- a brief introduction covering the artist, achievements, style and label fit;
- complete and polished tracks, although an unmastered track is acceptable when disclosed.

The form also offers a separate choice to have a demo considered for livestream A&R feedback on YouTube or Twitch. That optional promotional-review boundary requires a deliberate human decision.

No mandatory submission fee, login or visible CAPTCHA was found in the public route. The full form fields, privacy text, hidden anti-spam controls and submit-time verification were not exercised. Official support addresses on the terms page rendered only as obfuscated placeholders and were not decoded or inferred; the demo form remains canonical.

Activity is current. On 19 July 2026 the official catalogue listed 62 releases, with **NOS 053 — Whatchu Need** newest, **NOS 052 — NOS in Miami 2026** also out, and the official site promoted a live Summer 2026 collection plus active NOS Radio and NOS TV channels.

## 2. RekHeard Breakerz

Official route: `https://www.rekheardbreakerz.com/`.

RekHeard Breakerz is a DJ-driven Caribbean music discovery platform. The artist form accepts one track per submission and requires:

- Soca, Reggae or Dancehall only;
- one clean, radio-friendly and club-ready English-language single;
- no riddim tracks;
- an existing Spotify release;
- a tagged, non-expiring MP3 download link;
- a non-expiring high-resolution artist-photo link;
- Instagram, optional TikTok, location and a meaningful song story;
- ownership or control of all required rights.

Google Drive links must be shared with `info@rekheardbreakerz.com`. That address is first-party published, syntactically valid and exactly domain-aligned, but it is used for Drive access and general contact rather than as an alternative submission mailbox.

The form includes two significant mandatory consent boundaries. The artist grants permission to review, discuss, play excerpts, feature, publish, share and promote the track, name, image, likeness and submitted materials across podcast, video, website, playlists, social media, email and promotional channels if selected. A second required checkbox subscribes the artist to submission and platform email updates, with an unsubscribe option stated.

The platform is explicitly international: the official site reports more than **440 submissions**, representation from **25+ countries**, **12k+ YouTube subscribers**, a growing DJ network, four streaming Season 1 episodes and active curated playlists.

No fee, login or visible CAPTCHA was found. Hidden Wix anti-spam and submit-time controls remain unknown and were not tested.

## 3. COOL Recordings

Official route: `https://www.coolrecordings.com/`.

The Austrian label publishes a first-party A&R form for:

- House;
- Tech House;
- Afro House.

The visible route requires artist name, direct email, a private SoundCloud or Dropbox track link, social portfolio and an A&R note. Downloads must be enabled. The label rejects unfinished tracks and short snippets and states that it contacts fitting artists within two weeks.

No mandatory submission fee, login or visible CAPTCHA was observed. Hidden anti-spam, consent, privacy, retention and post-selection terms remain a manual-review boundary. No exact first-party business mailbox is published on the official single-page form, so no address was inferred and the form remains canonical.

Activity is current. Beatport lists COOL Recordings releases including **El Patronato** dated **5 June 2026**, **Banger Recipe** and **Disco Nect** dated **15 May 2026**, and **Midnight** dated **25 April 2026**.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact label and genre fit;
- track completion and audio quality;
- private-link access and download permissions;
- master, composition, sample and contributor rights;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- generative-AI origin and disclosure;
- privacy, retention and post-selection terms;
- every final form, link-sharing and consent action.

NOS additionally has a three-track maximum, a no-attachments rule and optional livestream-feedback consent. RekHeard additionally requires Spotify availability, clean English single status, non-expiring MP3/photo links and mandatory promotional and marketing-email consents. COOL additionally requires a finished House/Tech House/Afro House track, social portfolio and A&R note.
