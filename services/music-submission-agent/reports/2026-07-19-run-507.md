## 2. Sonaxx Records

Official route: `https://www.sonaxxrecords.com/en/contact-and-demos`.

Sonaxx Records focuses on Hard Techno, Techno, Industrial, Hard Dance and Schranz. Its first-party form requires or supports:

- complete MP3 320kbps or WAV tracks;
- private SoundCloud, Dropbox, WeTransfer or similar links;
- direct MP3 upload fields displaying a 15MB maximum;
- music that has never been publicly accessible;
- sharing only with Sonaxx rather than mass-submitting;
- privacy acknowledgement.

The page explicitly states that email, Facebook, SoundCloud and Instagram demo submissions are not accepted outside the form. `info@sonaxxrecords.com` is first-party published, syntactically valid and domain-aligned, but is not a demo route.

The process changed on **1 January 2026**: no response within seven days now means rejection. Activity is current. The official Bandcamp catalogue released **CENSURE – Cosa Nostra** worldwide on **10 July 2026**.

## 3. Get Known Radio

Official route: `https://getknownradio.com/submit-music.html`.

Get Known Radio is an independent 24/7 station for Hip-Hop, R&B and club-oriented electronic music, including UK Garage, Jersey Club, Footwork, Baile Funk, Amapiano, Drum & Bass, House and techno-adjacent tracks. The official route explicitly accepts non-US artists without geographic restriction.

The portal asks for:

- one MP3 at 320kbps minimum or WAV;
- a two-to-three-sentence note;
- a genre tag;
- a contact email;
- mandatory confirmation that the submitter has the right to license the track for broadcast.

The official homepage also publishes `contact@getknownradio.com` as a direct-email track-submission route. `support@getknownradio.com` is first-party but is support/newsletter contact only. The dedicated portal remains canonical because some protected email links on the submission page were not consistently resolvable in the public cache.

The terms state that the artist retains ownership but grants a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence for broadcast, streaming, promotion, distribution and reproduction, without compensation unless separately agreed. They also include rights warranties and indemnity. The privacy policy states that **Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise** is used for spam protection. No CAPTCHA was executed.

Activity is current. The official homepage displayed an issue dated **18 July 2026**, a 24/7 stream and recently submitted tracks; the submission, terms and privacy pages were accessible in July 2026.

Manual and legal review is required for programme fit, one-track format and quality, portal-versus-email choice, broadcast-rights confirmation, the broad licence and indemnity, sample clearance, Content ID, AI provenance, reCAPTCHA, privacy and US processing, file retention/removal and the final upload or email.

- exact label, station, genre and catalogue/programme fit;

- Content ID and distributor conflicts;

OX additionally requires unpublished status and an email-only no-attachment workflow. Sonaxx additionally requires never-public/private sharing, privacy acknowledgement and a first-party form.
