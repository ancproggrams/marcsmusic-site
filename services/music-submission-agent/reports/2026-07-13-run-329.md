## Verification and enrichment

### NoCopyrightSounds (NCS)

- Authorized route: NCS's official contact page labels an external link **Demo Submissions** and explicitly directs artists to submit music to NCS on LabelRadar.
- Portal boundary: the NCS-specific LabelRadar page rendered only a dynamic loading shell during passive review. Account, login, submission-credit, pricing, consent, rights and anti-abuse controls must therefore be inspected manually.
- Cost: NCS states no mandatory demo fee. LabelRadar may expose free allowances and optional paid functionality, but no payment is authorized or required by this queue item.
- Label and genre fit: NCS publicly lists bass, bass music, breakbeat, drum-and-bass, drumstep, dubstep, future bass, garage, house, UKG, wave and other electronic categories relevant to MarcsMusic.
- Activity: the official NCS library showed new releases listed as three and four days old on 13 July 2026.
- Email classification: no public music-submission mailbox is exposed. The general-enquiry path is not a substitute for the authorized LabelRadar portal.
- Manual boundary: a human must authenticate, confirm that the NCS campaign is open, review current terms and submission allowances, select the track, approve rights and metadata, and submit through the official portal.

### UKF / Pilot

- Authorized route: UKF's official page says artists who want a track released on UKF or Pilot should complete the first-party demo form.
- Route restriction: the same page explicitly says demos must not be sent to UKF's support address.
- Passive-form limitation: the embedded form did not expose its current fields, accepted file or link types, upload limits, privacy consent, rights declarations or runtime anti-spam controls.
- Cost and access: no account, login, submission fee or mandatory payment is stated on the official page.
- Label and genre fit: UKF's official catalogue and filters cover drum-and-bass, jungle, dubstep, 140, brostep, color bass, riddim, bassline, house, garage, trap, UKG and wave.
- Activity: official releases were dated 8 July 2026 and 9 July 2026.
- Email classification: no public demo mailbox was published; the support route is explicitly excluded.
- Manual boundary: a human must inspect the current form, select the UKF or Pilot destination, verify ownership and release status, approve all metadata and terms, and submit manually.

### Gravitas Recordings

- Authorized route: Gravitas's official demos page says it listens to every submitted demo and requests only unreleased music.
- Delivery requirements: mastered private SoundCloud playlist share links are preferred. The form asks for artist name, real name, email, whether the music was sent to other labels, an explanation of artist/label fit, and optional social profiles.
- Contact-page confirmation: the official contact page separately redirects demo senders to the demos form and specifies mastered private SoundCloud links.
- Cost and access: no account, login, submission fee or mandatory payment is stated.
- Label and genre fit: the catalogue strongly aligns with world bass, global bass, organic electronic, downtempo, dubstep, experimental and other bass-forward MarcsMusic material.
- Activity: the official music catalogue exposes a 2026 section with current releases.
- Email classification: no public demo mailbox was published; the form is the authorized route.
- Manual boundary: a human must verify that the music is mastered and unreleased, confirm private-link access, decide how to answer the simultaneous-submission question, review current privacy and rights text, approve personal data, and submit manually.
