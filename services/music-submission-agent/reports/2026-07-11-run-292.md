## Verification and enrichment

- Radio K / KUOM: the official submission page accepts a physical release or a digital package sent to the protected Music Department address. Digital submissions must link to a `.zip` file through Google Drive, Dropbox or a similar service and preferably contain lossless WAV or FLAC files; Bandcamp download codes or free-release links are also accepted. The page asks for concise artist, focus-track and FCC information and discourages tracking-status requests. The protected address was not decoded.
- WPRB: the official page accepts email submissions at `music@wprb.com` and physical CDs, CD-Rs, LPs, 12-inch, 10-inch and 7-inch media. Physical submissions are prioritized. Because complete digital delivery specifications are not published, final delivery remains manual.
- KALX: the official contact and FAQ pages currently accept only professionally pressed CDs or LPs. Downloads, file transfers, Bandcamp or Spotify links, YouTube videos, CD-Rs and all other digital forms are rejected. The published Music Director mailbox is not used as a digital-submission workaround.
- KVRX: the official contact page directs all music submissions to `music@kvrx.org`, confirms both physical and digital intake and explicitly excludes AI-assisted or AI-generated music. Track provenance and any undocumented delivery specifications require human confirmation.
- KXUA: the official staff page publishes `charts@uark.edu` for the Music Directors, but no full unsolicited-music policy is available. The queue item is therefore restricted to a short inquiry without audio, attachments or track links.
