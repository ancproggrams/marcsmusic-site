## Verification and fit

The current Wepa.Fm site exposes live-radio/now-playing surfaces, a current staff roster, 2026 artist spotlights and a 2026 Calle Ocho Freestyle/Latin Music Stage. The station describes a bilingual format including disco, salsa, freestyle, merengue, bachata, house, reggaeton and pop, plus a worldwide independent-music mission.

`info@wepa.fm` is published in plaintext on the first-party submission page specifically for independent-music submissions. Its syntax, domain and context alignment were checked. The staff page also publishes `jojeda@wepa.fm` for the program/music director, but that address is excluded from the canonical submission route because the dedicated page directs artists to `info@wepa.fm`. No SMTP, MX, catch-all, mailbox-level or deliverability probing was performed.

Carnival or Curaçao may be the closest MarcsMusic fit, subject to a human check of the exact arrangement, clean master, station format and licence suitability.

## Manual-review boundary

A human must review the royalty-free agreement before signing, verify composition/master/sample/contributor rights and authority to license, confirm exclusivity, duration, territory, revocation and royalty treatment, select one suitable clean track, verify 320 kbps MP3 metadata and artwork, confirm international eligibility and attachment limits, check the current AI-origin policy, and send manually.

The agreement itself was not downloaded, executed or returned in this run.

## Deduplication

Exact name, domain, submission-path and mailbox searches in the accessible repository code-search surface returned no match before creation. The page, mailbox and agreement prerequisite are consolidated into one canonical opportunity. Staff, marketing, event, booking and social contacts are excluded. Because the active branch contains a large unmerged historical dataset, the SQLite canonical-domain/email dedupe remains a required pre-send gate.
