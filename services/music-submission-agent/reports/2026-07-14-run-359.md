## Deduplication controls applied

The active pull-request patch was searched by normalized platform name, call sign, first-party domain, purpose-bound email, canonical submission URL and submission-method semantics. Exact text matching alone was not used. This exposed the false negatives caused by renamed routes and URL variations in the prior run.

Future insertion checks should normalize:

1. station or label identity and aliases;
2. registrable domain;
3. role mailbox or protected contact identity;
4. first-party submission path after removing protocol, `www`, trailing slashes and tracking parameters;
5. delivery method and eligibility constraints;
6. previously recorded canonical run.

A candidate should be treated as enrichment when these identity signals resolve to an existing platform, even when the title, page path or explanatory wording differs.
