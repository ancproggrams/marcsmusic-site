## Verification and enrichment

- Radio Wigwam: the official route invites grassroots and independent artists worldwide to register or log in and upload music. Its free Basic Artist plan includes one track per month; paid Premiere and Agency tiers offer more uploads and priority features. Current programming, 2026 awards navigation and current artist/review content demonstrate activity. The terms require ownership or authorization and grant a broad royalty-free, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide non-exclusive license for submitted content. The contact page presented request verification, and the displayed business contact remained protected or masked.
- Lonely Oak Radio: the official uploader accepts a single MP3 or WAV at 320 kbps or better, or an EP/full album as ZIP or RAR containing qualifying files. It asks for an email account, Twitter account and preferably an updated biography. The station says submissions enter its song database, are evaluated for recording quality and genre, and may be scheduled in its twice-daily New Music programming. It is restricted to independent rock and related rock styles. Separate promotion and donation options are optional pricing boundaries; the live single and album endpoints load dynamically.
- Radio Airplay: the official account workflow supports track uploads, targeted internet-radio airplay, reporting and fan discovery. The current pricing table includes a USD 0 plan with one song and 100 one-time bonus plays, plus optional paid packages from USD 12 to USD 250. Its terms, updated June 22, 2026, require worldwide content authorization and grant a non-exclusive, royalty-free, sublicensable worldwide streaming license. The official contact page publishes `support@radioairplay.com` and `premium@radioairplay.com`; these were classified as support and sales contacts, not alternate submission routes.
