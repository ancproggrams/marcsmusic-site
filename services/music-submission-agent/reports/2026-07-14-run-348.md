- Global eligibility: CKUT states that its DJs choose recordings sent by artists and record companies around the world.
- Release eligibility: singles are not accepted. Except for 7-inch vinyl, a submission must contain at least three distinct tracks; remixes do not count as distinct tracks.
- Physical route: CKUT also accepts CDs, cassettes and 12-inch or 7-inch vinyl at its published Music Department address. The station notes that physical releases may receive more attention.
- Genre fit: CKUT emphasizes uncommon, new and daring music and maintains electronic, experimental, techno, electro, house, hip-hop, reggae, world and open-format programming.

### CJAM 99.1 FM Windsor

- Authorized digital route: CJAM's official Submit page directs releases to `cjammd@gmail.com`, a purpose-bound role mailbox published for Music Director Austin DiPietro.
- Digital-only rule: CJAM states that it has moved to digital-only submissions and asks for a reliable downloadable link, preferably through Dropbox, WeTransfer or Google Drive.
- Prohibited delivery: Spotify-only links and direct MP3 email attachments are rejected.
- Release eligibility: the release must contain four or more original tracks; shorter singles and samplers for upcoming albums are not added.
- File requirements: MP3 or AAC must be at least 192 kbps; FLAC is also accepted. Files must contain embedded artist, song title, album, track number, composer and genre tags.
- Supporting information: the email must include artist and release information, either in the body or through a PDF EPK.
- Global and genre fit: CJAM says it shares independent music of any genre created anywhere and specifically programs electronic, techno, hip-hop, dub and other niche genres.
- Activity: the homepage features a July 2026 Singles Club item and charts for 29 June through 5 July 2026, and the current Contact page names the Music Director.
- Business-email verification: `cjammd@gmail.com` appears consistently on the official Submit, Music and Contact pages with explicit submission and Music Director purposes. It is hosted on Gmail rather than the station domain, so a reviewer must recheck the official pages immediately before sending. No SMTP, MX, catch-all, mailbox-level or deliverability probe was performed.
- Manual boundary: a human must confirm the current rules, choose an eligible MarcsMusic album or EP, create the permitted download, inspect quality and embedded tags, verify rights and content labels, and send manually.
