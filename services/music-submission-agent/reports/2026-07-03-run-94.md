## Verification and enrichment notes

- 95bFM was verified through its official Submit Music guidance and active 2026 homepage. The route asks for a streaming link plus a lossless download link, avoids account-gated or passworded download/streaming services and gives release-timing guidance. The public email was redacted by the fetch layer and was not decoded or guessed.
- WFMU was verified through its official Send Music page and Music Department Mail Page. It accepts physical review material routed to the Music Director and exposes a JavaScript-required Music Department contact form.
- KEXP was verified through its official Contact and Submission Guidelines pages. The Music Director route asks for streaming plus WAV download links, release metadata, focus tracks, clean edits, lyrics, credits, bio/one-sheet and relevant regional show context.
- triple j Unearthed was verified through the official active ABC/triple j Unearthed site and ABC Help articles. It requires ABC account login, artist profile setup, audio upload and track form submission; artist uploading is restricted to Australian citizens or residents.
- NTS was verified through the official active homepage, which showed a public `Now taking submissions - send us a mixtape!` call with an `APPLY NOW` prompt. The fetched page did not expose a complete static submission endpoint, so route resolution remains manual.
