### Authorized submission route

SoundChat Radio publishes a dedicated `Submit Your Music` form. The form requests:

- artist name;
- email address;
- track title;
- genre;
- a SoundCloud, Spotify, YouTube or direct-download track link;
- information about the track and artist;
- confirmation of ownership and permission for SoundChat Radio to broadcast the submitted track.

The official guidance says standard submission is completely free and indicates a review period of approximately one to two weeks.

No public email address is labelled as a music-submission destination. The dedicated form is therefore the only canonical route stored for music delivery.

### Published delivery guidance

The official page advises artists to provide:

- high-quality audio, with 320 kbps MP3 or WAV preferred;
- clean or radio edits alongside explicit versions when applicable;
- accurate artist, title and genre metadata;
- a short biography and social-media links.

The visible form itself requests one track-link field rather than a file upload. A human must therefore confirm that the chosen link provides the quality and version required and determine how multiple clean and explicit versions should be represented when relevant.
