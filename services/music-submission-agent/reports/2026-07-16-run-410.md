### Technical ambiguity

The official page describes the WAV option as `ADPCM or ACM 44.1KB, 16-bit stereo WAV`. This wording is unusual and does not provide a conventional, unambiguous sample-rate and codec specification.

The queue therefore does not authorize automatic attachment delivery. A human should prefer a permissioned streaming link or ask KZYX to clarify the required WAV encoding before attaching a file.
