## Verification and enrichment

- IndieXL: provides a public upload form for a new/current single, requires a tagged 320-kbps MP3 of no more than 12 MB, rejects streaming-platform links and explicitly excludes Hip Hop, Rap, House, EDM, Pop, R&B and Urban. Track fit must therefore be checked before any upload.
- KANM: explicitly routes artist submissions to an external Google Form and keeps live-studio-session applications separate. The live form could not be safely inspected through the crawler, so fields, session state, asset requirements and runtime controls remain manual.
- KZUU: says music sent by mail is generally the fastest route and publishes a General Manager address for information about an artist or managed band. Complete media, metadata, clean-edit and return requirements are not published.
- KWVA: directs band demos and music donations to its Music Director mailing address. Its public Music Director email is specifically presented for interview scheduling and is not treated as a digital-music-delivery route.
- KCOU: instructs first-time labels, promoters and independent artists to contact the Music Director for instructions before beginning record service or submitting music. Its policy requests radio-friendly, airable-quality recordings, band contact information and preferably a media kit, with no airplay guarantee.
