## 1. Fresh On The Net — United Kingdom / global

### Authorized route

Current first-party Fresh On The Net surfaces:

- Homepage and current weekly batches: `https://freshonthenet.co.uk/`
- Canonical submission page: `https://freshonthenet.co.uk/submit/`
- Submission help and field guidance: `https://freshonthenet.co.uk/submithelp/`
- Contact and route restriction page: `https://freshonthenet.co.uk/contact/`
- Privacy and data-contact page: `https://freshonthenet.co.uk/about-us/privacy/`

Fresh On The Net expressly welcomes any artist to submit one track per week. Its moderators state that every accepted track is heard. Selected tracks may reach the weekend Listening Post, public reader voting and the following week's Fresh Faves review. The Contact page says the proper music route is the first-party form and that music sent by CD or email is not considered.

### Form fields and delivery requirements

The current first-party form exposes:

- Artist Name
- Track Name
- SoundCloud Track URL
- a checkbox confirming acceptance of the terms and conditions

The track URL must be:

- public;
- available in full;
- worldwide accessible;
- embeddable on the site;
- a SoundCloud track rather than a private, SoundCloud Go or territory-restricted item.

Fresh On The Net also publishes these restrictions:

- only one track may be submitted per artist per week;
- a second track in the same week replaces the first;
- previously submitted tracks may not be submitted again;
- remixes or alternative versions of tracks previously submitted are not accepted;
- artists featured in Fresh Faves must wait three months before submitting another track;
- artist and track names must be labelled accurately;
- social links and a biography should be present in the SoundCloud profile;
- artists must not mobilise friends or fans to vote or publicise a Listening Post appearance.

### Schedule and capacity boundary

Fresh On The Net states that its inbox opens on Monday morning and closes when either:

- 200 tracks have been received; or
- Wednesday evening arrives.

During this run on July 16, 2026, the official submission page displayed the inbox as closed and indicated a July 20 reopening boundary. This route must therefore remain queued and must not be treated as continuously available.

No visible CAPTCHA, login or payment requirement appeared in the passive representation. Live validation, terms consent, anti-spam, honeypot and CAPTCHA state must be rechecked when the inbox opens. The weekly opening and 200-track cap must not be bypassed.

### AI-origin policy conflict

Current first-party representations are not fully consistent:

- the closed submission-page representation states that AI-generated tracks are not accepted and that there are no exceptions;
- another current form representation asks artists to declare when a track was composed wholly or partly using AI and warns that undeclared AI use may lead to removal and a ban.

Because MarcsMusic may include AI-assisted production workflows, a human must apply the strictest visible rule at the time of submission. No track may be submitted unless its provenance is known, accurately declared where permitted and clearly eligible under the live terms. The agent must not infer eligibility from older or conflicting text.

No SMTP, MX, catch-all, mailbox-level or deliverability probing was performed. No alternate address was guessed or inferred.

### Safe submission procedure

A human reviewer must:

- wait for the official Fresh On The Net inbox to reopen;
- reopen the canonical first-party submission page;
- inspect live CAPTCHA, honeypot, validation and consent controls without entering data or bypassing restrictions;
- stop and retain `needs_manual_review` if a protected or unexpected boundary appears;
- review the current AI-origin wording and apply the strictest live rule;
- choose a MarcsMusic track only if its production provenance is fully known and eligible;
- confirm the track is public, full-length, worldwide and embeddable on SoundCloud;
- confirm the exact track has never been submitted before and is not a disallowed remix or alternate version;
- verify artist name, track title, biography and social metadata;
- accept the terms only after reviewing them;
- submit one track once and retain the final confirmation evidence.

Do not submit by email, CD, privacy mailbox, individual volunteer account, direct message, BBC uploader or the conditional Exile FM route as a substitute for the Fresh On The Net form.
