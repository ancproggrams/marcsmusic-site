## 3. Without Warning Radio

Official route: `submissions@withoutwarningradio.com`, published on the station's music-submission and contact pages.

Published requirements include:

- subject line `Artist Music Submission – Artist Name`;
- Hip Hop, R&B, Soul, Dancehall, Poetry, Jazz or Gospel;
- broadcast-quality constant-320kbps MP3;
- tags containing artist/band name, song title and version;
- album or single artwork;
- social information;
- booking or management information.

Management reviews submissions weekly and does not guarantee placement. The station's broader programming description also mentions house, but house is not in the formal submission-genre line and therefore requires manual fit confirmation.

`submissions@withoutwarningradio.com` is first-party published, syntactically valid, domain-aligned and expressly designated for submissions.

Current activity is confirmed by the active 24/7 station presentation, current team schedule, distribution through the station app, TuneIn, Alexa and Roku, a blog post published seven days before verification and © 2026.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact genre, programme, network or release fit;
- clean/FCC-safe or brand-safe content;
- audio, link, attachment, artwork and metadata requirements;
- composition, master, sample, artwork and contributor rights;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- AI-assisted or AI-generated music eligibility;
- broadcast, commercial-premises and royalty treatment;
- privacy, retention and hidden controls;
- international eligibility;
- the final email or form submission.

Radio 1190 additionally requires full-album/EP eligibility and University retention review. Sonair requires manual inspection of the complete form and any submit-time controls. Without Warning Radio requires constant-bitrate verification, package completeness and confirmation of any genre not expressly listed in the submission guidance.
