## 1. CJSF 90.1 FM — Burnaby, British Columbia, Canada

### Authorized route

Current first-party CJSF surfaces:

- Music Department and National Music Director submission guidance: `https://www.cjsf.ca/contact-us/departments/music-department`
- General Submissions page: `https://www.cjsf.ca/contact-us/submission`
- Contact and current staff page: `https://www.cjsf.ca/contact_us`
- Homepage and current programmes: `https://www.cjsf.ca/`
- Separate !earshot Distro information: `https://earshot-distro.ca/`

The Music Department page asks artists to read and complete the National Music Director form to submit a release for distribution. It states that submitted releases are made available to CJSF programmers and may also be added to the !earshot Distro system for access by participating non-profit Canadian campus and community stations.

The general Submissions page also directs all music to the Music Coordinator by mail or `cjsfmusc@sfu.ca`. The direct first-party form and mailbox are treated as one canonical opportunity, not two separate outreach targets.

### Published rights and eligibility requirements

CJSF publishes several material restrictions:

- the person submitting must hold the rights needed to grant broadcast rights;
- submission provides broadcast rights to CJSF and stations with which CJSF shares the release;
- releases are made available to station staff and volunteer programmers, not to the public;
- CJSF does not accept singles into its local library;
- singles may still be shared with partner stations through the distribution workflow;
- CJSF does not accept non-local physical submissions;
- physical delivery is limited to releases from listed communities in the Burnaby/Vancouver region;
- accepted local physical formats are vinyl, CD, cassette and box sets, in that preference order.

MarcsMusic is based outside Canada and is not eligible for the local physical route. A non-Canadian EP or album may only proceed after the current digital eligibility rules are confirmed.

### Form, login and payment boundary

The official page identifies a National Music Director submission form, but passive rendering does not expose its live fields, upload/link controls, validation, consent, CAPTCHA or anti-spam state.

The page also promotes the separate !earshot Distro platform. That platform currently states:

- account sign-up and login are required;
- uploads start at CAD 7 per song or CAD 50 per album, plus tax;
- it is currently available to Canadian labels, distributors, publishers, promoters and independent artists with distribution rights.

The paid/account-gated !earshot Distro path is therefore excluded from this free-first queue item. It must not be used without separate confirmation of MarcsMusic eligibility and explicit payment approval.
