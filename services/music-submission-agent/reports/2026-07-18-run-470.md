## Qfm verification

Qfm 94.3 publishes an official free route for regional, national and international artists. It accepts Jazz, Smooth Jazz, Cool Jazz, Swing, Acid Jazz, Nu-Jazz, Soul, Funk, Blues, World Fusion and related music, while expressly rejecting Rock, Pop, Country and unrelated styles for this route.

The published workflow requires 192 or 320 kbps constant-bitrate MP3 files sourced from the master, complete Artist + Title metadata, trimmed silence, artist information or biography, a ZIP folder and a WeTransfer or Dropbox download link pasted into Qfm's first-party contact form. YouTube, Facebook, Google Drive and SoundCloud links are explicitly rejected. The public form exposes name, email address, subject and message fields.

Current activity was verified from first-party pages carrying a 2026 copyright notice and advertising current FM, DAB+ and three streaming formats. The current program schedule includes daily Sunset Sessions, weekly World Grooves and Chillout Zone programming, and a monthly unsigned-artist show.

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because a human must select a strictly compatible track, prepare and inspect the MP3 and ZIP package, review WeTransfer or Dropbox privacy, expiry and permissions, verify all rights, clarify the unpublished AI-music policy, inspect hidden contact-form controls and approve every external upload and final submission action.
