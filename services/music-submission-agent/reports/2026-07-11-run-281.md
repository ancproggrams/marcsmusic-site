## Verification and enrichment

- WYSO/Novaphonic: the official submission page accepts singles and albums or EPs by email or physical CD. It asks for downloadable high-quality mixed and mastered audio, with WAV preferred, complete artist and release metadata, clean radio edits, a bio or one-sheet and relevant regional show information. Streaming-only links are explicitly rejected.
- KUTX: the official page publishes `music@kutx.org` for on-air rotation and requires a specific subject, a streaming link, a downloadable WAV link, release and artist information, a highlighted top track when applicable, final mastered audio and clean radio edits. MP3 attachments are explicitly rejected.
- Radio Milwaukee: the official music-submission page requires uncompressed mixed and mastered audio, WAV preferred, radio edits and an informational document containing musician details, comments, lyrics, release or show information and useful links. Physical media is not accepted. The live delivery destination or control is not exposed in the static page, so it remains a human-inspection boundary.
- WUMB: the official contact page publishes `wumb@umb.edu` and identifies Jay Moberg as Music and Programming Director, but does not publish current unsolicited-music delivery requirements. Only a concise asset-free routing inquiry is queued.
- WEVL: the official site publishes `wevl@wevl.org`, identifies an active Program Director and documents daily broadcasting, broad music programming and playlists, but does not publish a current unsolicited-music delivery policy. Only a concise asset-free routing inquiry is queued.

Activity was verified from current official pages. WYSO and KUTX maintain live schedules, playlists and music programming; Radio Milwaukee displayed New Music Friday coverage for July 10, 2026; WUMB maintains active new-music and playlist sections; and WEVL states that it broadcasts daily and links recent playlists and archives.
