## Verification and enrichment

- NoCopyrightSounds: the official NCS contact page explicitly labels its authorized route as `Demo Submissions` and links directly to the NCS LabelRadar portal. The official music library showed releases listed as one day, two days and five days old and covers a broad range of electronic styles including bass music, dubstep, drum and bass, future bass, house, trap and dance-pop. The LabelRadar portal is dynamically loaded, so its current account, field, pricing and rights requirements must be inspected in a user-controlled session.
- STMPD RCRDS: the official homepage listed a release dated 2026-07-03 and linked directly to `demo.stmpdrcrds.com`. The Demo Drop accepts original tracks only and excludes remixes and bootlegs. It requires login and SoundCloud connection. The official terms state that no contract or release license is requested at submission, but grant a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual, worldwide and irrevocable right to store the demo and make it public during demo-drop listening sessions. They advise assuming rejection after three months and warn artists to check whether another demo portal has already encumbered the track. `admin@stmpdrcrds.com` is limited to account/platform problems and `privacy@stmpdrcrds.com` is a privacy contact; neither is a demo mailbox.
- Spinnin' Records: the live Talent Pool displayed current most-popular and latest-added tracks and explicitly required an account before demo submission. Its official terms state that use is free and tracks are supplied through SoundCloud links. The terms prohibit bots and automated scripts, require full rights, grant a limited platform-publication license and describe an exclusive four-month option for Spinnin to enter an exploitation agreement. Because the linked terms PDF is labelled `1-2017` while the platform is active in 2026, every legal and operational provision must be rechecked in the live authenticated workflow. The published removal-request email is not an authorized submission route.
