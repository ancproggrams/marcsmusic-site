## 1. Reggae Vibe Media

Official route: `https://reggaevibe.org/media-submission/`.

The first-party upload form and published guidance request:

- artist name and song title;
- a direct audio upload, with a visible maximum of 268.44 MB;
- high-resolution MP3 at 320 kbps or WAV;
- clean radio edits for daytime rotation where applicable;
- metadata containing artist, title and production credits;
- positive or uplifting reggae, dancehall, soca or Afrobeat;
- a short biography and promotional photo for stronger editorial consideration.

Selected tracks may be considered for 24/7 radio rotation, DJ shows, editorial/social features and app placement.

The form exposes a visible `If you are human, leave this field blank` honeypot. It was not touched or bypassed.

The generic site Terms contain unusually broad ownership and use language for “Submissions”. Because the media-upload page is a specifically requested route, the exact legal interaction between those generic provisions and uploaded masters requires human legal review before any file is supplied.

`thereggaevibe@gmail.com` is first-party published and syntactically valid, but it is a free-mail general contact and not an authorized replacement for the form.

Activity is current: the official station page shows active 24/7 programming, and the site published editorial items on **9 July and 15 July 2026**.

## 2. Crags Radio

Official route: `https://cragsradio.co.uk/submit/`.

The first-party airplay form requests:

- artist or band name;
- track title;
- email address;
- a direct track upload;
- social media links;
- additional information about the track or artist.

A presenter reviews the submission and may notify the artist if a programme will play it.

The current schedule includes Unsigned Sessions, dance, house, electronic, chillout, indie, pop, rock and globally oriented programming. The station states that it streams 24/7 to a global audience and also operates a Best International Artist category in its Unsigned Music Awards.

`studio@cragsradio.co.uk` and `info@cragsradio.co.uk` are first-party published, syntactically valid and domain-aligned, but neither is presented as an alternative music-submission route.

The published privacy page is an uncustomized demo template that names a demo-site URL rather than the live Crags domain. Audio-file storage, retention, access and deletion therefore require explicit manual confirmation before upload.

Activity is current: the official site provides a full weekly schedule, describes a Studio 2 opening in **July 2026**, and published a news item on **16 July 2026**.

## 3. Future SynthPop Radio

Official route: `https://synthpopradio.com/music-submission`, linking to `https://form.jotform.com/252044079959063`.

The officially linked Jotform requests:

- first and last name;
- email;
- a link from which the station can download the music;
- relevant website, YouTube and social context;
- a yes/no choice for permission to upload and feature a music video on the station’s YouTube channel;
- mandatory agreement to broadcast-permission terms.

The form states that submission grants permission to play the track on air, that ownership remains with the original rights holder and that removal can be requested. Video permission is separately optional.

Relevant formats include synthpop, futurepop, darkwave, EBM, industrial, synthwave and broader electronic pop.

`futuresynthpopradio@gmail.com` is first-party published and syntactically valid, but it is a free-mail contact, is not domain-aligned and is not identified as a substitute for the Jotform.

Activity is current: the official homepage displayed recently played tracks dated **18 July 2026**, a live programme schedule and a June 2026 chart.

- exact station, programme and genre fit;
