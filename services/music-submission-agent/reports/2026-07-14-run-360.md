Official evidence:

- Website: `https://wfmu.org/`

WFMU's official homepage contained program archives and playlists dated July 14, 2026 and identified the station as independent and freeform. Its format is compatible with selected MarcsMusic electronic, experimental, ambient, dub, reggae, global-fusion and independent releases, although no placement is implied.

### Contact verification

Jessica Romoff is named on the first-party submission page as Music Director. WFMU's official Music Department contact route is a JavaScript-dependent form. No plaintext music-submission mailbox was exposed, so no email address was decoded, inferred or guessed.

### Manual-review controls

- Confirm the current postal or courier address immediately before shipping.
- Select an appropriate physical format and release.
- Prepare international postage, carrier documentation and customs declarations manually.
- Use the Music Department form only for a concise routing question after a human inspects its live JavaScript validation and anti-abuse controls.
- Do not automatically enter form data or send a physical package.

## 2. KZSU Stanford 90.1 FM — Stanford, United States

### Authorized route

KZSU's official Contact page names the current Music Directors and publishes `music@kzsu.stanford.edu`. Its Wednesday Night Live page explicitly states that music for regular air rotation may be sent as a digital copy to that mailbox or mailed as physical media. The official Contact page also publishes postal and UPS/FedEx addresses.

Official evidence:

- Website: `https://kzsu.stanford.edu/`
- Contact and staff directory: `https://kzsu.stanford.edu/contact/`
- Regular-rotation submission statement: `https://kzsu.stanford.edu/wnl/`
- Linked guideline shortcut: `https://go.kzsu.fm/sendmusic`

### Activity and catalogue fit

KZSU's official homepage exposed live on-air functionality, its Contact page listed current staff, and the site contained a featured program originally broadcast December 3, 2025. KZSU's freeform programming and its published history of electronic, experimental, hip-hop, reggae, Brazilian and other diverse music make selected MarcsMusic releases potentially relevant.

### Email verification

`music@kzsu.stanford.edu` was verified by:

- repeated first-party publication;
- valid email syntax;
- alignment with Stanford's institutional domain;
- explicit Music Director and regular-air-rotation purpose.

No SMTP, MX, catch-all, mailbox-level or deliverability probe was performed.

### Manual-review controls

The official guidelines could not be fully inspected passively. The music portal requested a temporary cookie and the official `go.kzsu.fm/sendmusic` shortcut returned a gateway error. A human must therefore verify the current accepted release types, file formats, bitrate, attachment or download-link rules, metadata, release window, international eligibility and any live anti-abuse controls immediately before outreach.

Only the Music Director route may be used. Unrelated staff mailboxes must not be bulk-copied.

Queue status: `needs_manual_review`
