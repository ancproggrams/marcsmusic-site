## Verification and enrichment

### KALX 90.7FM

- Authorized route: the official KALX contact page accepts only professionally pressed physical CDs or LPs addressed to the Music Director at the University of California, Berkeley.
- Delivery address: `KALX, Attn: Music Director, University of California, 26 Social Sciences Building #5650, Berkeley, CA 94720-5650, United States`.
- Excluded formats and routes: KALX explicitly rejects downloads, file transfers, Bandcamp, Spotify, YouTube, digital submission forms and CD-Rs. The route must not be converted into an email or web-form submission.
- Business-email validation: `music@kalx.berkeley.edu` is officially published for Music Directors Dasha Shevchenko and Ava Hoener, but is not authorized as a digital music-delivery route. `mail@kalx.berkeley.edu` is a valid official general mailbox that explicitly says not to send music submissions, interview requests or press releases.
- Genre fit: current specialty programming covers electronic, techno, house, jungle, experimental, avant-garde, world music, reggae, dub and underground hip-hop.
- Activity: the official site displayed current schedules, recently played tracks and July 2026 station programming and events.
- Cost and access: there is no stated submission fee, account, login, CAPTCHA or mandatory payment. Physical manufacturing, postage and any customs costs remain with the sender.
- Manual boundary: a human must confirm that an eligible professionally pressed MarcsMusic CD or LP exists, verify rights and release status, select the release, confirm the current address, approve costs, prepare customs information and mail the package.

### WFMU

- Authorized route: WFMU's official Send Us Music page welcomes vinyl, CDs, CD-Rs and cassettes for Music Director review.
- Postal address: `WFMU, c/o Jessica Romoff (Music Director), PO Box 2011, Jersey City, NJ 07303-2011, United States`.
- UPS/FedEx address: `WFMU, Jessica Romoff, 43 Montgomery Street, 4th Floor, Jersey City, NJ 07302, United States`.
- Contact boundary: the linked Music Department Mail Page contains name, email, subject and message fields and requires JavaScript. It is classified only as a manual inquiry or follow-up route; it does not publish a plaintext submission mailbox or authorize digital music delivery.
- Genre fit: WFMU's independent freeform programming is relevant to experimental electronic, ambient, avant-garde, world, reggae, dub, noise and other unconventional independent music.
- Activity: official playlists, programme archives and station pages showed current activity through July 13, 2026.
- Cost and access: there is no stated submission fee, account, login, CAPTCHA or mandatory payment for the physical route. Media production, postage and any customs costs remain with the sender.
- Manual boundary: a human must choose the release and physical format, verify rights and release status, confirm the current address and carrier method, approve costs, prepare the package and customs information and send it manually.
