### CJSW

- Authorized route: the first-party Music Submissions page links directly to the 2026 CJSW Jotform.
- Release eligibility: only digital EPs or albums released within the previous three months, containing at least three unique tracks. Singles are not accepted.
- Audio requirements: the full release must be supplied as MP3 files inside one ZIP archive.
- Required form data: email, artist and release names, label or self-released status, EP/full-length status, official release date, genre, website or streaming URL, location, short bio or release description and RIYL.
- Required assets: full-release ZIP and album artwork. A one-sheet PDF is optional.
- Rights and retention boundary: the form asks permission to share the release with programmers for airplay and permanently add it to CJSW's digital library.
- Local-only boundary: Calgary-area artists may separately opt into a signed campus and community station mailout. This option must not be selected automatically for non-local artists.
- Anti-spam boundary: the rendered form contains a `Should be Empty` field. It is treated as a honeypot and the workflow is manual-only.
- Protected-contact boundary: the Music & Performance Director assistance email is exposed through a protected first-party link and was not decoded or guessed.
- Pricing and access: no submission fee, login or confirmed CAPTCHA requirement was observed.
- Activity: CJSW displayed active live programming, an operating schedule and 2026 editorial content.
