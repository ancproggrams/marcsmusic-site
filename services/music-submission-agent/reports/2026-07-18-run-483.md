## 2. Hot Wax Radio

Official route: the first-party JavaScript-dependent submission form at `https://hotwaxradio.com/wp/submit-music/`.

The published route accepts MP3 files through Dropbox or Google Drive and rejects unsolicited attachments, ZIP/WAV delivery and Spotify/SoundCloud links. Eligible categories include reggae, world music and several electronic/dance styles. The station explicitly rejects AI-generated submissions.

The published terms also require legal review: they allow promotional distribution through radio, promotion, podcasts and suitable channels, state that Hot Wax does not collect or remit royalties, and include a waiver of mechanical and airplay royalties for its stream. Third-party material may require releases or mechanical waivers. Optional paid promotional services are separate from the editorial submission route.

Manual review is required for the AI prohibition, track fit, cloud-link access, rights, releases, royalty waiver, promotional scope, privacy/retention, JavaScript/anti-spam controls, paid-service separation and the final submission.

The site presents an active 24/7 station and schedule. Official anniversary material describes continuous operation since 2009 and worldwide artist support, and public playlist monitors recorded station activity in July 2026.

No direct submission email was stored. `patrick@relaxmusicgroup.com` is an official media contact but not an authorized submission route.

Official route: the first-party demo form and `music@zaccone-music.it`.

The Italian electronic label states that it works with DJs and producers worldwide. It invites demos in Afro House, Melodic House, Progressive House and related melodic electronic styles using a private SoundCloud or other valid music link.

The visible form requests website, name, email, music link, request type and message. No mandatory fee, account, direct upload or visible CAPTCHA was identified.

Manual review is required for subgenre and label fit, private-link accessibility, all music and contributor rights, AI eligibility, privacy/retention, separation from booking/newsletter contacts, potential contract terms and the final form or email action.

`music@zaccone-music.it` was verified through first-party publication, syntax, domain alignment and explicit demo purpose. `booking@zaccone-music.it` and `newsletter@zaccone-music.it` were excluded from the submission route. No mailbox-level or deliverability probe was performed.
