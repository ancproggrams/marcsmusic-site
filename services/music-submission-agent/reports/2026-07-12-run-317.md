## Verification and enrichment

### KZSU Stanford

- Authorized digital route: the official Contact page links a dedicated `send music` portal at `go.kzsu.fm/sendmusic`.
- Portal boundary: the destination redirects to KZSU's first-party Zookeeper music system, which requires a small temporary cookie to verify the browser and maintain a secure session.
- Passive-verification limit: the cookie was not accepted, so the current fields, file limits, rights language and any runtime verification controls were not inspected.
- Authorized postal route: KZSU Music, P.O. Box 20510, Stanford, CA 94309-0510.
- UPS/FedEx route: KZSU, Memorial Hall, 540 Memorial Way, Stanford, CA 94305.
- Business-email verification: `music@kzsu.stanford.edu` is published for the Music Directors. It was verified as plaintext and first-party, but the dedicated portal and postal addresses remain the authoritative submission routes for this record.
- Pricing: no submission fee is stated; optional physical media, packaging and international postage are sender-funded.
- Activity: the official site displayed a currently-on-air section, live streams, archive player, schedule system and current staff listing.

### WCBN

- Authorized route: WCBN MUSIC, WCBN-FM, 515 E. Jefferson St. (530 Student Activities Bldg.), Ann Arbor, MI 48109-1316.
- Eligibility: WCBN says it accepts music from any and all musicians willing to send a hard copy of their album.
- Preferred media: CD or vinyl.
- Editorial statement: WCBN says it listens to everything received and gives every submission a chance with its DJs.
- Genre routing: a release may be sent care of the relevant specialist director, such as jazz or hip-hop; otherwise it should be addressed to WCBN MUSIC.
- Contact boundary: the Music Directors email is displayed through a protected or redacted first-party link in passive extraction. It was not decoded, guessed or used as a digital submission route.
- Pricing: no submission fee is stated; hard-copy media, packaging and international postage are sender-funded.
- Activity: WCBN states that it broadcasts 24/7 and provides active live-stream, archive and schedule pages, current staff listings and board terms extending through 2026.

### KDVS

- Authorized UPS/FedEx route: KDVS 90.3 FM, ATTN: MUSIC DEPARTMENT, 14 Lower Freeborn, UC Davis, Davis, CA 95616.
- Authorized USPS route: ATTN: MUSIC DEPARTMENT, ASUCD-KDVS, University of California, 1 Shields Ave, Davis, CA 95616.
- Physical-only boundary: the Contact page states that all new music must be sent to the mailing address and that submissions are physical-only.
- Genre routing: the FAQ permits replacing `ATTN: MUSIC DEPARTMENT` with `ATTN: METAL DEPT.`, `ATTN: HIP HOP DEPT.`, `ATTN: PUNK DEPT.`, `ATTN: ELECTRONIC DEPT.` or `ATTN: EXPERIMENTAL DEPT.`.
- Business-email verification: `musicdept@kdvs.org` is published for music review, library maintenance, mail sorting and record-label contact. Because the adjacent instruction requires physical delivery, it is not queued as a digital submission inbox.
- Pricing: no submission fee is stated; physical media, packaging and international postage are sender-funded.
- Activity: the official site displayed a live web player, current staff, schedule and charts, including a Weekly Top 30 dated 2026-03-10.
