## Verification and enrichment

### Kiss FM Australia

- Authorized route: the official producer page directs artists to the first-party contact form and says the application is free.
- Audience and fit: Kiss FM identifies itself as an independent Melbourne station broadcasting and streaming to Australia and the world. Its submission page targets local and international electronic-music listeners and accepts all forms of dance music except commercial material already played by commercial stations.
- Required context: genre, artist name, track, album or EP name and mix, a biography written directly in the message, artist location, artwork, release date and label information.
- Accepted delivery: attached MP3 or WAV, a password-free Dropbox link or a private SoundCloud link with downloading enabled. YouTube and Spotify links are explicitly rejected.
- Rights boundary: the submitter must have the right to distribute and share the track.
- Form boundary: the contact form requires email, name, subject, query category and message. It contains a honeypot field and must be completed manually with `Programming` selected.
- Protected-contact boundary: the page also presents a direct station delivery address through protected passive rendering. It was not decoded or stored.
- Current activity: the official site displayed July 2026 news and a June 2026 A-rotation update.
- Cost: no submission fee is stated. Membership, patron and advertising routes are separate and are not submission requirements.
- MarcsMusic fit: strong for underground electronic, house, techno and bass-oriented releases, provided the selected track is not positioned as mainstream commercial programming.

### Fresh 92.7

- Authorized route: the official FAQ publishes `music@fresh927.com.au` specifically for music enquiries.
- Station scope: Fresh describes itself as an Adelaide youth and community station presenting international and local music and says it was established to serve demand for electronic music.
- Locality boundary: the music-submission answer is phrased for a `local artist`. International eligibility for a Netherlands-based MarcsMusic release is therefore not assumed.
- Format boundary: the FAQ does not state accepted attachment or link formats, release-age limits, metadata rules, rights declarations, AI-origin policy or response expectations.
- Safe next step: a human should first confirm international eligibility or send an asset-light enquiry, then prepare a track package only after the station confirms its preferred method.
- Activity: the official site displayed a live player and current weekly shows, including `Fresh Off The Block`.
- Email verification: `music@fresh927.com.au` was checked through first-party publication, syntax, domain alignment and explicit role relevance. `admin@fresh927.com.au` and `sponsorship@fresh927.com.au` are not alternative submission routes.
- Cost: no submission fee, login or payment requirement is stated.
- MarcsMusic fit: high genre alignment for electronic and dance releases, but geographic eligibility must be confirmed before sending music.
