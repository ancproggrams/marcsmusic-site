## Verification notes

- KVRX was verified from its official KVRX site and contact page. The route lists Music Department submission routing, says both physical and digital submissions are currently accepted, and notes that AI-assisted/generated music will not be considered.
- KSCU was verified from its official site and About page. The page explicitly instructs artists to submit music for airplay through the Music Director route, while the live site identifies KSCU as Santa Clara University's student-run station.
- KALX was verified from its official Berkeley site and contact page. The route lists Music Directors and says airplay submissions are currently physical-only: professionally pressed CDs or LPs, with no downloads, file transfers, Bandcamp/Spotify links, YouTube videos, CD-Rs or other digital forms.
- KZSC was verified from its official Santa Cruz site, staff/contact page and Send Us a Message workflow. The station has current 2026 activity, public Music Director roles, physical package addresses and a contact form with a Music Submission selector plus validation field.
- KCSB was verified from its official Santa Barbara site and Artist Inquiry page. The route invites artists, bands and DJs for live on-air performance, station events/concerts, music-library addition and song/video premieres through an external form.
