## Verification notes

- FBi Radio was verified from its official active site and Contact page. The station describes itself as championing emerging Sydney music and culture, and the Contact page lists a public Music Submissions route. Human review is required for pitch fit, release metadata and link/package readiness.
- PBS 106.7FM was verified from its official active site and Submit Your Music page. PBS accepts local and international music, prefers the digital form, requires a stream and high-quality download link, rejects Spotify/Deezer links for the stream field and exposes CAPTCHA. Physical CD/vinyl handling is also listed.
- 4ZZZ was verified from its official Music Submissions page. It says 4ZZZ is not playlisted, submissions are considered for the digital library and announcer access, non-downloadable streams are not accepted, and the page links to external music-submission and Album of the Week forms.
- Triple R was verified from its official active site and Submit Music page. It is not playlisted, lets artists directly service presenters or the station, and asks for no-login stream links, high-quality non-expiring download links, clear file names, short bios, gig details and optional physical release routing.
- 2SER was verified from its official active site and How to Submit Music page. The page exposes a Submit Your Music form for airplay consideration with required identity, contact, location, release, bio and social fields after a form-loading boundary.

## Deduplication

Repository code search for FBi Radio, PBS, 4ZZZ, Triple R and 2SER returned no existing matching run entries before creation. The new rows were therefore treated as unique for this batch.
