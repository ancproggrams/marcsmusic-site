## Radio Nano — Oslo, Norway

Radio Nano's official submission page explicitly welcomes **local and international acts**. It offers two authorized digital routes:

1. the preferred first-party **Submit Your Music** form; or
2. the purpose-specific mailbox `music@radionano.com`.

The email alternative permits either a song attachment or a link to an online service. A one-page press release or artist biography should accompany the music.

The observed form fields are:

- name;
- email;
- role;
- message, including a song URL;
- file uploads.

Only one route should be used for a release.
