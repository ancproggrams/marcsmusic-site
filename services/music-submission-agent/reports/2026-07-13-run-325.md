## Verification and enrichment

### Edge Radio 99.3FM

- Authorized route: the official Submit Your Music page provides a protected first-party address for digital submissions and a physical Music Department address for CDs.
- Geographic scope: Edge states that it supports new and emerging artists in Tasmania, Australia and around the world.
- Digital package: short bio, upcoming-gig details, a streaming link and a high-quality downloadable file in 320 kbps MP3, AAC, WAV, FLAC or OGG.
- Physical option: the station suggests multiple CD copies for distribution to relevant programs; this is optional and requires postage and human approval.
- Identity boundary: only Tasmanian artists should label submissions as `Tasmanian Music Submission`; MarcsMusic must not use that subject marker.
- Protected-contact boundary: the digital mailbox is rendered through first-party email protection and was not decoded or stored.
- Current activity: the official site showed July 2026 posts and a current weekly guide with `New Music on Edge Radio`, the electronic/experimental `Sub-sequence` program, digital-beats/world programming and reggae/hip-hop programming.
- Cost: no editorial fee is stated. Paid on-air promotional campaigns are separate and are not required for airplay consideration.
- MarcsMusic fit: strong for electronic, experimental, bass, reggae, hip-hop and world-fusion material.

### SYN Media

- Authorized route: `music@syn.org.au` is published on SYN's official contact page specifically for music submissions.
- Required package: a download link to a 320 kbps MP3 or WAV, a press release or artist bio, track or EP/album title, release date and relevant website, social, gig or previous-release links.
- Attachment rule: audio files should not be attached directly to the email.
- Review process: Music Directors review submissions on a rolling basis and may not respond to every submission.
- Route separation: `music.content@syn.org.au` is for interview requests, not the primary music-delivery route.
- Email verification: the mailbox was checked for first-party publication, syntax, domain alignment and explicit role relevance.
- Current activity: SYN's 2026 archive includes a live review dated 25 May and coverage of the Glitch Melbourne electronic festival dated 30 April.
- Cost: no submission fee, account, membership or payment requirement is stated.
- MarcsMusic fit: good for independent electronic, dance, pop, hip-hop and alternative releases aimed at a youth audience.

### JOY 94.9

- Authorized route: the official Submit Your Music page provides a protected first-party music address and asks for a stream link.
- Eligibility: JOY invites artists who are Australian, independent or identify as part of LGBTIQA+ communities. MarcsMusic can be reviewed under the independent-artist criterion.
- Programming fit: daytime programming includes upbeat pop, R&B and uplifting remixes; specialist programs cover alternative, world and additional genres.
- Global scope: JOY states that it celebrates Australian and global music, with a strong focus on rainbow artists where possible.
- Optional routes: physical CD and bio delivery is accepted; an artist may also approach a relevant specialty show directly. Both require separate human approval.
- Protected-contact boundary: the central music mailbox was not decoded or stored.
- Adjacent-form boundary: the CAPTCHA and honeypot on the page belong to an unrelated newsletter signup and are not a music-submission form.
- Current activity: Season Two began on 6 July 2026, and the official homepage displayed podcasts dated 10–12 July 2026.
- Cost: no editorial submission fee, login or payment requirement is stated. Donations, memberships and commercial services are separate.
- MarcsMusic fit: strong for uplifting electronic, remixes, pop/R&B crossover, alternative and world-fusion releases.
