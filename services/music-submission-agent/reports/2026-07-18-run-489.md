## 1. SoundChat Radio

Official route: `https://soundchatradio.com/submit-music`.

The free first-party form accepts a SoundCloud, Spotify, YouTube or direct-download track link and asks for artist name, email, title, genre and background information. Published guidance prefers MP3 320kbps or WAV source quality, requests radio edits alongside explicit versions where applicable, and asks for correct metadata, a short bio and social links.

Submission confirms ownership and grants SoundChat Radio permission to broadcast. The exact licence duration, royalty treatment, AI policy, privacy/retention and submit-time anti-spam controls require manual review.

`info@SoundChatRadio.com` and `irishandchin@gmail.com` are first-party general contacts but were not substituted for the official form.

Activity is supported by the live 24/7 music schedule and an official story dated March 16, 2026. Audience-size figures are retained only as unverified first-party claims.

## 2. JAM Audio Live

Official route: `https://jamaudio.live/artist-submissions`, which links to an external Google Form.

JAM Audio Live accepts independent artists from every genre and supports Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, YouTube, Bandcamp, Dropbox, Google Drive or direct music links. Artists are asked to provide a short bio, origin and song story, mark explicit lyrics and confirm permission to submit.

Every submission is described as human reviewed for possible live rotation, artist spotlights, charts, interviews and future broadcasts. The linked Google Form could not be passively loaded, so its live fields, login/session state, CAPTCHA, upload behavior, consent and storage terms remain unresolved and require manual inspection.

Optional paid artist services were recorded as a separate pricing boundary and are not required for editorial submission. A protected contact address was not decoded.

Activity is confirmed by the live 24/7 stream and June 2026 spotlight pages for artists from Croatia, Brazil and Glasgow.

## 3. Discover YOU RADIO

Official route: `https://discoveryouradio.com/artist-submissions`.

The station says initial rotation evaluation is free and that only its official application is reviewed. Tracks must be radio-ready and non-explicit. The form asks for artist and contact information, an iTunes or alternate song link, biography, inspiration, lyrics or instrumental status, one or two genres, a fit statement, optional ISRC/PRO/social/website/purchase links, a Suno Pro/record-label-interest response and a yes/no paid-promotion preference.

If selected, Discover YOU RADIO says it purchases the track on iTunes, adds it to rotation and makes it searchable in its listener request catalog. International eligibility, iTunes territory availability, rights, AI/Suno disclosure, optional marketing choices, privacy/retention and submit-time controls require manual review.

`submit@discoveryouradio.com` and `admin@discoveryouradio.com` are first-party published in the April 13, 2026 privacy policy. Email was not treated as a canonical substitute because the submission page requires the official form.

Activity is supported by the active Human Ear Queue, current request lineup, 2026 privacy policy and current 2026 Scout's Briefing.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact station and genre fit;
- track selection and clean/explicit status;
- music-link access, permissions and expiry;
- artist metadata, biography, lyrics and social details;
- composition, master, sample and contributor rights;
- broadcast and promotional-use permission;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- AI-assisted or AI-generated eligibility;
- privacy, retention, consent and hidden controls;
- international eligibility where not explicit;
- the final form action.

JAM Audio Live additionally requires complete manual inspection of the external Google Form. Discover YOU RADIO requires separation of free evaluation from optional paid services and explicit opt-in choices. SoundChat requires review of the broad broadcast-permission wording.
