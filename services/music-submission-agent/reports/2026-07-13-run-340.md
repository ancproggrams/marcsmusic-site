## Verification and enrichment

### RTRFM 92.1

- Authorized route: the official Submit Your Music page invites artists to share a latest or upcoming release through the first-party form for review by the Music Team.
- International eligibility: the required State field includes an `Overseas` option, so the route is not limited to Australian artists.
- Required material: personal and artist details, city and state, optional biography upload, social links, upcoming gigs, a stream link and a free music-download link.
- Audio guidance: RTRFM asks for a downloadable WAV or high-quality MP3 link through services such as Google Drive, Dropbox, SoundCloud or Bandcamp and says not to attach audio to email.
- Timing: the station recommends submitting approximately two weeks before release when seeking consideration for premieres or airplay.
- Physical route: finished CDs or EPs are optional, but physical copies must be accompanied by a free digital submission. The physical route is not automated.
- Anti-spam boundary: the form contains a validation field explicitly marked to remain unchanged. It was not touched.
- Protected business contacts: the submission and Music Director email links are displayed through Cloudflare protection. They were not decoded, guessed, stored or used.
- Genre fit: RTRFM's independent programming is relevant to selected electronic, dance, beats, experimental, ambient, reggae, dub and world-fusion releases from MarcsMusic.
- Activity: the official site displayed current live programming, weekly featured music, current stories and events during verification on 2026-07-13.
- Cost and access: no submission fee, login, account or mandatory payment is stated.
- Manual boundary: a human must select the release, verify rights and release timing, approve the personal data and any biography upload, prepare the free high-quality download, leave the anti-spam field blank and perform the final submission.

### PBS 106.7FM

- Authorized route: the official Submit Your Music page says PBS welcomes new music from local and international acts and prefers submission through its first-party digital form.
- Required material: identity and location details, artist or band name, a short biography, optional social and gig information, a stream link and a required high-quality download link.
- Stream rules: SoundCloud, YouTube and Bandcamp are accepted as stream examples; Spotify and Deezer links are explicitly not accepted.
- Download rules: PBS requests a 320 kbps AAC/MP3 or WAV-equivalent download.
- Human-verification boundary: the form contains both a leave-blank anti-spam field and CAPTCHA verification. Neither was entered or completed.
- Optional consent: newsletter signup is optional and must not be preselected without user approval.
- Physical route: CDs or vinyl may be mailed or delivered in person; PBS recommends four to five copies with a one-page press release or biography. Physical delivery is not automated.
- Genre fit: PBS's support for under-represented music and broad community programming is relevant to selected electronic, experimental, downtempo, reggae, dub, roots and world-fusion releases from MarcsMusic.
- Activity: the official site displayed current programming, 2026 feature music, news, sessions and scheduled events during verification on 2026-07-13.
- Cost and access: no submission fee, login, account or mandatory payment is stated.
- Manual boundary: a human must choose the release, verify rights and broadcast suitability, approve all personal information and optional consent, prepare a compliant stream and download link, leave the anti-spam field blank, complete the CAPTCHA and submit manually.
