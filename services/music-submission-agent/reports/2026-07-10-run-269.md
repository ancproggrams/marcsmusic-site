## Verification and enrichment

- CKXU: official digital submission form and physical route verified. It accepts EPs and LPs, rejects singles, accepts MP3 only, rejects streaming-only links, and requests detailed release, track, identity, language and content-warning metadata. The page also contains a separate older paid mail-out section with 2023 deadlines; it is not treated as part of the free station-library route.
- UMFM/CJUM: official Music Department FAQ verified. It accepts physical CDs or secure downloadable links through services such as WeTransfer, Dropbox, Google Drive, Bandcamp and Disco, rejects individual audio-file email attachments, and accepts EPs of four songs or more. Current 2026 news, schedules and charts verified station activity.
- CFUR: official Submissions page verified. It accepts digital MP3s through cloud links, attachments, Earshot Distro or Bandcamp codes and physical CDs, vinyl and cassettes. WAV is not accepted, metadata is mandatory, and albums or EPs are preferred.
- CHMR: official Submit Your Music page and linked digital form verified. MP3 is preferred and physical submissions are accepted. The published terms state that releases become station property and include disposal, fundraising-sale and copyright-risk waiver provisions, so explicit rights review is mandatory.
- CIVL: official current Music Director contact and public inquiry form verified. The station showed active programming, charts and May 2026 news, but complete file-format and submission-package instructions are not published; a manual pre-submission inquiry is required.

No email address was guessed, SMTP-probed, MX-probed or contacted. Protected/non-plaintext contact links were not decoded.
