## Verification and enrichment

- KRFC: the official submission page welcomes releases across genres and provides both a public upload form and a physical-CD route. It requests artist and release metadata, focus tracks, regional dates, a streaming link, an MP3/WAV file and a one-sheet, states a 300 MB maximum per file, and contains a hidden validation/honeypot field. The route is manual only.
- Radio Boise/KRBX: the official contact page explicitly directs artists to the station's Submit Your Music page. The embedded form loads dynamically, so its live fields, declarations, file or link constraints and any runtime human-verification controls require human inspection. The publicly listed general station mailbox is not treated as a substitute submission route.
- WNCW: the official page prefers mailed CDs and also accepts WAV or MP3 digital delivery to `martin@wncw.org` through a common downloadable service such as Dropbox or Google Drive. Singles, EPs and full-length releases are eligible, and a concise one-sheet is recommended.
- KZMU: the official site identifies Cozy Soul as Music and Programming Director and publishes `cozy@kzmu.org`, but does not publish a current unsolicited-music delivery policy or asset specifications. Only a concise pre-submission inquiry without files or track links is queued.
- KDNK: the official contact and people pages identify Cody Lee as Music Director and publish `cody@kdnk.org`, but do not publish current formats, link rules, attachment limits, release windows or metadata requirements. Only a routing inquiry without music assets is queued.
