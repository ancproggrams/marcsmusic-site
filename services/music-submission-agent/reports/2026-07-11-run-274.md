## Verification and enrichment

- WXYC: the official contact page explicitly directs music submissions and distribution concerns to `md@wxyc.org`. It separately reserves `outreach@wxyc.org` for event coordination, promotion and artist contacts. No audio-format, attachment, cloud-link, metadata, clean-edit or release-window requirements are published, so the delivery package must be confirmed manually.
- WUSB: the official contact page authorizes music delivery to `md.wusbradio@gmail.com` or to the Music Director at its Stony Brook University postal address. The site does not publish digital format, attachment, link, metadata or physical-media preferences, so a human must choose and prepare the route.
- WHRB: the official contact page says promo requests should preferably use physical CDs or LPs and must be addressed to the appropriate department or a named member. Unassigned submissions may be treated as inappropriate. Programming and department business mailboxes are public, but they were recorded as contacts rather than inferred as permission for digital music delivery.
- WFMU: the official Send Music page accepts vinyl, CD, CD-R and cassette review copies and publishes separate postal and UPS/FedEx addresses addressed to Music Director Jessica Romoff. A linked Music Department message form requires JavaScript and is appropriate for questions, not an automated substitute for the physical route.
- WPRK: the official Music Submissions page explicitly directs submissions to `wprkmusic@rollins.edu`. It does not publish accepted formats, attachment limits, download-link rules, metadata fields, clean-edit rules or response expectations, so a human must confirm the package before sending.

Official activity was verified from current station pages. WXYC publishes 2026 station and festival coverage; WUSB lists active May-June 2026 programming and playlists; WHRB publishes 2026 music coverage and a live schedule; WFMU publishes playlists and archives dated July 10, 2026 and current July 2026 events; WPRK exposes a live on-air schedule with electronic and global-music programming.
