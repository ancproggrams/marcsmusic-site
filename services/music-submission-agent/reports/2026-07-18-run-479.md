## FBi Radio verification

FBi Radio publishes `music@fbiradio.com` as its official digital general-servicing mailbox. A submission should contain a clear artist/release subject, short biography, optional press image or gig details, a streaming link and a free high-quality download link for a 320 kbps MP3 or WAV. Links should remain accessible; physical CDs are no longer accepted. The station auditions music on Wednesdays and asks artists seeking the next week's consideration to submit before Tuesday midnight.

The current schedule includes electronic, club, bass, ambient, diaspora and new-release programming. Official programme archives showed *The Playlist* through July 10, 2026, *M5* through July 12, 2026 and *Real to Reel* through July 1, 2026.

## WNAA verification

WNAA 90.1 FM HD publishes `wnaafm@ncat.edu` for music submissions. The policy accepts one original, fully mastered, clean and radio-ready song as WAV or MP3. AIFF, AAC, protected iTunes M4P, streaming links and videos are rejected. The filename must include artist and song title, a digital press kit must be included, and the subject line must read `WNAA Music Submission`.

Activity was verified through the official live-listening page, the current weekly grid with House, Island Reggae, Soul and Jazz programming, and a 2026 community calendar containing events through August 2026. The music-submission page carries a 2026 copyright notice.

## Kennesaw State Owl Radio verification

Kennesaw State University's official Owl Life form states that Owl Radio accepts submissions from anyone with recordings. The visible first step asks for artist name, artist email, a short description and an MP3/WAV file and/or music link. Physical media is an alternative, but submitted CDs, vinyl and cassettes become Owl Radio property and are not returned. `owlradioksu@gmail.com` is published for follow-up, not as a replacement for the form.

The official station homepage exposed a Now Spinning area. Its current weekly schedule included experimental, underground, K-pop, alternative and cross-genre shows, while Kennesaw State's Student Media pages describe Owl Radio as a current 24/7 streaming station.
