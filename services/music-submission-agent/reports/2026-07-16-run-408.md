## 1. KXCI 91.3 FM — Tucson, Arizona, United States / international community radio

### Public first-party routes

Reviewed official surfaces:

- Homepage: `https://kxci.org/`
- Music Department: `https://kxci.org/about/music-department/`
- Current programmes: `https://kxci.org/programs/`

KXCI states that it accepts and broadcasts music from all over the world while prioritizing Tucson, regional and Arizona artists.

The canonical digital process is:

1. open the official Music Department page in a normal browser;
2. use the purpose-specific protected Music Department email action shown there;
3. provide an accessible MP3 or WAV download link;
4. include the published release and artist information;
5. send one carefully selected release rather than a catalogue.

KXCI also permits physical CDs and vinyl. Vinyl must include a digital download. National and international physical submissions have a separately published Music Department postal destination. Digital remains the free-first route; physical production and postage are optional sender costs.

### Published submission requirements

KXCI requests:

- artist or band name;
- album title, artwork and tracklist with song timings;
- record label, or a statement that the release is self-released;
- release date;
- genre, sub-genre and similar artists;
- identification of explicit or questionable language, including the affected track number and a lyrics link when available;
- a brief biography;
- social links;
- upcoming Tucson or regional performances when applicable;
- contact information;
- an MP3 or WAV download link for digital delivery.

KXCI describes this digital route as accepting digital albums. A standalone MarcsMusic single must not be sent until a human confirms that singles are eligible.

### Capacity and editorial boundary

KXCI states that it receives more than 500 physical and digital submissions each week and cannot review, comment on or broadcast every release.

This means:

- delivery does not imply listening or airplay;
- repeated follow-up is inappropriate unless the station publishes a follow-up policy;
- one well-matched release should be selected;
- the queue must remain `needs_manual_review`.
