## Verification and enrichment

- 4ZZZ: the official JavaScript form asks for artist and release details, biography or press-pack context, optional demographic information, content warnings, cover art, social links and a downloadable WAV or 320-kbps MP3 link. Spotify is not accepted, Bandcamp or Apple Music needs a download code, and the form asks for pre-release and broadcast permissions. The separate Album of the Week form is limited to Australian artists and was not used as the primary route.
- 2SER: the official public airplay form requests contact identity, location, release date, artist and release names, biography and social links. The form loads dynamically, so live track-delivery requirements, declarations, international eligibility and anti-spam controls require human inspection.
- Triple R: the official station route is `music@rrr.org.au`. Submissions need a clear subject, no-login stream, non-expiring 320-kbps MP3 or WAV download, clearly named files, short biography and gig details. Large audio attachments are prohibited. Physical CDs, LPs and cassettes are also accepted by the Music Department.
- SYN Media: the official route is `music@syn.org.au`. SYN requests a downloadable 320-kbps MP3 or WAV link, no direct audio attachments, a press release or biography, release title and date and relevant web, social, gig and previous-release links. Music Directors review submissions on a rolling basis.
- 95bFM: the official guidance requires both a stream and a lossless WAV or FLAC download with no account, password or Spotify dependency. The message should contain artist and song names, release date, origin and only brief context. Tracks older than one month are no longer eligible for high-rotation playlisting. The protected email link was not decoded.
