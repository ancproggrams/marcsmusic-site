## Verification notes

- CJSR was verified through its official Submit Music page, which gives a Music Department postal route, accepts digital albums/EPs at music@cjsr.com, allows Bandcamp download codes/cloud links, rejects single tracks and asks for metadata, track lengths and language warnings.
- KBOO was verified through its official Submit Your Music page, which says KBOO lacks digital-library/download capacity, accepts mailed submissions, provides genre-specific attention lines including electronic and world music/reggae, and states submissions become station property.
- KOOP was verified through its official Contact Us page, which lists info@koop.org, a station mailing address and states that the Music Library welcomes music submissions at music@koop.org. The active homepage showed current July/August 2026 station updates/events.
- KDVS was verified through its official contact page, which lists Music Directors, musicdept@kdvs.org and says to send all new music to the mailing address as physical submissions only.
- WREK was verified through its official Music page and linked official submission page, which says music may be submitted by snail mail or emailed to music.director@wrek.org, accepts common media formats and notes CDs/LPs have better airplay odds. The active homepage showed July 2026 music programming and playlist context.
