### Authorized submission route

Airhug Radio publishes a dedicated four-step `Music Submission` form for independent artists. The page states that submissions may be considered for:

- airplay;
- educational features;
- licensing opportunities.

The form defines an independent artist by ownership and control of master recordings and collects label status, artist identity and royalty-related information.

No public business email is labelled for music submissions. The first-party form is therefore the only canonical route stored for delivery.

### Published eligibility requirements

Before submitting, Airhug Radio requires:

- music registration with a Performing Rights Organization;
- SoundExchange registration;
- English-language responses;
- compliance with a strict prohibition on AI use.

The form asks for:

- label status and label name when applicable;
- artist or band name;
- legal first and last name for royalty purposes;
- email address;
- country of origin;
- song titles;
- ISRC codes when applicable;
- PRO membership and work ID;
- SoundExchange registration and Artist ID;
- closest genre;
- BPM;
- uploaded song files and lyrics;
- explicit-language status and clean-version availability;
- a short artist biography;
- interview or artist-spotlight interest;
- promotional use of artist photo and cover art consent;
- rights, ownership and non-interactive broadcast authorization;
- a typed full-name digital signature;
- date and time.

### Audio and document delivery

The official form permits up to ten uploaded files. WAV is preferred, and lyrics must be supplied as PDF.

The page does not unambiguously publish:

- maximum size per upload;

- required sample rate, bit depth or channel format;
- whether all tracks must belong to one release;
- whether a specific clean-version naming convention is required.

These points require manual confirmation before delivery.

### Hard AI restriction

Airhug Radio states that AI use is strictly prohibited in submissions.

This is stored as a hard provenance gate. No MarcsMusic release may be submitted unless a human can truthfully confirm that the exact master, composition, vocals, lyrics, artwork and supplied materials comply with the current policy. A track made with Suno or another generative music system must not be submitted unless Airhug Radio explicitly confirms that the specific use is permitted.

The precise boundary for editing, mastering, assistive tools and partially AI-assisted workflows is not defined on the page, so ambiguity must be resolved before submission rather than interpreted optimistically.

### Rights, consent and signature boundary

The form requires confirmation that the submitter:

- meets the stated eligibility criteria;
- grants permission for non-interactive broadcast;
- retains copyright and ownership;
- has authority to submit and use the music;
- will report changes in ownership or licensing status;
- agrees to the linked broadcast terms.

It separately asks whether Airhug Radio may use the artist photo and cover art for promotion. These consent decisions must be reviewed independently.

The final form stage requires a typed full-name digital signature and date/time. This makes the route ineligible for unattended or automated completion.
