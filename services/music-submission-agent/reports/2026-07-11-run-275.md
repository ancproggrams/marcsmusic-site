## Verification and enrichment

- KTRU: the official contact page states that all music submissions must be physical and accepts CD, LP or 7-inch formats through separate USPS and UPS addresses. Its public form includes a Music Director recipient option, but the form is a question route rather than authorization to replace physical delivery.
- WCBN: the official contact page accepts hard copies from any musician, preferably CD or vinyl, and asks genre-specific releases to be sent care of the relevant director. The Music Directors email is protected/non-plaintext in the retrieved page and was not decoded.
- WNUR: the official contact page publishes `gm@wnur.org` and a general message form. It does not publish a music-submission policy, so the route is limited to a pre-submission inquiry without attachments or track links.
- WNMC: the official contact page tells record companies and anyone contacting a Music Director to use the Station Manager form for forwarding. It lists current directors for Electronic, Reggae, World, Rock, Jazz, Blues and other formats.
- WTBU: the official current-staff page explicitly directs artists interested in sending an album to `wtbu.musicdirectors@gmail.com`. Accepted formats, attachment limits, cloud-link rules and metadata requirements are not published, so the package must be confirmed manually.

Official activity was verified from current station pages. KTRU published station articles dated May 21 and June 3, 2026; WCBN broadcasts 24/7 and exposes current archives and 2025-2026/2026 personnel; WNUR displays a July 8, 2026 schedule; WNMC exposes current playlists, schedules and active genre programming; WTBU published music coverage dated May 20 and May 25, 2026.
