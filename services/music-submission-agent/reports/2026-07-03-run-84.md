## Manual-review reasons

All five routes remain manual-review only. The batch includes third-party curator submission workflows, email/EPK and download-link workflows, redacted or protected public contact routes, a staff/DJ contact form with reCAPTCHA v3, and an official audio-upload form. Manual review is required for login/session handling, pitch copy, route selection, audio file or link selection, metadata, artist bio, local/show context, contact-email handling, clean/explicit labeling, rights/originality assertions, file-format/file-size compliance, station/editorial fit and any possible paid-credit/payment choice.
