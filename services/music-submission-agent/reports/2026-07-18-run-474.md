## CJSW verification

CJSW publishes a first-party music-submission page linking to a public Jotform. The station accepts digital EPs with at least three unique tracks and full-length albums released within the previous three months. Singles are explicitly rejected. The release must be supplied as MP3 files in a ZIP.

The visible form requests email, artist or band name, release name, label or self-released status, EP/full-length classification, release date, genres, artist website or streaming URL, location, biography or release description, RIYL information, the full ZIP release, optional PDF one-sheet and album artwork. It also asks for permission to share the release with programmers for airplay and permanently add it to the digital library. A separate optional local-artist campus-mailout choice can require a signature.

Current activity was verified from the official July 17–23, 2026 programming schedule and live now-playing information. Relevant shows include Road Pops, Electric Company, Fade to Bass, Remote Emissions, Speaking in Tongues, Level The Vibes and Carnival Mix, covering electronic, global bass, reggae, world and Caribbean music.

Sources:

- https://cjsw.com/music/submit/
- https://form.jotform.com/260625704327253
- https://cjsw.com/programming/
- https://cjsw.com/contact/

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because it requires direct audio and artwork uploads, rejects singles, imposes a release-age condition and includes airplay/permanent-library consent. International and AI-music eligibility, upload limits, all music and artwork rights, hidden controls and the final submission require human approval.

## KAOS verification

KAOS publishes `kaosmusicdirector@gmail.com` as its digital music-submission mailbox. Digital submissions must use the subject **Music Submission** and contain streaming and download links rather than attachments. KAOS rejects singles, requests a full release and a one-sheet or press release with focus tracks and FCC or clean-edit information, and states that it never airs profane or indecent material because it has no safe harbor.

Physical CD, vinyl and cassette delivery is also authorized and preferred. The official contact page confirms the Music Department address and expressly tells artists not to use the general station address for music submissions.

Activity was verified through KAOS's current Spinitron page, which displayed programming and recent shows dated July 16, 2026, together with the official schedule, playlist and live-stream links.

Sources:

- https://www.kaosradio.org/submitmusic
- https://www.kaosradio.org/contact
- https://spinitron.com/KAOS/

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because MarcsMusic must select a complete clean release, prepare the one-sheet, validate every external link and all rights, confirm international and AI eligibility and approve the final email or physical shipment.

## Glacer FM verification

Glacer FM publishes a global paid promotional-airplay route for original music in all genres. Cover songs are explicitly excluded. The page directs artists to `administration@glacerfm.com` to select a package and receive a payment invoice.

Published packages include USD 30 for at least 30 days or USD 50 for at least 90 days of one-song regular rotation at three or more spins per day. Genre-block packages are USD 40 or USD 60, while full-album or compilation music-focus packages are USD 30 or USD 45. This is guaranteed paid rotation, not free editorial consideration.

The station's activity was verified through active daily programming blocks, the current Top 50 chart, 24/7 broadcast claims and a 2026 copyright notice.

Sources:

- https://www.glacerfm.com/services/submit-your-music/
- https://www.glacerfm.com/about-us/
- https://www.glacerfm.com/music-charts/

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because package choice, invoice, payment, terms, refund/cancellation conditions, rights, AI eligibility, audience value and return on investment require explicit commercial and legal approval. No email or invoice request may be sent automatically.
