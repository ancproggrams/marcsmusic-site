## 1. 4ZZZ 102.1FM — Brisbane, Australia

### Authorized route

4ZZZ's official Music Submissions page links directly to a station-controlled form at `jeff.4zzz.org.au`. The station says submissions are considered for inclusion in its digital library for access by announcers.

The form requests:

- one contact email address;
- artist name and location;
- release name, date, genre, label or distributor and member information;
- a biography, release information or press-pack link;
- content or language warnings where applicable;
- a downloadable release link in WAV or 320-kbps MP3 format;
- a download code for Bandcamp or Apple Music where required;
- a cover-art link and relevant social links;
- acknowledgement that 4ZZZ may play the release on 102.1FM and Zed Digital.

Spotify-only links are not accepted because the station must be able to download the audio.

Official evidence:

- Website: `https://4zzz.org.au/`
- Submission instructions: `https://4zzz.org.au/music-submissions`
- First-party form: `https://jeff.4zzz.org.au/form/submit-your-music-to-4zzz`

## 2. RTRFM 92.1 — Perth, Australia

### Authorized route

RTRFM's official Submit Your Music page provides a first-party form and explicitly includes `Overseas` in the required location selector. The station asks artists to submit their latest or upcoming release, preferably about two weeks before release.

The form requests:

- first and last name, email, city and state or region;
- artist or band name;
- an optional biography upload in PDF, DOC or DOCX format;
- social-media links and upcoming gigs;
- a music stream link;
- a music download link that must be free to download.

RTRFM prefers a downloadable WAV or high-quality MP3 link delivered through services such as Google Drive, Dropbox, SoundCloud or Bandcamp. The page says not to attach audio files to email.

Official evidence:

- Website: `https://rtrfm.com.au/`
- Submission instructions and form: `https://rtrfm.com.au/submit-your-music/`

### Activity and catalogue fit

RTRFM's official site showed active programming, RTR2 streaming, featured music and stories published on July 14, 2026. Its current programming includes electronic, dance, ambient, experimental, global and other independent styles, making selected MarcsMusic releases potentially relevant.

### Form and contact verification

The first-party form includes an `Overseas` location option, confirming that international submissions are supported. It also includes a field labelled `Comments` with the instruction that it is for validation purposes and must be left unchanged. This is classified as a honeypot-style anti-bot control.

The page names Music Director Matt Perrett and exposes email actions, but the rendered destinations are protected. They were not decoded, inferred, guessed or stored. The queued route is the official form.

No visible CAPTCHA, login or mandatory payment requirement was confirmed in passive review.

### Manual-review controls

- Use the `Overseas` location option and provide only data required for the submission.
- Leave the validation-only `Comments` field unchanged.
- Confirm the chosen release is recent or upcoming and ideally submit about two weeks before release.
- Provide a free downloadable WAV or high-quality MP3 link and verify access permissions.
- Review privacy, rights, biography-upload and optional gig-information implications before entering data.
- Inspect the live form for CAPTCHA or additional validation controls.
- Do not automate the form, populate the honeypot or decode protected email contacts.

Queue status: `needs_manual_review`
