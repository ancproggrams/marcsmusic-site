## Verification and enrichment

- KBOO: accepts physical music routed to named genre contacts covering electronic, rock, hip-hop, jazz, folk, world music and reggae. It states that it has no digital library and cannot download or burn digital submissions. Submitted media becomes station property, is not returned and carries no airplay guarantee.
- KZSU: publicly lists `music@kzsu.stanford.edu` and USPS/UPS delivery addresses. The detailed send-music workflow is protected by temporary-cookie/browser-verification controls and must be reviewed in a normal browser without bypassing the verification boundary.
- KZSC: offers an official form with a Music Submission category, publishes physical delivery addresses and lists genre-specific Music Directors. The form includes a hidden anti-spam phone field, and the director email links are protected; neither boundary was manipulated or decoded.
- KCSB: explicitly invites new releases and demos in all formats, publishes `int.md@kcsb.org` for music inquiries and provides USPS and courier addresses. Complete digital attachment, file-size and metadata rules are not published, so the final delivery method must be confirmed manually.
- KDVS: publicly lists `musicdept@kdvs.org` for the Music Department but explicitly requires all new music to be sent to its mailing address as physical submissions only. The email is not a digital submission workaround, and the authoritative postal destination must be confirmed before mailing.
