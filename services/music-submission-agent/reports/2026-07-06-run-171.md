## Verification notes

- KBOO was verified through the official KBOO site and Submit Your Music page. The route is an emerging-music physical postal submission route with genre-specific attention lines and no digital-library download capacity.
- WXPN was verified through the official WXPN active site and contact page. The page exposes music-submission email actions for general airplay and The Local Show, with EPK, digital download, WAV/high-quality MP3 and bio/show-info guidance.
- SomaFM was verified through the official active SomaFM site, contact page and Submit Music page. The route is digital-only, accepts Bandcamp/YUM or file-transfer options, requires high-bitrate audio, exposes channel/director email routes and warns against emailing multiple directors.
- WWOZ was verified through the official WWOZ active site, Musician Resources path and Getting Airplay guidance. The route prefers finished CDs, accepts high-quality WAV if digital is used, provides a Music Director mailing address and requires clean/profanity marking for airplay suitability.
- KUTX was verified through the official KUTX active site and Submit Your Music page. The route exposes music@kutx.org, requires a specific subject format, streaming link, WAV download link, quick bio/release context, mastered clean tracks and separate specialty-show routing.
