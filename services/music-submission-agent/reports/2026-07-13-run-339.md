## Verification and enrichment

### WPRB 103.3 FM

- Authorized route: the official Music Submissions page invites artists, labels and promoters to submit new music and publishes `music@wprb.com` as the email route.
- Physical route: WPRB accepts CDs, CD-Rs, LPs, 12-inch, 10-inch and 7-inch records at the Music Director postal address and explicitly states that physical submissions are prioritized.
- Genre fit: WPRB operates a broad freeform schedule with electronic, experimental, ambient, reggae, world-music and hip-hop relevance for selected MarcsMusic releases.
- Activity: the official site displayed a live now-playing track, current daily schedule, active playlist links and a 2026 copyright footer during verification on 2026-07-13.
- Business-email validation: `music@wprb.com` is a syntactically valid first-party WPRB-domain address published specifically for music submissions.
- Cost and access: no submission fee, account, login, CAPTCHA or mandatory payment is stated for the email or postal routes.
- Manual boundary: a human must choose the track and delivery method, verify rights, release status, broadcast suitability, metadata and clean-version needs, approve all files, media and personal data, reconfirm the instructions and perform the submission manually.

### WCBN-FM

- Authorized route: the official contact page states that WCBN accepts music from any musicians willing to send a hard copy of their album to the station music department.
- Physical requirements: CD or vinyl is preferred. Genre-specific packages may be addressed care of the relevant director; otherwise they may be addressed to WCBN MUSIC at the published station mailing address.
- Review statement: WCBN says it listens to everything sent and gives every submission a chance with its DJs.
- Genre fit: WCBN is a student-run freeform station with broad electronic, experimental, techno, reggae, world-music, hip-hop and jazz relevance.
- Activity: the official homepage states that WCBN broadcasts 24/7, provides working live-stream and archive routes, and the current contact page lists staff and board terms extending through 2026.
- Protected business contact: the Music Directors email is displayed through Cloudflare email protection. It was not decoded, guessed, stored or used as a submission route.
- Cost and access: no submission fee, account, login, CAPTCHA or mandatory payment is stated, although the sender bears physical production and postage costs.
- Manual boundary: a human must decide whether physical production and shipping are justified, select the release and optional genre-director routing, verify rights, metadata, clean-version needs and packaging, and arrange shipment manually.
