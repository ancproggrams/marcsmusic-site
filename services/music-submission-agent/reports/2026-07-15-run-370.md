## 1. Fresh On The Net — United Kingdom / global

### Authorized route

Fresh On The Net's official Send Us A Track page states that any artist may submit one track each week. Its inbox opens Monday morning and closes after 200 submissions or on Wednesday evening, whichever occurs first.

The first-party form requests:

- artist name;
- track name;
- a SoundCloud track URL;
- acceptance of the published terms and conditions.

The SoundCloud track must be:

- public;
- available in full;
- available worldwide;
- embeddable on the Fresh On The Net site.

Private tracks, SoundCloud Go tracks and territory-restricted tracks are not accepted.

Official evidence:

- Website and current activity: `https://freshonthenet.co.uk/`
- Submission form and current terms: `https://freshonthenet.co.uk/submit/`
- Submission help: `https://freshonthenet.co.uk/submithelp/`
- Operating model: `https://freshonthenet.co.uk/about-us/`
- Contact-routing restrictions: `https://freshonthenet.co.uk/contact/`

### Eligibility and policy controls

The current terms specify:

- one track per artist per week;
- no resubmission of a track entered in a previous week;
- no remixes or alternate versions of previously submitted tracks;
- a three-month wait after appearing in Fresh Faves before submitting again;
- correct artist and track labelling;
- social and biography links should be present in the artist's SoundCloud profile;
- AI-generated tracks are not accepted;
- artists selected for the Listening Post must not mobilise friends, fans or social followers to influence voting.

The page stated `DROPBOX OPEN` when reviewed on July 15, 2026. Because the route is schedule- and capacity-dependent, its state must be rechecked immediately before any submission.

The site carried current posts through July 14, 2026 and displayed Batch 615 activity.

The platform describes its team as having wide-ranging tastes and focuses on helping independent musicians reach new listeners. Selected MarcsMusic electronic, electronica, experimental, ambient, dub, reggae, bass, hip-hop and global-fusion tracks therefore have a plausible discovery fit without implying selection, review or airplay.
