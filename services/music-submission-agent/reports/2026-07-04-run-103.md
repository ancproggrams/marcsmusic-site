## Verification notes

- WMFO was verified from its official site, which shows a 2026 footer, round-the-clock freeform broadcasting, a 2025-2026 board page with a Music Director route and a Music Department that receives CDs/vinyl/cassettes and adds digital media to Rivendell.
- Dandelion Radio was verified from its official contact and current monthly-program pages. It invites demos by link/contact routing, lists recently active DJs, shows PRS/PPL licensing and publishes current monthly shows with electronic, dub, jungle, reggae, world and experimental fit.
- Radio Free Brooklyn was verified from its official site and public music submission form. The route requires freely downloadable music links and includes validation/reCAPTCHA, so it was queued only for manual handling.
- Obscure Sound was verified from its active official site and Contact/Submissions page. It supports SubmitHub, SubmitHub Marketplace, MusoSoup and direct email, with paid/premium side-routes left untouched.
- The Line of Best Fit was verified from its official contact page, which welcomes music from new and unsigned artists through a linked form and lists editorial email routes.
