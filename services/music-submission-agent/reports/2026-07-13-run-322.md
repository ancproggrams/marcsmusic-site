## Verification and enrichment

### OverClocked ReMix

- Authorized route: the official Submit navigation and Submission Standards direct artists to the first-party community Submissions form.
- Platform scope: OverClocked ReMix is a global non-commercial community dedicated to curated arrangements of video-game music, rather than a general original-music upload platform.
- Current activity: the official homepage lists ReMixes through 30 June 2026 and a new album announcement dated 25 June 2026.
- Source eligibility: the arranged music must have actually appeared in a video, computer or arcade game and must have been composed for, first published as or recognized as the game's soundtrack. Licensed popular songs and most non-game source material are not eligible.
- Arrangement threshold: the source must remain identifiable and dominant, while the arrangement must be substantial and original. Merely adding loops, replacing MIDI instruments or making minimal production changes is insufficient.
- File requirements: final WAV audio, at least 16-bit/44.1 kHz stereo, normally 2-7 minutes, with an original title. One submission is allowed at a time and artists must wait two weeks between submissions.
- Production requirements: clean mixing/mastering, no unintentional distortion or noise, and significant attention to sound design, performance and spatial balance.
- Ownership and collaborators: the submitter must have participated in the arrangement, list collaborators and confirm their agreement to the submission terms.
- AI restriction: tracks created wholly or partly with prompt-based generative AI are prohibited; the standards explicitly name Suno and Udio.
- Rights and license boundary: submitting grants OverClocked ReMix a non-exclusive, sublicensable, perpetual and universal license to use, distribute, perform or incorporate the material. The artist retains ownership, but removal generally requires mutual agreement and third-party copyright risk remains with the submitter.
- Account boundary: the public form is the primary route. If it cannot be accessed, the official instructions require forum registration; no account or session was used.
- Business-email verification: `admin@ocremix.org` is officially published for questions and suggestions only. It is not an authorized substitute for the submission form.
- Pricing: no submission fee or mandatory payment is stated. Donations, Patreon and merchandise are separate optional support routes.
- MarcsMusic fit: suitable only for a specifically prepared, fully human-created and rights-reviewed video-game arrangement. It is not suitable for a general original single or a track involving prompt-generated audio.
