## Verification and enrichment

### Liquicity Records

- Authorized route: Liquicity's official demo page says the linked Label Engine form must be used for all music submissions and that every submitted track is listened to, although a response is not guaranteed.
- Delivery fields: the external form requests artist name, real name, email, up to five titled MP3 files, optional social profiles and a short message.
- Form boundary: unresolved `{{demo.NAME}}` template variables appeared during passive review. Current binding, file limits, rights text, privacy consent and runtime anti-spam controls must be confirmed manually.
- Cost and access: no login, account, submission fee or mandatory payment is stated on the official page or passively rendered form.
- Label and genre fit: Liquicity's active catalogue is strongly aligned with liquid, melodic and dancefloor drum-and-bass.
- Email classification: no public demo mailbox was published; the form is the authorized route.
- Manual boundary: a human must confirm that the form is operational and correctly bound to Liquicity, select authorized final MP3s, approve personal data and terms, and submit manually.

### Circus Records / Circus Electric

- Authorized route: Circus explicitly says it accepts demos through LabelRadar only and links the Circus Records/Circus Electric destination from its official about page.
- Portal boundary: the Circus-specific LabelRadar page rendered only a dynamic loading shell. Account, login, session, campaign, submission-credit, pricing, consent and rights conditions require human review.
- Label and genre fit: Circus Records focuses on memorable bass-driven dance-floor music; Circus Electric focuses on futuristic, emotional and boundary-pushing bass.
- Activity: the official catalogue currently lists Circus Records and Circus Electric singles and EPs across many release pages.
- Business-email classification: `info@circus-records.co.uk` is publicly posted for questions and requests, has valid syntax and matches the first-party domain, but it is not a demo mailbox and was not queued for music delivery.
- Cost: Circus states no mandatory demo fee. Optional LabelRadar credits or paid functionality may exist and require human approval.
- Manual boundary: a human must authenticate, select the correct imprint, verify that the campaign is open, inspect current limits and terms, approve rights and metadata, and submit through LabelRadar.

### Monstercat Uncaged / Instinct / Silk

- Authorized routes: Monstercat's official contact and brand pages link separate LabelRadar demo destinations for Uncaged, Instinct and Silk and state that submitted demos are listened to.
- Eligibility: Monstercat asks artists to submit only their best original work.
- Imprint fit: Uncaged covers hard-hitting dubstep, drum-and-bass, trap and electro; Instinct focuses on contemporary melodic dance music; Silk covers progressive and deep house, chillout, trance and ambient.
- Activity: Monstercat's official homepage listed releases dated 3 July, 10 July and 13 July 2026.
- Portal boundary: both the main Monstercat portal and the separate Silk portal rendered only dynamic loading shells. Account, login, session, campaign, credit, pricing, consent and rights conditions must be inspected manually.
- Contact classification: support, press, event and general contact routes were not repurposed for demos; protected addresses were not decoded or stored.
- Manual boundary: a human must select the correct imprint, authenticate, review current campaign and pricing conditions, verify originality and ownership, approve metadata and rights, and submit manually.
