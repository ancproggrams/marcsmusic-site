### SomaFM

- Authorized route: SomaFM's official Submit Music page publishes currently accepting, channel-specific music-submission email routes.
- Artist eligibility: commercial, independent, emerging and unsigned artists are supported, but AI-generated music is explicitly excluded.
- Delivery: preferred delivery is a Bandcamp link with a YUM download code. The alternative is an approved WeTransfer or Dropbox shared link.
- File rules: 256 kbps or V0 highest-quality VBR, 44.1 kHz MP3 with correct artist, track, release and genre metadata. WAV, AIFF, FLAC, 128 kbps MP3, Haulix, Facebook, SoundCloud, YouTube and Mixcloud deliveries are excluded.
- Channel routing: `promosubmissions@somafm.com` serves multiple currently accepting electronic and specialist channels. `dion@somafm.com` serves 7-inch Soul and Heavyweight Reggae, `shawn@somafm.com` serves Beat Blender, and other channel-specific recipients are listed on the same official page.
- Duplicate-contact restriction: SomaFM says the same release must not be sent to multiple music directors or it will be ignored.
- Availability boundary: only the channels listed as currently accepting should be used; Covers is on hiatus and other unlisted channels are backlogged or unavailable.
- Pricing: no submission fee is stated. Optional external delivery tools may have their own account or plan terms.
- Activity: SomaFM's homepage described more than 30 hand-curated, listener-supported channels broadcasting worldwide, including multiple electronic, ambient, world and reggae-adjacent formats relevant to MarcsMusic.
- Business-email verification: submission addresses were confirmed directly on the first-party Submit Music page. Syntax and stated purpose were validated. No SMTP, MX, catch-all or mailbox-level probe was performed.
