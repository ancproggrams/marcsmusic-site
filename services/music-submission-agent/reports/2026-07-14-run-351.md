### DI.FM

- Deduplication action: DI.FM already existed as a generic Run 13 discovery record, so no second platform seed was created. The canonical record was updated in place.
- Authorized route: DI.FM's Help & Support page explicitly redirects new-music and channel requests to its official Submissions page.
- Submission process: artists send a brief and specific email request to a mailing list reviewed by multiple Channel Directors. Media links are allowed, but attachments are prohibited and attachment-bearing messages are automatically ignored.
- Required information: the request must state a genre corresponding to a DI.FM channel and describe the intended submission, including track names or mix track listings.

- Window distinction: waiting lists for regular shows are currently closed for Progressive, Techno, Tech House and Trance. The same page continues to accept new-music and demo requests, so those closures were recorded without incorrectly closing the music route.
- Corrected pricing: DI.FM listener subscriptions and the 30-day listening trial are separate from the new-music submission process. No music-submission fee or mandatory payment is stated.
- Activity and fit: the official service currently lists active Trance, House, Techno, Chillstep, Ambient, Drum and Bass, Dubstep and other electronic channels and carries a 2026 copyright notice.
- Manual boundary: a human must select the closest channel, distinguish a track/demo pitch from a regular-show proposal, prepare the concise rights-cleared request and media links, and send manually through the protected official link.
