## Verification and enrichment

### CJSR

- Authorized digital route: `music@cjsr.com`, published by CJSR specifically for albums and EPs.
- Authorized physical route: the Music Department postal address published on the same official submission page.
- Eligibility: albums and EPs are accepted; individual single-track submissions are not.
- Delivery: use downloadable files. Bandcamp download codes, Dropbox and Google Drive are accepted; paid-streaming-only access is unsuitable and expiring transfer links are discouraged.
- Required information: artist or band origin, focus tracks, track lengths, language warnings and correctly tagged MP3 metadata.
- Business-email verification: `music@cjsr.com` is plaintext, purpose-specific, first-party and domain-matched. No mailbox-level probe was performed.
- Relevant programming: the active schedule includes underground electronic, global dancefloor, trance, drum-and-bass and jungle shows.
- Pricing boundary: no music-submission fee is stated. A separate optional local advertising package is not part of the submission route and was excluded.
- Activity: the official homepage and schedule were active in July 2026 with current live programming.

### CFUV

- Authorized route: `music@cfuv.ca`, published by CFUV for all digital music submissions.
- Minimum release size: at least three unique tracks. The station also describes a conditional radio-only EP option assembled from qualifying prior singles.
- File format: downloadable MP3 files at 320 kbps or better.
- Required information: a one-page biography, artist or band name, album title and a proper track listing. Releases lacking a usable track listing are not considered.
- Delivery options: the official page lists services including !earshotDistro, Dropbox, Google Drive, WeTransfer, Box and Disco.
- Physical boundary: physical submissions are limited to artists from Victoria, Sooke, Duncan or the Gulf Islands; MarcsMusic is queued only for the digital route.
- Business-email verification: `music@cfuv.ca` is plaintext, purpose-specific, first-party and domain-matched. No mailbox-level probe was performed.
- Relevant programming: current shows cover electronica, ambient, synthwave, experimental music, dub, reggae, house, hip-hop and global music.
- Pricing: no music-submission fee is stated.

### KXCI

- Authorized digital route: the official Music Department page provides a protected first-party email link for MP3 or WAV album-download submissions.
- Contact protection: the Music Department address was not decoded, guessed or stored as plaintext.
- Authorized physical route: national and international artists may mail CDs or vinyl to the KXCI Music Department address published on the official page.
- Required information: artist name, album title, artwork, timed track list, label or self-release status, release date, genre, comparable artists, explicit-language details, biography, social links and contact information.
- Review process: KXCI states that submissions are reviewed for genre, quality and explicit language or content.
- Geographic scope: KXCI accepts music from all over the world while prioritizing Tucson, regional and Arizona artists.
- Relevant programming and activity: the official site displayed a live playlist, a two-week archive, current July 2026 posts and the active electronic-oriented program Electric Feel. It also featured Thievery Corporation in May 2026.
- Pricing: no music-submission fee is stated; physical-media and postage costs remain the sender's responsibility.
