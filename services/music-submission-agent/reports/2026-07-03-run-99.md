## Verification and enrichment notes

- Spotify for Artists was verified through its official playlisting page and playlist-pitching help article. It is the official route for pitching upcoming unreleased songs to editors, requires Spotify for Artists access, asks for at least 7 days lead time and permits one active pitch at a time.
- MusoSoup was verified through its official artist-facing site. It provides a Submit music route, says artists can upload a single, EP or album for free using a link or MP3, reviews submissions before campaign activation, shows campaign-fee and free-offer mechanics, and displayed July 2026 blog activity.
- DailyPlaylists was verified through its official homepage and pricing page. It exposes Submit a Song, says artists can submit music for free, lists 18,000+ playlists, 75M+ submissions, 500k+ submitting artists, a Free tier with 10 weekly standard submissions, paid standard add-ons and Premium Credits.
- KBOO was verified through its official Submit Your Music page. It states that KBOO does not yet have a digital library, cannot download and burn digital submissions, and provides a physical mailing workflow with genre attention lines including Electronic, World Music and Reggae.
- WRIR was verified through its official contact page and live site schedule. The contact page was updated in 2026, provides a Music Department submission route and mailing address, and the current schedule shows relevant world, house, hip-hop, electronic and DJ-oriented programming.
