## Verification and enrichment

### 4ZZZ Brisbane

- Authorized route: 4ZZZ's official Music Submissions page directs artists to the first-party Music Department form hosted on `jeff.4zzz.org.au`.
- Access boundary: the form states that JavaScript must be enabled. No login, account, fee or mandatory payment is stated. No CAPTCHA was confirmed in passive inspection, but any runtime control must be reviewed manually.
- Required material: contact email, artist name and location, release name and date, genres, label or distributor, credits, biography or release information, content warning if applicable, a downloadable audio link, cover art and social links.
- Audio rules: the station requests WAV or 320 kbps MP3. Downloadable SoundCloud, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive and similar routes are supported; Spotify is not accepted because the station cannot download it. Bandcamp or Apple Music delivery requires a download code.
- Rights and consent: the form asks whether 4ZZZ may play the release before its release date and requires a final acknowledgement giving 4ZZZ permission to play the release on 102.1FM and Zed Digital.
- Sensitive-data boundary: optional fields ask whether participants identify with specified demographic groups. These fields must never be populated automatically or without the user's explicit choice.
- Adjacent form excluded: the separate Album of the Week route is limited to Australian artists and requires existing support across multiple 4ZZZ programs, so it was not queued for MarcsMusic.
- Email classification: `reception@4zzz.org.au` is published for general station contact, not music submissions, and was not used as a substitute route.
- Genre fit: the station's programming and charts include independent electronic, dance, hip-hop, experimental, reggae, dub and international music, making selected MarcsMusic releases potentially relevant.
- Activity: the official homepage displayed live programming dated 13 July 2026, a 13 July 2026 Album of the Week, current charts and scheduled events through October 2026.
- Manual boundary: a human must select the release, verify ownership and broadcast suitability, decide on pre-release permission, approve personal and optional identity information, prepare compliant audio and artwork links, inspect runtime controls, accept the final broadcast acknowledgement and submit the form manually.

### fbi.radio

- Authorized route: the official contact and Music Submissions pages both publish `music@fbiradio.com` specifically for music submissions.
- Business-email verification: syntax, official publication and purpose were verified. The legacy `fbiradio.com` web domain redirects to the current official `fbi.radio` website, supporting the domain relationship. No mailbox-level probe was performed.
- Required material: a clear subject containing artist and track, album or EP name; a short biography; press image or upcoming gig information; a public or private stream; and a free high-quality 320 kbps MP3 or WAV download.
- Link rules: SoundCloud, Bandcamp and Spotify may be used for streaming, but a separate high-quality download must also be supplied. Download links should not expire; SoundCloud downloads must be enabled.
- Timing: general music auditioning occurs on Wednesdays. The station advises sending before midnight Tuesday for consideration in the following week's playlist process.
- Digital-only route: CD submissions are no longer accepted.
- Response expectation: the station says music sent to the Music Director is normally heard within two to three weeks, but individual feedback is not guaranteed.
- Adjacent contacts excluded: `musicinterviews@fbiradio.com`, `gigs@fbiradio.com`, partnerships and general-enquiry mailboxes have separate published purposes and were not used as submission substitutes. Individual presenter servicing was not harvested or queued.
- Optional paid promotion: fbi.radio separately offers paid promotional campaigns, but these are not required for editorial music servicing and were not queued or treated as a route to influence airplay.
- Genre fit: current programs and editorial coverage include electronic, bass-oriented, experimental, ambient and global sounds, while the station prioritizes emerging Sydney and Australian music. A human should therefore assess international editorial fit before sending.
- Activity: official episodes were dated 12 and 9 July 2026, and editorial posts were dated 10 and 3 July 2026.
- Manual boundary: a human must choose the release, verify ownership and release status, assess geographic and editorial fit, approve the subject, pitch, image, stream, download and personal information, reconfirm the address and send the email manually.
