## 2. WCBN-FM 88.3 — Ann Arbor, United States

### Authorized route

WCBN's official Contact page states that it accepts music from any musician willing to send a hard copy of an album, preferably in CD or vinyl form. It says all received music is listened to and given a chance with station DJs. Genre-specific releases may be marked care of the appropriate genre director; otherwise packages should be addressed to WCBN MUSIC at the official station address.

Official evidence:

- Website: `https://wcbn.org/`
- Contact, staff and submission instructions: `https://wcbn.org/c/`
- Schedule: `https://wcbn.org/schedule/`

### Activity and catalogue fit

WCBN states that it broadcasts 24/7 and provides live listening, recent two-week archives and a current schedule. Its Contact page lists current management, general and genre Music Directors and board terms extending through October and December 2026. Its freeform and specialist programming includes electronic, techno, reggae, dancehall, hip-hop, world and experimental music, making selected MarcsMusic albums potentially relevant.

### Contact verification

The first-party Contact page identifies current general and genre Music Directors and exposes a Music Directors email action. That destination is protected by Cloudflare email protection. It was not decoded, inferred, guessed or stored. Because WCBN's explicit submission instruction is for hard-copy albums, the protected contact is classified as an observed business-contact route rather than an authorized digital music-submission route.

### Manual-review controls

- Confirm that the selected release is an album rather than a single.
- Choose an eligible physical format, preferably CD or vinyl.
- Decide whether general WCBN MUSIC routing or a specific genre director is appropriate.
- Reconfirm the current mailing address and prepare international postage and customs documentation manually.
- Do not decode the protected email or use it to bypass the physical-submission instruction.
- Do not automatically send or ship anything.

Queue status: `needs_manual_review`
