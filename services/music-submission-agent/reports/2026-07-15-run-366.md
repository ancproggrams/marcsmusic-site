## 1. CFUV 101.9 FM — Victoria, British Columbia, Canada

### Authorized route

CFUV's official Music Submissions page directs all digital submissions to:

- `music@cfuv.ca`

An eligible submission must:

- contain at least three unique tracks;
- use MP3 files at a minimum of 320 kbps;
- be delivered through a downloadable link;
- include the artist or band name, album title and a proper track listing.

The station lists !earshot Distro, Dropbox, Google Drive, WeTransfer, Box and DISCO as acceptable examples. A one-page biography is helpful but a glossy press kit is unnecessary.

CFUV no longer accepts physical music submissions unless the artist is local to Victoria, Sooke, Duncan or the Gulf Islands. MarcsMusic should therefore use only the digital route.

Official evidence:

- Website: `https://cfuv.uvic.ca/`
- Submission instructions: `https://cfuv.uvic.ca/music-submissions/`
- Contact page: `https://cfuv.uvic.ca/contact/`
- Current shows: `https://cfuv.uvic.ca/shows/`

### Activity and catalogue fit

CFUV's official site exposes a live stream, a current weekly schedule and active show descriptions. Programming includes electronica, hip-hop, reggae, dub, breakbeat, ambient, experimental, world music, amapiano, gqom, house and synthwave, giving selected MarcsMusic releases a plausible format fit without implying acceptance or airplay.

### Business-email verification

The role mailbox `music@cfuv.ca` is published both on the official Music Submissions page and on the first-party staff-contact page for Music Director Troy Lemberg.

Verification covered:

- repeated first-party publication;

- explicit Music Director and submission purpose.

No SMTP probe, MX probe, catch-all test, mailbox-level check or deliverability test was performed.

## 2. CHSR-FM 97.9 — Fredericton, New Brunswick, Canada

### Authorized routes

CHSR states that it is always looking for new music of all genres and accepts submissions at any time.

Digital submissions must:

- use MP3 at a minimum of 320 kbps;
- be delivered through a download link such as WeTransfer, Dropbox or Google Drive, or as email attachments;
- use the Music Director route displayed on the official page in human-readable obfuscated form.

Physical submissions are also authorized:

- playable CDs rather than data discs;
- LPs;
- cassettes.

Physical packages should include a clear track list, artist information, genre, location and contact details. The page discusses longer delivery times for packages sent from outside Canada, confirming international physical eligibility.

Official evidence:

- Website: `https://chsrfm.ca/`
- Submission instructions: `https://chsrfm.ca/blog/about/get-your-music-on-chsr`
- Contact page: `https://chsrfm.ca/blog/about/contact`

### Activity and catalogue fit

The submission guideline is marked last updated in March 2023, but it remains linked in the current site navigation. CHSR's official site published shows, playlists, archives and posts through July 2026, confirming current station operation. Current programming includes electronic, remix, chiptune, experimental, global and hip-hop material.

### Business-contact verification

The same Music Director role route is published on both the official submission page and first-party contact page in this form:

- `MusicDirector [at] chsrfm [dot] ca`

Verification covered:

- repeated first-party publication;

- explicit Music Director and submission purpose.

The route was left in its published obfuscated form. It was not machine-decoded or stored as a plaintext email address. No SMTP, MX, catch-all, mailbox-level or deliverability probing was performed.

The submission page also contains an adjacent form framed around sessions and interviews. It was not treated as the canonical music-submission route. Separate sessions, PSA and general-contact destinations were excluded.
