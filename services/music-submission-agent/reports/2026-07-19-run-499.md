## 1. 7clouds Music Group

Official route: `https://7clouds.org/`.

The first-party form requests:

- full name and email;
- artist and track name;
- a stream link;
- released or unreleased status;
- label-signed, independent or other applicant status;
- one desired national or genre channel;
- optional additional context;
- agreement to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Relevant MarcsMusic channels include Drum N Bass, Dubstep, Afro House, Afrobeats, Amapiano, Dance, Future House, Tech House, Techno, Trance, Trap and Tropical.

The Terms require the submitter to own or control all rights. A submission does not guarantee a feature; selected tracks may be featured on 7clouds channels with credit. The Privacy Policy states that demo information may be used for review and possible collaboration and may be retained for up to two years unless earlier deletion is requested.

`contact@7clouds.org` is first-party published, syntactically valid and domain-aligned, but it is a general/privacy contact rather than an authorized replacement for the demo form.

Activity is current: the official site is marked © 2026, describes itself as worldwide, reports 45 million YouTube subscribers in 2026 and more than 7,000 curated releases, and states that it searches continuously for new talent.

## 2. DNBB Music Group

Official route: `https://dnbbrecords.com/`, linking to `https://www.labelradar.com/labels/dnbb/portal`.

DNBB describes a global Drum & Bass and Bass Music ecosystem based in São Paulo, with 12 active labels, more than 1,400 releases and listeners in 180 countries. Relevant genres include:

- drum-and-bass and jungle;
- dubstep and breakbeat;
- UK garage and bass house;
- house, tech house and progressive house;
- Afro House, techno, melodic techno and trance.

The published submission guidelines request:

- WAV 24-bit or MP3 320 kbps;
- BPM, key and title in the filename;
- a short biography and social links;
- a private SoundCloud or Dropbox link.

DNBB reports more than 120 demos in the current month, eight signings in 2026 and a 14-day average response.

The portal is hosted by LabelRadar. A direct label submission requires five credits. LabelRadar's free Lite plan provides five credits monthly, while additional credits and PRO are optional paid products. Account access, credit balance, upload and the final send action remain manual.

`dnbbrecords@dnbb.com.br` and `suporte@dnbb.com.br` are first-party published, syntactically valid and domain-aligned, but neither is published as an alternative to the official LabelRadar route.

## 3. Reggae Global Radio

Official route: `https://reggaeglobalradio.com/music-submission` using `music@reggaeglobalradio.com`.

The official requirements are:

- state whether the music is released or pre-release;
- include a biography for new, upcoming or inspiring artists;
- provide MP3 files only; WAV files are rejected;
- name each file `Artist Name - Title of Song`;
- provide two correctly labeled Reggae Global Radio station-ID jingles;
- send all required material in one email, not multiple messages.

The mailbox is first-party published, syntactically valid, domain-aligned and explicitly authorized for music submissions.

The station's site is marked © 2026, publishes active daily and weekly programmes and positions Reggae Global Radio as a worldwide reggae platform.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact channel, label, programme and genre fit;
- released or unreleased status;
- stream links, audio files, metadata and biographies;
- master, composition, sample and contributor rights;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- generative-AI eligibility or disclosure;
- privacy, retention and personal-data handling;
- hidden verification and platform terms;
- final form, portal or email action.

7clouds additionally requires acceptance of Terms and Privacy and documents up to two years of demo retention. DNBB additionally requires LabelRadar login and credits. Reggae Global Radio additionally requires MP3-only delivery, one-email packaging and two original or properly licensed station-ID jingles.
