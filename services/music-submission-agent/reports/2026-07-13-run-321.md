## Verification and enrichment

### 4ZZZ

- Authorized route: the official first-party Music Department submission form hosted on the station's `jeff.4zzz.org.au` subdomain.
- Eligibility: the general form asks for artist location and states no Australian-only restriction. The adjacent Album of the Week route is explicitly Australian-only and was excluded.
- Form boundary: JavaScript is required. Passive review exposed the main fields but did not establish every runtime validation or anti-spam control.
- Required release data: contact email, artist name and location, release title/date/genres, label or distributor, member names and pronouns, biography or press-pack information and content warnings.
- Optional privacy-sensitive data: demographic identity fields are offered and may be left unanswered; they require human privacy review.
- Audio delivery: a downloadable WAV or 320 kbps MP3 link. SoundCloud must permit downloads; Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Bandcamp or Apple Music download codes are supported. Spotify-only access is not accepted.
- Asset and permission boundary: artwork and social links are requested, along with pre-release airplay permission where relevant and an acknowledgement allowing airplay on 102.1FM and Zed Digital.
- Business-email verification: `music-department@4zzz.org.au` is published by the station only for following up an existing submission. It was not classified as an alternative initial submission route.
- Relevant programming: active schedules include alternative, experimental, electronic, hip-hop, reggae, dub and world-oriented shows.
- Activity: official pages displayed live/on-demand programming dated 12 July 2026, a July 2026 Album of the Week and current July/August events.
- Pricing: no submission fee is stated.

### 2SER

- Authorized routes: the official first-party Submit Your Music form and `submitmusic@2ser.com`, published specifically for digital airplay consideration to the Music Director.
- Form fields visible in passive review: first name, last name, email, required music-submission input, location, release date, artist or band name, release title, artist bio and social links.
- Dynamic boundary: the page reports `Loading form...`; complete asset restrictions, consent text and runtime anti-spam controls were not fully exposed passively.
- Route-selection boundary: a human must choose the form or Music Director email and avoid sending the same release through both paths.
- Physical restriction: the official contact page says not to send physical copies unless requested.
- Business-email verification: `submitmusic@2ser.com` is plaintext, purpose-specific and first-party. `info@2ser.com` and `feedback@2ser.com` are valid published business contacts but were excluded from the music queue.
- Geographic fit: no international exclusion is stated, but 2SER's music coverage strongly features Australian and Sydney scenes, so fit remains a human decision.
- Activity: the site identifies 2SER as a 24-hour broadcaster and displays active 2026 programming and music content.
- Pricing: no submission fee is stated.

### Triple R

- Authorized route: `music@rrr.org.au`, published on the official Submit Music page and again on the staff/contact page as the shared Music Content Producer inbox.
- Optional presenter route: artists may directly service a suitable program using the public email or social contact listed on that program's official page.
- Required email content: a clear subject line, login-free stream, non-expiring download, correctly named files, a short one-to-two-paragraph bio and relevant gig or launch details.
- Audio delivery: 320 kbps MP3 or WAV through Dropbox, Google Drive, non-expiring WeTransfer or a comparable authorized host.
- Attachment restriction: large audio files must not be attached to email. A PDF press release is allowed, but key information should remain in the email body.
- Contact minimization: the staff page also publishes individual Music Content Producer addresses. They were not queued because the shared purpose-specific inbox is sufficient.
- Physical option: CDs, LPs or cassettes may be posted to the Music Department; production and postage remain sender costs.
- Relevant programming: current programs include rave/electronic, techno, footwork, jungle, ambient, experimental, dub, reggae, hip-hop and global music.
- Activity: the official site displayed episodes dated 12-13 July 2026, videos dated 8 July 2026 and current events and weekly music selections.
- Pricing: no editorial submission fee is stated.
