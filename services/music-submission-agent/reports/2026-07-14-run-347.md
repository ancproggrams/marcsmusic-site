- Physical route: CKUW also accepts cassette, CD, vinyl and reel-to-reel submissions at its published station address.
- Airplay process: CKUW states that volunteers must play 30% new music and that everything received is available for on-air play, while making no airplay guarantee.

- Activity: the homepage displayed live programming and station news dated 25 May through 9 June 2026.

- Manual boundary: a human must confirm the current rules, prepare the permitted high-quality download link and concise metadata, verify rights and content labels, select a suitable release and send manually. Physical delivery also requires manual handling.

### 101.5 UMFM / CJUM Winnipeg

- Authorized digital route: UMFM's official Music Department FAQ accepts submissions through secure downloadable links delivered using WeTransfer, Dropbox, Google Drive, Bandcamp, DISCO or embedded links.
- Attachment restriction: UMFM explicitly rejects individual audio-file attachments sent by email.
- Physical route: artists may mail or drop off a CD addressed to the Music Director at the published University of Manitoba station address.
- Release eligibility: physical singles are rejected; physical EPs must contain at least four songs. The FAQ is framed around CDs, albums and EPs and does not explicitly authorize standalone digital singles.
- Protected contact boundary: the authorized Music Department email is exposed through a Cloudflare-protected link. The destination address was not decoded, guessed or stored. A human must open the official page and use the authorized link.
- Airplay process: UMFM states that volunteer programmers decide what is played and that delivery does not guarantee airplay.
- Genre fit: the active program directory includes electronic programming, including breakbeat, UK garage and bassy house, as well as hip-hop, world and broad multi-genre shows.
- Activity: UMFM's homepage displayed news dated 6 July 2026, active charts, current on-air programming and scheduled events extending into November 2026.
- Submission-rule gap: the FAQ does not publish complete codec, bitrate, metadata, rights, release-window, AI-origin or response requirements.
- Manual boundary: a human must open the authorized email link, confirm the destination and current rules, verify release eligibility and rights, prepare the permitted download link and metadata, and send manually.
