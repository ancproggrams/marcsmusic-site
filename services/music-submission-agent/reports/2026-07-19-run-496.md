## 1. Protocol Zero

Official route: `demo@protocolzero.online`, published at `https://protocolzero.online/`.

Protocol Zero is an independent European drum-and-bass label founded between Slovakia and Italy. It focuses on darker and technical drum and bass, including deep neurofunk and atmospheric techstep.

The first-party site explicitly asks artists to send a **private SoundCloud link** to `demo@protocolzero.online`. The mailbox is first-party published, syntactically valid, domain-aligned and explicitly intended for demos. No country restriction is published, although universal international eligibility is not expressly guaranteed.

Activity is current: the official site presents **Ominate — Command** as its newest release, and Beatport lists the release date as **July 11, 2026**.

## 2. Quantum Circle Records

Official route: `https://qcrecords.com/demo`.

Quantum Circle Records is a Swiss independent underground-electronic label. Its first-party demo page requires:

- a private SoundCloud link with downloads enabled;
- no more than three tracks;
- unreleased and unshared material;
- WAV at 44.1 kHz and 16-bit minimum;
- no attachments;
- no AI-generated music.

The visible form requests Artist Name, Artist e-mail, SoundCloud private link and a short message. It also contains an image-based **Verification Code**, which was not attempted or bypassed.

`demo@qcrecords.com` is first-party published, syntactically valid and domain-aligned, but the page limits this email alternative to artists already in contact with the label. The public form remains canonical.

Activity is current: Beatport lists **Ashmacro — Hybridised** under Quantum Circle Records on **May 15, 2026** and a QCR remaster on **January 9, 2026**.

## 3. ACTIVATED Records

Official route: `https://activated-records.com/demo.html`.

ACTIVATED Records is the hard-dance label division of Germany's ACTIVATED Network. The official site presents international artists and reach across Europe, Asia, Australia and the Americas. Accepted styles include hardstyle, rawstyle, X-Raw, hard techno, rave, uptempo, Frenchcore, hardcore, gabber, reverse bass and crossbreed.

The first-party form requires:

- first name and artist name;
- contact email and confirmation;
- track title and genre/style;
- a WAV or MP3 upload of no more than 100 MB;
- acceptance of the Terms and Privacy Policy.

Phone/WhatsApp, a streaming/cloud link, social profiles and notes are optional. Newsletter consent is separately optional.

The privacy policy states that demo email addresses are stored in an internal demo-submitter contact list for feedback, special submission calls and relevant label information. Uploaded audio is stated to be deleted automatically after **30 days**, and IP addresses are collected for abuse protection.

`k.schroetter@activated-network.com` and `info@activated-network.com` are first-party published, syntactically valid and organizationally domain-aligned, but they are management/general contacts rather than authorized demo substitutes.

Activity is current: Beatport lists **Heart Of A Lion** on **June 12, 2026** and numerous other 2026 Activated Records releases. The official release hub states that it is updated continuously.
