## Verification and enrichment notes

- CJSW was verified through its official active 2026 site, Music Department route and Music Submissions page. The route is digital-only through an external Jotform upload and requires a recent album release, three or more unique tracks, MP3 ZIP packaging and no singles. The Music & Performance Director contact was redacted by the fetch layer and was not decoded or guessed.
- CKUT was verified through its official active 2026 site, Submit Music page and Contact page. It accepts physical releases and 320 MP3 digital submissions through the Music Department but rejects most singles, requires at least three distinct tracks for most formats and applies library/station fit review.
- WMBR was verified through its official MIT site, Your Music page, Contact page and Staff page. Its official route strongly prefers tangible physical releases and includes protected image-based Music Department and genre-director contacts that were not decoded or guessed.
- WMSE was verified through its official active 2026 site, Music Department page and Contact page. It accepts physical and digital-download routes through the Music Director, requires FCC-clean content checks and exposes a public Music Department contact form with CAPTCHA.
- WRPI was verified through its official site and Contact page. The page lists the Music Director contact and station mailing address, but no complete public auto-submit form or detailed music package policy was exposed.
