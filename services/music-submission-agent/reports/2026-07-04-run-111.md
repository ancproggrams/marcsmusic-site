## Verification notes

- CJSR was verified from its official Submit Music page. The route supports physical mail/drop-off and digital album/EP delivery to the Music Department, rejects singles, discourages paid streaming links and requires durable downloadable files plus metadata and language-warning review.
- CJSF was verified from its active 2026 site and official Music Department page. The route gives a Music Coordinator path, physical local-submission guidance, National Music Director/!earshot Distro distribution language and broadcast-rights conditions.
- !earshot Distro was verified from its official NCRA/ANREC-associated public site. It is an account-based paid upload platform for campus/community radio distribution with sign-up/login routes and public per-song/per-album pricing.
- RTRFM was verified from its active July 2026 site and official Submit Your Music page. The route exposes a public submission form with artist/contact fields, file-upload and music-link fields, redacted email text and physical-copy side instructions.
- Triple J Unearthed was verified from the official ABC site, which shows active July 2026 featured artists, staff reviews and station discovery links. The upload/discovery workflow remains manual because account/session and Australian-artist eligibility boundaries must be respected.
