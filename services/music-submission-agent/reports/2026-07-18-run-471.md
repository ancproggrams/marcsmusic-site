## WRIU verification

WRIU's official submission page welcomes bands, singers, labels and promotional companies and explicitly welcomes unsigned, local and independent artists. It publishes separate genre mailboxes. The three most relevant to MarcsMusic are `rpmsubmissions@wriu.org`, `worldsubmissions@wriu.org` and `reggaesubmissions@wriu.org`.

The official site carries a 2026 copyright notice and exposes current FM and RIU2 streams, a programme guide, programme archives and active department routes.

The opportunity remains `needs_manual_review` because the central page does not publish exact digital file, bitrate, attachment-size, subject-line, clean-content, international or AI-music rules. A human must select only the appropriate department or justified combination, verify all rights and approve the final email, link or attachment.

## The Edge verification

The Edge describes itself as Jamaica's Global Reggae Top 40 station and publishes a dedicated first-party form. It accepts radio-ready singles only, rejects albums, requires MP3 at 320 kbps or higher, a visible online presence, artist and contact information, social handles, a music link and a description.

The content rules prohibit profanity, curse words, defamatory lyrics, glorification of violence, guns, scamming or illegal activity, and derogatory or objectifying lyrics. Material must be uplifting, feel-good or informative and meet Broadcasting Commission of Jamaica standards. The page also states that the artist must be a member of a Jamaican music-industry association.

Current activity was verified from the official Monday-Saturday programme schedule, named current hosts and shows, daily live-stream route, nationwide FM frequencies and published Reggae, R&B and gospel programming.

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because the association requirement may make MarcsMusic ineligible. A human must verify qualifying membership, clean content, MP3 quality, online presence, link permissions, all rights, unpublished AI eligibility, hidden controls and the final submission.

## Indie Global 365 verification

Indie Global 365 explicitly publishes a free first-party route for possible radio rotation. The criteria accept Hip-hop, R&B and Caribbean reggae/soca; the home page also lists Afro Pop. Up to two positive, uplifting, motivational, conscious or feel-good tracks may be submitted.

The form requires 192 kbps MP3 files, no more than 10 MB per track, artist-and-title filenames, artist name, email, song titles, up to two song files and one image per song, and acceptance of the Terms & Conditions. The site says approved artists are notified by email with the rotation start date.

The linked 2019 policy does not align cleanly with the current upload form: it discusses local stations and CD delivery, while also requiring ASCAP/BMI registration or personally controlled performance rights and SoundExchange registration or personally controlled streaming rights. A separate $15 artist-membership plan is optional and is not required for the free submission route.

The opportunity remains `needs_manual_review` because of direct media uploads, terms acceptance, rights warranties, inconsistent legacy policy text, file-size ambiguity, optional paid boundaries, unpublished AI policy and hidden live controls.

## Smooth Jazz Club verification

Smooth Jazz Club publishes `info@smoothjazz.club` as the official submission mailbox. It accepts Smooth Jazz, Contemporary Jazz, Instrumental Jazz, radio-friendly Jazz Fusion and Chill/Lounge with a Smooth Jazz feel. Required supporting material includes artist, track and album data, release date, a short biography, a WAV or high-quality MP3 link, artwork or a press photo, and website and social links.

The route accepts WeTransfer, Dropbox, Google Drive or a private SoundCloud link. The platform states that its programmes are carried by 71 stations and reach more than 250,000 weekly listeners. Its contact page lists programme posts dated March 1-3, 2026.

The opportunity remains `needs_manual_review` because the format is narrow and only suitable MarcsMusic chill, lounge or jazz-fusion-adjacent material should be considered. A human must review audio and artwork preparation, external-link permissions, all rights, unpublished AI eligibility and the final email.
