## Verification and enrichment

### 95bFM Auckland

- Authorized route: the current official homepage links directly to the station's Submit Music instructions, which direct artists to a dedicated music-submissions email link.
- Required delivery: the station requires both a streaming link and a download link to a lossless file. WAV and FLAC are explicitly supported.
- Authentication boundary: the stream and download must not require an account, login or password; Spotify-only delivery and password-protected Dropbox access are explicitly unsuitable.
- Required context: artist name, song name, origin and release timing should be supplied; only a short contextual note is needed rather than a long biography.
- Release-age note: tracks older than one month may still be submitted, but are no longer eligible for A/B high-rotation playlisting.
- Protected contact: the submission destination is exposed through a Cloudflare-protected email link on both the Submit Music page and Staff Directory. It was not decoded, guessed or stored.
- Adjacent form: the Staff Directory contains a general first-party contact form, but it was not queued because the station provides a purpose-specific music-submission route.
- Genre fit: the current schedule includes electronic works, dub and reggae, alternative hip-hop, house, jungle, experimental music and globally sourced programming.
- Activity: the official site displayed live programming on 14 July 2026, current shows and an announced station event for 31 July 2026.
- Manual boundary: a human must open the official protected email link, confirm the current destination and international eligibility, select the track, prepare the public stream and lossless download, verify rights, content labels and current AI-origin policy, and send manually.

- Business-email verification: `music@radioactive.fm` was validated through official publication, valid syntax, first-party domain alignment, cross-page station context and explicit submission purpose. No SMTP, MX, catch-all, mailbox-level or deliverability probe was performed.
- Manual boundary: a human must reconfirm current delivery and international rules, avoid unsolicited attachments unless expressly allowed, choose a suitable MarcsMusic release, verify rights and content labels, and send manually. Physical delivery also requires human preparation and postage.
