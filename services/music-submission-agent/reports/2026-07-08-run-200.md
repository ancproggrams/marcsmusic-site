1. WERS 88.9FM Wicked Local Wednesday MP3 Upload Local Boston Route

   - Queue status: needs_manual_review
   - Review reason: local Boston-area eligibility, upload form, contact fields, MP3 choice, rights, radio-clean status and human-verification boundary.

2. WMBR MIT Physical CD Music Director Freeform Airplay Route
   - Official route: WMBR's Your Music page gives a Music Director postal address, treats unsigned bands like major label bands and urges artists/labels to send tangible physical music.
   - Queue status: needs_manual_review
   - Review reason: physical submission preference, one-sheet, freeform DJ fit, FCC-clean status, genre targeting and no unsolicited update/attachment policy.

3. WMFO Tufts Music Director CD Digital Library Route
   - Official route: WMFO lists the 2025-2026 Music Director contact, describes constant CD intake and a digital Rivendell music database/library.
   - Queue status: needs_manual_review
   - Review reason: Music Director route requires owner review for email versus physical format, metadata, rights, clean edits and package/link choice.

4. KXCI Tucson MP3 WAV Download Link Music Department Route
   - Official route: KXCI accepts music from all over the world, prioritizes local/regional/Arizona artists, and accepts MP3/WAV download links plus CDs/vinyl.
   - Queue status: needs_manual_review
   - Review reason: protected contact boundary, download-link preparation, explicit-language metadata, local/regional fit and physical-copy decision.

5. KFAI Fresh Air Music Library Email Submission Route
   - Official route: KFAI's Music Department & Music Library invites artists to share music by email and notes that receipt may not be confirmed because of request volume.
   - Queue status: needs_manual_review
   - Review reason: protected email route, diverse volunteer programming fit, link-only pitch, metadata, rights and no repeated follow-up.
