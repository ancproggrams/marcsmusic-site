## Verification notes

- CJSW: official submission page accepts only digital music submissions, requires new albums released within the last three months, three or more unique tracks, MP3 format and .zip album delivery, and exposes a Jotform upload route. The rendered contact email was protected/non-plaintext and was not decoded.
- CFUV: official submission page lists the Music Director, public music submission email, digital download-link requirements, at least three unique tracks, 320 kbps MP3 guidance, bio/tracklisting guidance and a local-only physical exception.
- WMSE: official music department page accepts CD, vinyl, cassette and digital download submissions, requires FCC-clean track awareness and gives the Music Director route for digital submissions.
- KALX: official contact page lists the Music Directors, public music email, physical mailing route and states that KALX currently accepts only physical, professionally pressed CDs or LPs for airplay consideration.
- Radio Milwaukee: official Submit Music page requests uncompressed audio, WAV preferred, mixed/mastered radio edits and supporting documentation, and the contact page includes Music Submissions as a contact category.
