## Verification and enrichment

### KEXP

- Authorized route: KEXP's official Submission Guidelines and Contact pages direct digital airplay submissions to `md@kexp.org`, the Music Director and Music Department address.
- Required package: streaming and WAV download links, artist name and pronouns, title and label, phonetic guidance where needed, focus tracks, release dates, clean edits for FCC issues, lyrics, credits, biography or one-sheet and relevant Pacific Northwest or Bay Area show dates.
- Restrictions: no attachments, no physical submission to the music department, no expectation of a reply and no social-media harassment. The official guidelines identify the Music Department mailbox as the best route.
- Pricing: free submission; KEXP explicitly rejects pay-for-play.
- Genre fit: KEXP describes its rotation as an eclectic and diverse mix of new singles, albums and EPs. MarcsMusic electronic, bass, world-fusion and crossover material may fit after human review.
- Activity: the official homepage displayed live programming, current new-music reviews and July 2026 events.
- Business email verification: `md@kexp.org` was confirmed directly on two first-party pages. Syntax, domain alignment and stated purpose were validated. No SMTP, MX, catch-all or mailbox-level probe was performed.

### KBOO

- Authorized route: KBOO's official Submit Your Music page accepts general submissions by physical mail to KBOO-FM, 20 SE 8th Ave, Portland, OR 97214.
- Digital boundary: KBOO states that it does not yet have a digital library and lacks volunteer capacity to download and burn CDs from digital submissions. No digital route was inferred or substituted.
- Genre routing: the official page names Keith Bloom for electronic and other genres; Diane Karl for folk; Brandon Lieberman for rock, punk, electronic and other genres; Rita Rega for jazz; Brendon Reyes for hip-hop; and Charlie Rooney for world music and reggae. Artists may instead address a package to a matching show from the official program directory.
- Ownership and outcome: submissions become KBOO property, are not returned, may be passed on and carry no guarantee of airplay.
- Pricing: no submission fee is stated, but physical media and postage require human approval and expense.
- Activity: the first-party website remained operational with live/on-air programming, a 2026 schedule and recent 2026 playlists and audio.
- Contact verification: no public general music-submission email was identified for this route, and none was guessed.
