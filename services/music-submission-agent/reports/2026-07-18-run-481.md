## Camaradio verification

Camaradio's official first-party form accepts one song by either a downloadable music-file link or a direct WAV/MP3 upload. Direct uploads must be 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz, 16-bit stereo and under 10 MB. The form asks for contact details, artist name, Ventura County yes/no, city, song title and release date, with optional album information. Submission requires the artist to affirm the necessary rights and authorizes Camaradio to air the track if selected.

The form contains visible arithmetic human verification. No answer was entered and no anti-bot control was completed or bypassed. The Ventura County yes/no field indicates that nonlocal submissions can be represented, but international eligibility is not expressly confirmed.

Relevant current programming includes Indie Beginnings, DJ Space Cat's Galactic Dance Party, Modern Jetset, jazz, blues and other eclectic formats.

## WUAG verification

WUAG's university-hosted form accepts a single song, EP or full-length album. It supports direct WAV/MP3 upload with a published 2 GB maximum or a Dropbox/Google Drive download link. YouTube, SoundCloud and Spotify links are rejected because they cannot be downloaded. The form asks whether the artist is North Carolina-based and permits both Yes and No, but international eligibility is not separately stated.

The form asks for date, email, artist name, song-or-album selection, title, genre, file or cloud link, FCC-violation disclosure and optional notes. WUAG advises artists to provide their own clean edit when needed. No visible CAPTCHA appeared in passive indexing; hidden or submit-time anti-spam controls remain unconfirmed.

Current activity was verified through the official FAQ and contact pages. WUAG states that it operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year through FM and Live365. The current board and office-hours page identifies the Music + Productions Director and links directly to the canonical submission form.

Sources:

## KAZI verification

KAZI publishes `music@kazifm.org` as the official Untapped music-submission mailbox. The instructions request one new, quality radio-edited song, a streaming link and downloadable MP3 or WAV, plus the submitter's first and last name, artist name, website or social handle, music format and email address.
