## Verification and enrichment

- Dirty Workz: the official native form accepts MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC or OGG up to 50 MB and asks for track title, email, artist name, genre and an originality/rights confirmation. The label permits one track per submission and three per day, discourages unofficial remixes and bootlegs, requires cleared samples and asks for 30 days of exclusivity. `licensing@dirtyworkz.com` is public but explicitly restricted to licensing and is not a demo route. Current official activity includes 2026 news, releases and playlists.
- Scantraxx: the official demo page accepts finished Hard Dance productions only, in MP3 or WAV, and rejects works in progress, snippets and fully AI-generated tracks. It uses an embedded or directly linked Tstack upload workflow and states that suitable demos are contacted within 30 days. The official site lists releases through 2026-07-10. No public demo email was exposed or guessed.
- Dirtybird: the official site directs demos to its LabelWorx DemoBox, which requires JavaScript and asks for artist/track/contact data, country, prior-label history, exclusivity status and an MP3 or WAV file. The submission language includes rights, data-storage and gratis social/live-stream playback permissions. The official site links current releases, radio programming and a 2026 Campout event. No direct demo email was exposed.
- Critical Music: the official contact page welcomes demos through the Demo Submission drop-down, prefers 320 kbps audio and protects the form with reCAPTCHA. It separately publishes `info@criticalmusic.com` for press and `badger@criticalmusic.com` for label-management enquiries; those addresses are not used to bypass the demo form. The homepage displays an active release catalogue and event listing.
- Shogun Audio: the current Demos navigation points to LabelRadar, while the official contact page also publishes a Label Engine demo URL. The current destination, label/imprint fit, account/session, rights, metadata and any LabelRadar credits or plan limits require manual confirmation. `info@shogunaudio.co.uk`, `shop@shogunaudio.co.uk` and `licensing@shogunaudio.co.uk` are public business contacts but are not substitute demo routes. The site carries a 2026 copyright notice and active release/event content.
