## Verification and enrichment

### KSPC 88.7FM

- Authorized route: KSPC's official Music Submissions page welcomes albums and EPs from artists of all genres through either the station's dedicated email action or professionally mailed CDs and records.
- Digital requirements: digital submissions may contain files or a downloadable cloud-storage location. Streaming-only YouTube or SoundCloud links, Spotify and iTunes are not accepted. Singles and demos are not accepted.
- Physical requirements: CDs and records may be sent to the Music Director at the official Pomona College address. Cassettes are not accepted.
- AI-origin restriction: KSPC does not accept work that is substantially AI-generated or falsely represented as non-AI. The selected MarcsMusic release therefore requires a human authorship and production-origin review before any submission.
- Contact boundary: the dedicated submission address is exposed through a Cloudflare-protected email action. It was not decoded, guessed or stored; a reviewer must open the official page manually.
- Form boundary: KSPC's separate general-contact form is explicitly for other inquiries and contains a leave-unchanged honeypot field. It was not queued, filled or treated as a music-submission fallback.
- Commercial boundary: KSPC explicitly states that it does not accept pay-for-play.
- Activity and fit: KSPC's official site contains 2026 editorial content, active programming and a mission supporting new, local and underrepresented voices. Its all-genres policy and independent/community-radio format can fit selected electronic, reggae, world-fusion, experimental and alternative MarcsMusic releases.
- Manual boundary: a human must select an eligible album or EP, confirm that it is not substantially AI-generated, verify rights and metadata, choose a downloadable or physical route and complete delivery without touching the general form or its honeypot.

### KALX 90.7FM

- Authorized route: KALX's official Contact page instructs artists to send professionally pressed physical CDs or LPs to the Music Directors at the published University of California Berkeley address.
- Digital prohibition: KALX explicitly rejects downloads, file transfers, Bandcamp, Spotify, YouTube, CD-Rs and other digital submission methods. Its public Music Director mailbox is not permission to deliver music digitally.
- Format detail: the official FAQ references LPs, CDs and 7-inch records and states that CD singles are not accepted. The current Contact page specifically describes professionally pressed CDs or LPs, so format selection and packaging require manual confirmation.
- Public business contact: `music@kalx.berkeley.edu` is published for current Music Directors Dasha Shevchenko and Ava Hoener. It was verified as a current first-party role address but was not queued as a digital delivery route.
- Adjacent route exclusion: interview requests and other departmental contacts are separate and were not treated as alternative airplay-submission routes.
- Activity and fit: current operation was confirmed through live recent-play data, a populated daily schedule, event listings for July 17-19, 2026, 2026 specialty charts and a 2026 copyright notice. KALX's freeform programming can accommodate electronic, experimental, world, reggae, alternative and independent music.
- Manual boundary: a human must select a compliant professionally pressed, non-CD-single release, confirm current international acceptance and packaging rules, prepare customs and shipping documents and mail the package to the official address.
