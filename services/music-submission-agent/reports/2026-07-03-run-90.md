## Verification notes

- WXDU: official Contact Us page says to submit music for airplay, prefers Bandcamp YUM codes over other downloads, lists music@wxdu.org and provides a physical mailing address. The same page states WXDU's progressive alternative mission and music-industry integrity posture.
- KTRU: official contact page includes a contact form and states music submissions should use USPS/UPS physical routes with physical formats only: CD, LP or 7-inch.
- WHPK: official site shows current now-playing data, genre categories including Electronic, Rap, Jazz, Folk, Rock and International, and explicitly says not to contact the station manager for music submissions while linking a music-submissions route.
- WICB: official site navigation exposes a Submit Music route. Public station reference material confirms WICB is active with alternative programming plus reggae, jazz, blues, hip-hop and local-music support.
- KCRW: official site is active in 2026 with current music playlists, music shows, DJs and a public Contact Us page. Public station reference material says local and regional touring artists can send recordings to KCRW for airplay consideration.
