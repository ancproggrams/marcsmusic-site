## 1. Dirty Disco Recordings

Official route: `https://www.dirtydiscorecordings.com/contact`.

Dirty Disco Recordings is an independent electronic-dance label based in Melbourne. Its official demo form accepts all electronic styles and allows the submitter to choose between the main label and the emerging-artist imprint Dirty Disco Imminent.

The visible form requests:

- first and last name;
- email;
- a message explaining the release and label fit;
- a SoundCloud, Dropbox, WeTransfer or similar track link;
- a best social-media link;
- the target Dirty Disco imprint.

The page rejects unfinished or already-released music. Submission confirms that the artist owns or controls the relevant rights. No public business email was identified as an authorized alternative to the form.

Activity is current: the official site presents an active 2026 catalogue, and Beatport lists **POIZZONED — Techno Beat** on **April 10, 2026** and **JAXX T — No Escape** on **March 27, 2026**.

## 2. Play Records

Official route: `https://www.playrecords.net/demos.php`.

Play Records is a Toronto-based international electronic and dance label founded in 1996. The official Demos page contains a LabelWorx-hosted form and requires:

- completed electronic or dance music;
- unreleased material;
- a guarantee that the submitter owns 100% of the content;
- no unauthorized remix or cover;
- disclosure of the source of an uncredited vocal;
- use of the official form rather than staff email.

Unmastered demos are permitted, but a mastered version is required if Play Records wants to sign the track.

This route is **permanently manual-only**. Play Records explicitly states that it ignores automated and third-party demo-submission services. The embedded LabelWorx form's exact fields, upload method, login state and CAPTCHA were not passively exposed.

`melody@playrecords.net`, `simon@playrecords.net`, `sydney@playrecords.net` and `admin@playrecords.net` are first-party published, syntactically valid and domain-aligned business contacts. The same official contact page expressly prohibits sending demos to staff email. The privacy policy states that form data is transferred directly to Play Records' LabelWorx account.

Activity is current: the official catalogue lists **Angelo Maria Calderano — BOOM** released **July 17, 2026**, **Spirited** scheduled for **July 24, 2026**, and **Summer Sessions 2026, Vol. 2** scheduled for **August 7, 2026**.

## 3. HEXAGON

Official route: the Trackstack-powered demo section at `https://www.hexagonhq.com/`.

HEXAGON's first-party site states that demos are open, that Don Diablo opens every file, and that the label is seeking:

- House;
- Bass House;
- Club Bangers.

The embedded inbox is powered by Trackstack. Trackstack states that artists can send tracks to public inbox links for free, while optional paid tiers exist for broader platform functionality. Its terms state that most functionality requires an account, users must be over 18, automated use is prohibited, and uploaded tracks are subject to limited platform and receiver review/promotional-performance licences.

The exact HEXAGON inbox URL, fields, registration flow, OTP or CAPTCHA state were not passively exposed. No public HEXAGON business email was identified as an authorized demo alternative. `support@trackstack.app` is provider support only.

Activity is current: Beatport lists multiple HEXAGON releases through **May 15, 2026**, and the official site advertises a HEXAGON ADE label night on **October 22, 2026**.

- exact label, genre and imprint fit;

HEXAGON additionally requires manual review of Trackstack registration, age eligibility, account/OTP controls, platform licences, free-versus-paid tier separation and the anti-automation terms.
