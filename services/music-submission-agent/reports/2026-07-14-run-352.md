## Verification and enrichment

### Edge Radio 99.3FM

- Authorized route: the official Submit Your Music page explicitly invites new and emerging artists from Tasmania, Australia and around the world.
- Digital requirements: include a short biography, relevant upcoming-gig details, a streaming link and a high-quality download link.
- Accepted digital formats: 320 kbps MP3, AAC, WAV, FLAC and OGG.
- Physical option: CDs may be posted to the Music Department at the published Hobart address; the station suggests multiple copies to help distribute the release to relevant programs.
- Local-status boundary: only genuine Tasmanian artists may use the `Tasmanian Music Submission` marker. MarcsMusic must not claim that status.
- Protected contact: the dedicated music-submission destination is exposed through a Cloudflare-protected email link. It was not decoded, guessed or stored.
- Cost separation: the station separately sells optional promotional campaigns, but no fee or paid campaign is required for editorial music submission or airplay consideration.
- Activity: July 2026 homepage posts and the current program guide confirm operation. The guide contains recurring `New Music on Edge Radio` blocks and relevant electronic, experimental, digital-beat, hip-hop, reggae and world-music programming.
- Manual boundary: a human must reopen the official page, use only the displayed music destination, select a rights-cleared MarcsMusic release, prepare the required stream and download links, omit the Tasmanian marker and send manually.

### SYN Media 90.7FM

- Authorized route: the official Contact page publishes `music@syn.org.au` specifically for music submissions.
- Editorial workflow: SYN Music Directors review submissions on a rolling basis and may not respond to every submission.
- Audio delivery: use a download link for 320 kbps MP3 or WAV files; direct file attachments are prohibited.
- Required supporting material: press release or artist biography, track or EP/album title, release date and relevant website, social-media, upcoming-gig and previous-release links.
- Contact separation: `music.content@syn.org.au` is for interview requests and was not queued as a duplicate airplay route.
- Eligibility boundary: the page does not expressly state international eligibility or AI-origin rules, so these must be confirmed before sending.
- Activity: current 2026 staff listings, the official schedule and reviews dated through May 25, 2026 confirm operation; the review archive includes electronic-festival coverage relevant to MarcsMusic's electronic catalogue.
- Cost and access: no submission fee, login, CAPTCHA or payment requirement is stated.
- Manual boundary: a human must verify current eligibility, select a rights-cleared release, prepare the required download and metadata package and send the email manually.
