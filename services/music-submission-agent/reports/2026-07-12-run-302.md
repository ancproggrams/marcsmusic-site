## Verification and enrichment

- XRAY.FM: the official contact page publishes `submissions@xray.fm` specifically for music submissions and links to separate submission guidelines. The official homepage shows a live schedule and stream. The linked guidelines are hosted in a dynamically rendered Google Drive document, so a human must inspect the current requirements before composing an email or supplying links or files.
- EARMILK: the official Submit Music page says the publication currently accepts submissions exclusively through SubmitHub or Pillargram. Its general contact form explicitly does not accept new music and automatically filters such messages. The site published current editorial content on July 10 and July 11, 2026. `david@earmilk.com` is publicly listed for media partnerships and events, not for music submissions.
- Stereofox: the official contact page directs music submissions to SubmitHub or Groover and explicitly says music sent by email or Discord will not be processed. The homepage shows current editorial and playlist activity. Login, credits, possible payment, privacy and cookie choices must be reviewed in the selected external platform.
- Acid Stag: the official Submit Music navigation leads to `acidstag.gumroad.com`. The publication posted new content dated July 7 through July 10, 2026. The external storefront is dynamically rendered, so the exact product, price, deliverables, disclosure, refund and checkout terms must be checked manually. A hidden anti-spam field was observed only in the adjacent newsletter form and was not treated as part of the submission workflow.
