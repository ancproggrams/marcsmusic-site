- SomaFM: official site confirms active listener-supported, commercial-free, hand-curated internet radio with a 2026 copyright footer and a music-submission page listing no-AI, digital-only, high-bitrate MP3, metadata, Bandcamp/YUM/WeTransfer/Dropbox and channel-specific music-director routing.
- WRTC-FM: official Trinity College WRTC site confirms non-commercial educational radio in Hartford with recent 2025 programming posts; the official contact page says people with music they wish WRTC to have can contact the relevant genre coordinator or send music to Attn: Music Director at the station mailing address.
- WMFO: official Tufts WMFO site confirms 2026 activity, round-the-clock freeform broadcasting, a Music Department, and public Music Director email md@wmfo.org; the Music Department page describes CDs, occasional vinyl/cassettes and new digital media being added to Rivendell.
- WESU: official WESU site confirms 2026 activity and identifies the station as a Wesleyan community service since 1939; the official contact page explicitly tells submitters to contact the Music Directors at wesumd@wesufm.org, asks for links rather than files and lists station/mailing addresses.
