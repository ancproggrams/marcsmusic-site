## Verification and enrichment

- STMPD RCRDS: the official label homepage links directly to its Demo Drop. The portal accepts original demo tracks only, excludes remixes and bootlegs, and requires connecting and logging in through SoundCloud. Account authorization, original-work status, ownership, private-link permissions, metadata and all portal declarations must remain manual. The official site displays current 2026 releases.
- Selected Base: the official Demo Drop states that the label is looking for new music and accepts only streamable links to unreleased tracks, preferably SoundCloud, YouTube, Dropbox or Google Drive. Its legal imprint publicly identifies `inbox@selectedbase.com` for demos and separates licensing, booking and general-contact mailboxes. Rights, unreleased status, link privacy and the final route must be reviewed manually.
- Future House Music: the official homepage and contact page direct artists to a Discord demodrop and describe community demo and feedback activity. Discord membership, login, channel rules, posting permissions, format, moderation and rights require manual review. The adjacent general contact form is not treated as a substitute submission route.
- Spinnin' Records: the official contact page directs new talent to the Talent Pool. The pool requires an account before upload and publicly displays ranked and recently added tracks. Account terms, public visibility, upload format, rights, master, metadata, voting or contest conditions and consent fields must be reviewed by a person.
- Dim Mak: the official navigation exposes a dedicated Send Demos page. The statically accessible page does not expose the complete live field set, upload or link requirements, declarations, consents, limits or runtime verification state, so the rendered workflow must be inspected manually before any data or audio is supplied. General, licensing, sync and publicity contacts remain separate from demos.
