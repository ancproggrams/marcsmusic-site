## Verification notes

- EARMILK was verified from its official Submit Music page and active July 2026 music posts. It accepts submissions through SubmitHub or Pillargram only.
- Stereofox was verified from its official contact page, active 2026 site footer and current editorial/playlist activity. Music submissions are routed to SubmitHub or Groover; email and Discord submissions are explicitly not processed for music.
- A&R Factory was verified from its official Submit Your Music page and active July 2026 posts. Its form supports one-track submissions across many genres, including EDM, electronic, drum and bass, dancehall, reggae, world, synthwave, techno and trap, with Free Submission plus Standard and Premium paid options.
- EDM Identity was verified from its official contact page and active July 2026 electronic-music coverage. It lists a track-submission route for links only, but the fetched contact values were redacted and were not decoded or guessed.
- House Nest was verified from its official Media & Promotion page and 2026 electronic-music activity. Its public submissions form includes enquiry types for news, interview, feature, single/EP review, album review, premiere, collaborations and in-the-mix.
