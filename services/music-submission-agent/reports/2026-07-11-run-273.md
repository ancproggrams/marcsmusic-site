## Verification and enrichment

- KEXP: the official Music Department route is `md@kexp.org`. Submissions must use streaming and downloadable WAV links rather than attachments and should include artist/pronoun information, release metadata, focus tracks, release dates, clean edits where needed, lyrics, credits, biography or one-sheet and relevant regional show dates. Singles, albums and EPs may be considered; physical submissions are rejected and airplay cannot be purchased.
- WREK: the current official music page links directly to the station's official legacy submission instructions. Those instructions publish `music.director@wrek.org`, permit email or physical delivery, welcome independently produced music and state that common formats are accepted, while CDs and LPs may receive more operator attention. The current and legacy pages show different street numbers, so the physical address requires confirmation before use.
- WTUL: the official contact page directs physical submissions to the station and digital submissions to the appropriate music director in its 2025-2026 staff directory. Head, Assistant and International/World Music Director roles are listed, but their email addresses are JavaScript-protected. The page also contains a CAPTCHA-protected general contact form; neither the CAPTCHA nor protected addresses were handled automatically.
- WUOG: the official contact page instructs artists seeking airtime consideration to mail an album in the appropriate format to the Music Directors. Public director email addresses are listed for contact, but the page does not authorize replacing the physical submission with email delivery. Accepted format and packaging details therefore require human confirmation.
- WKDU: the official public contact form includes a dedicated `Music Submission` topic with required name, email, subject and message fields. It does not publish detailed format, link or metadata requirements, so a human must inspect and complete the live form without attaching or supplying unrequested materials.

Official activity was verified from current station pages. KEXP lists programming and events for July 11-23, 2026; WREK publishes playlists dated July 9-10, 2026; WKDU publishes playlists through July 10 and events for July 12 and 15, 2026. WTUL provides a live stream and current staff-year directory, while WUOG maintains its live station site and current director roster.
