## 1. KXCI 91.3FM — Tucson, Arizona, United States

### Authorized routes

KXCI's official Music Department page states that it accepts and broadcasts music from all over the world while prioritizing Tucson, regional and Arizona artists.

Accepted delivery methods are:

- CDs;
- vinyl accompanied by a digital download;
- MP3 or WAV download links for digital albums.

The station requests:

- artist or band name;
- album title, artwork and a track list with song times;
- label name or self-released status;
- release date;
- genre, subgenre and comparable artists;
- explicit or questionable language disclosures, track numbers and lyrics where available;
- a brief biography, social links and relevant Tucson or regional performances;
- contact information.

Digital submissions are directed to a dedicated KXCI Music Department email action. The destination was protected in passive rendering and was not decoded, inferred or guessed. KXCI also publishes a separate national and international physical-delivery route addressed to the Music Department. Digital delivery is sufficient, although the station says multiple physical copies can help distribute releases across its DJs and studio libraries.

Official evidence:

- Website: `https://kxci.org/`
- Music Department and submission instructions: `https://kxci.org/about/music-department/`

### Activity and catalogue fit

KXCI's official site showed current playlist and two-week archive navigation, podcasts dated through July 12, 2026, news dated through July 10, 2026 and scheduled July-August 2026 programming and events.

Its eclectic program catalogue includes electronic, electro-pop, global, reggae, experimental and related music. Selected MarcsMusic releases may therefore be suitable, but no placement is implied.

### Business-contact verification

The purpose-bound Music Department email action is published directly on KXCI's official submission page. Its exact destination was protected by the site's email-protection layer in passive rendering. No decoding, pattern inference, SMTP probing, MX probing, catch-all testing or mailbox-level deliverability test was performed.

The separate Locals Only route is intended for Arizona artists and was excluded from the MarcsMusic queue.

### Manual-review controls

- Open the official Music Department page and use only the displayed authorized destination.
- Do not decode, infer or guess the protected address.
- Select an eligible album rather than assuming a single qualifies.
- Prepare a stable MP3 or WAV download link and complete album metadata.
- Verify rights, release timing, artwork, track times and explicit-content disclosures.
- For physical delivery, manually prepare CDs or vinyl, postage and customs documentation.
- Do not use the local-only route.
