## Verification and enrichment notes

- SoundCloud was verified through its official upload page. It exposes an Upload your first track route, says creators can connect with audiences for free, lists 180 minutes of free uploads and requires JavaScript/session handling before upload.
- Bandcamp was verified through its official Bandcamp for Artists page. It describes a customizable direct-to-fan artist store, says artist accounts are free, lists 15% digital revenue share, 10% merch revenue share and an optional Bandcamp Pro tier.
- Jamendo was verified through its official Artist Services page. It routes artists through sign-in/start-now actions and covers Jamendo Music reach, sync licensing, in-store placement, release promotion and Content ID monetization.
- Mixcloud was verified through its official upload page. It offers Upload for free, sign-up/login routes, creator tools, unlimited uploads and live streaming under Pro, plus monetization, stats and host-tagging side-routes.
- hearthis.at was verified through its official homepage/upload workflow. It exposes Upload, Sign in and Create Account actions, says users can upload their own music, lists up to 400 MB of free weekly uploads, and shows relevant genres for MarcsMusic including Drum & Bass, Dubstep/Bass, Dub, Reggae/Dancehall, EDM, World and Electronic.
