## Verification and enrichment

- UKF: the official Submit your Demo page invites artists to use its first-party form for a possible release on UKF or Pilot and explicitly says not to submit demos to the support address. The official homepage showed active releases dated 2026-07-08 and 2026-07-01 and describes UKF and Pilot as active bass-music labels. The passive extraction did not expose the full form fields, asset limits, rights declarations or runtime protections, and a cookie-consent interface was present.
- Circus Records: the official About page says it accepts demos through LabelRadar only and links the Circus Records + Circus Electric profile. The same page publishes `info@circus-records.co.uk` for general questions and requests; that address was validated as an official business contact but excluded as a demo mailbox. LabelRadar is dynamically loaded and requires a human account/session review, including current campaign availability, imprint choice, rights, free-slot or credit pricing and any optional payment.
- Liquicity Records: the official demo page directs all music submissions to its Label Engine form and says every track is listened to, although a response is not guaranteed. The form requires artist name, real name and a confirmed email; accepts up to five titled MP3 tracks; makes social profiles optional; and includes a message section. The homepage lists active 2026 releases and events, including Liquicity Festival scheduled for 2026-07-24 through 2026-07-26.
