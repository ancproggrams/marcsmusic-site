### Release-type and delivery restrictions

KSPC accepts albums and EPs. It expressly does not accept:

- singles;
- demos;
- cassette tapes;
- Spotify links;
- iTunes links;
- YouTube or SoundCloud delivery when the supplied site does not also provide a download method;
- work substantially generated through AI;
- pay-for-play inquiries.

A MarcsMusic single is therefore ineligible even when its genre fits the station. Only a complete album or EP may enter the review queue for this route.

A downloadable delivery method is mandatory for digital submissions. A public or private stream by itself is insufficient unless the same destination also permits KSPC to download the material.

### AI-provenance boundary

KSPC states that work substantially generated through AI is not accepted and that work must not falsely claim to have been created without artificial generation.

Before submission, a human must document the album or EP's production provenance and determine that the material complies with the strictest reasonable reading of this rule.

The route must not be used when:

- substantial musical composition, performance or production was generated by AI;
- provenance is unknown or cannot be substantiated;
- the release description would misrepresent artificial generation;
- only a single version exists and no eligible album or EP package is available.

The exact threshold for "substantially generated" is not further defined on the reviewed page, so any borderline release remains blocked pending clarification from KSPC.
