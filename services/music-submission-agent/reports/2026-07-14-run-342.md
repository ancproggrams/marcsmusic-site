## Verification and enrichment

### Triple R 102.7FM

- Authorized route: Triple R's official Submit Music page publishes `music@rrr.org.au` for station-wide digital servicing when an artist is unsure which program is the best fit or a program has no public contact.
- Business-email verification: syntax, official publication, first-party domain alignment and stated purpose were verified. No mailbox-level probe was performed.
- Required material: a clear subject such as artist plus release title, a stream that requires no login, a non-expiring high-quality download, clearly named files, a one- or two-paragraph biography and relevant gig or launch information.
- Audio rules: the download must be a 320 kbps MP3 or WAV. SoundCloud, Bandcamp and YouTube are acceptable for streaming, while streaming-service-only links are less desired. Dropbox, non-expiring WeTransfer and Google Drive are suggested for delivery.
- Attachment rule: large audio files must not be attached to the email. PDF press releases are accepted, but the important information should be placed in the email body.
- Editorial model: Triple R is not centrally playlisted; individual presenters choose their tracks. Direct presenter servicing is allowed only through public program contact details and was not harvested or queued in this run.
- Optional physical route: the station also accepts CDs, LPs and cassettes at its published Music Department address. This was recorded as an adjacent physical route, not a duplicate queue item.
- Genre fit: current programs and editorial material cover electronic, dance, experimental, ambient, hip-hop, reggae, dub and international music.
- Activity: the official homepage displayed programs and episodes dated 13 July 2026, videos dated 8 July 2026 and scheduled events through December 2026.
- Manual boundary: a human must select the release, verify ownership, release status, broadcast suitability and international editorial fit, approve the subject, biography, stream, download and gig details, reconfirm the mailbox and send the email manually.

- Dynamic-form boundary: the page initially displays a `Loading form` state and then exposes required fields. No login, account, fee or mandatory payment is stated. No CAPTCHA was confirmed in passive inspection, but any runtime anti-spam or human-verification control must be handled manually.

- Manual boundary: a human must inspect the live form for current upload or link behavior, file limits, rights or consent wording and runtime protection; choose the release; verify ownership, release status and geographic fit; approve all personal and promotional information; and submit manually.
