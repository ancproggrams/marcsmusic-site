## 1. Monstercat

Official route: `https://www.monstercat.com/contact-us`, linking to:

- `https://www.labelradar.com/labels/monstercat/portal` for Uncaged and Instinct;
- `https://www.labelradar.com/labels/monstercatsilk/portal` for Silk.

Monstercat states that every submitted demo is listened to and asks artists to submit only their best, original work. The three imprints provide distinct fit paths:

- **Uncaged** — dubstep, drum-and-bass, trap, breaks and other heavy bass;
- **Instinct** — melodic dance, house, pop and indie dance;
- **Silk** — progressive/deep house, trance, ambient and chillout.

The portals are hosted by LabelRadar. LabelRadar requires an artist account and five credits for a one-to-one label submission. A Lite account receives five free credits monthly when its balance is zero, so cash payment is not necessarily required; purchasing credits or PRO is optional.

Activity is current: Monstercat released **Vicetone feat. Cozi Zuehlsdorff — Nevada (Gabry Ponte Remix)** on **July 3, 2026**, published **Call of the Wild 617** on **July 8, 2026**, and **Silk Showcase 862** on **July 15, 2026**.

The protected support, press and event contacts on the official contact page are not authorized demo alternatives and were not decoded.

## 2. NCS / NoCopyrightSounds

Official route: `https://ncs.io/contact`, linking to `https://www.labelradar.com/labels/ncs/portal`.

NCS is a UK-based electronic label and creator-music platform. Its official catalogue spans House, Dubstep, Trap, Drum & Bass, Electro Pop and many related electronic genres, including several strong MarcsMusic fits.

The route is account- and credit-controlled by LabelRadar:

- an artist account is required;
- a direct label submission consumes five credits;
- the Lite tier can supply five free monthly credits when the balance is zero;
- extra credits and PRO are optional paid products;
- the artist uploads the track and selects a representative 20-second preview;
- optional fan-app exposure must be reviewed before enabling it.

Activity is current: the official NCS library lists **ZKG — RIGHT HERE** on **July 14, 2026**, **Kkriegerz — Waiting For You** on **July 10, 2026**, and **2frers — FLIRT LIGHTS** on **July 9, 2026**.

No public NCS business email was identified as an authorized demo alternative.

## 3. Steyoyoke Recordings

Official route: `https://www.steyoyoke.com/submit-your-demo/`.

Steyoyoke's first-party form accepts only a **private SoundCloud playlist submitted exclusively to Steyoyoke**. The artist must confirm that:

- the music has not been sent to other labels;
- other DJs are not currently playing or testing it;
- the submission does not infringe third-party rights;
- the submitted personal information may be stored for review and used to contact the artist.

Visible fields include Artist Name, Email, Country, Spotify, Instagram and the secret SoundCloud playlist link. Newsletter consent is separate and optional.

`info@steyoyoke.com`, `booking@steyoyoke.com`, `management@steyoyoke.com`, `licensing@steyoyoke.com`, `shop@steyoyoke.com` and `jobs@steyoyoke.com` are first-party published, syntactically valid and domain-aligned business contacts, but none is authorized as a demo alternative.

The site presented a Cloudflare/browser-verification page during passive inspection. It was not attempted or bypassed.

Activity is current: the official catalogue displays **Steyoyoke Anniversary, Vol. 15** and newer releases; Beatport lists anniversary tracks dated **May 15, 2026**.

## Manual-review boundaries

All three routes require human review of:

- exact label, imprint and genre fit;
- account, login, credit or browser-verification controls;
- track upload, private playlist or 20-second preview;
- release and exclusivity status;
- master, composition, sample and contributor rights;
- Content ID and distributor conflicts;
- generative-AI eligibility or disclosure;
- privacy, retention and personal-data handling;
- platform terms and optional paid-product separation;
- post-selection contract terms;
- final form or portal action.

Steyoyoke additionally imposes an exclusivity condition covering other labels and DJ testing. Monstercat and NCS additionally require manual use of LabelRadar and confirmation that free credits are available before any optional purchase.
