## Most FM verification

Most FM publishes a dedicated first-party form for music submissions. The station requests a streaming link and a downloadable audio link, prefers singles rather than complete albums or EPs, and asks the artist to identify a focus track when submitting more than one song.

The form visibly asks for name, email, song name, artist name, explicit-lyrics status, streaming link, download link, origin or current base, release date and a message. The published guidelines accept WAV, MP3 and M4A, request a clean version where possible and ask for a short artist description plus relevant release information.

Current activity was verified from the official live site, weekly schedule, named presenters and current June 2026 music and station events. Relevant shows include Fresh Cuts New Music, Studio Kinda Cloudy, The BPM Radio Show and Music Without Frontiers.

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because international-artist eligibility and AI-music policy are not explicit. A human must select the focus single and clean edit, verify streaming and download-link permissions, check metadata and all music rights, inspect hidden controls and approve the final submission.

## The Detour Network verification

The Detour Network publishes `music@thedetour.us` as its specific music-submission mailbox. It accepts MP3, WAV or AAC attachments and asks artists to submit no more than their best three tracks. MP3 files must be at least 96 kbps and are preferably no more than 192 kbps. The filename must follow the `Artist Name – Song Title` pattern, ID3 metadata must be complete and clean versions should be supplied where possible.

The route also offers postal CD delivery. The official submission terms require the sender to be legally authorized to submit the music, grant terrestrial and internet broadcast permission, confirm that the music may be broadcast in the United States and accept a clause stating that the network need not pay royalties.

Activity was verified from the current detour MUSIC and detour TALK stream routes, the official listening page and a January 2026 acceptable-use policy. The station describes worldwide internet availability and a freeform music service covering independent and mainstream alternative, rap, reggae, pop, techno, blues, jazz and other genres.

The route remains `needs_manual_review` because audio attachments, filename and ID3 preparation, clean-content labeling, composition/master/sample/contributor rights, foreign-law eligibility and the explicit no-royalty clause require legal and human approval before any email is sent.
