## Verification notes

- Playlist Push was verified through the official Playlist Push site and pricing page. The site exposes app sign-up/sign-in, Spotify playlist and TikTok creator promotion routes, vetted curator language, live reporting dashboard context, a Spotify campaign minimum of $280 and no playlist-result guarantee.
- SoundCampaign was verified through the official SoundCampaign site and pricing page. The site exposes Get Started/sign-in app links, Spotify and TikTok promotion routes, curator feedback, A&R campaign review, genre/language targeting, dynamic campaign budgets and an average campaign cost around $100.
- One Submit was verified through the official One Submit homepage, artist sign-up page, contact page and pricing page. The route covers Spotify playlists, YouTube channels, TikTok influencers, blogs, online radio stations, music magazines and record labels, with paid per-review pricing and PayPal/credit-card payment.
- Stereofox was verified through the official Stereofox site and contact/submission page. The site exposes Submit Music navigation, current editorial and playlist activity, electronic/chill-electronica playlist context and instructs artists to use SubmitHub or Groover rather than email or Discord.
- EARMILK was verified through the official EARMILK homepage, Submit Music page and Contact page. The site shows active July 2026 publishing, genre coverage including Dance/Electronic/Experimental/Hip-Hop/Indie/Pop/Rock, accepts music and premiere submissions through SubmitHub or Pillargram, and explicitly filters music sent through the general contact form. A public business/brand-partnership email was observed but it is not a music-submission inbox.

The three campaign platforms require human review for account/app sessions, campaign setup, paid budget/payment choices, route selection, targeting, rights and campaign terms. The two publication routes require manual review of external SubmitHub/Groover/Pillargram account boundaries, free versus paid credit options, pitch copy, premiere status, media assets and genre fit. No auto-submit route was selected.
