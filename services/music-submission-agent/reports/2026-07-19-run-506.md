## 1. Bass Darkness

Official route: `https://www.bassdarkness.com/submissions`.

Bass Darkness is a Germany-based independent label focused on Phonk, Bass and dark, heavy-driven electronic music. Its first-party form requests:

- artist name(s), email and track title;
- a private SoundCloud or Dropbox link;
- finished tracks rather than rough drafts or WIPs;
- optional notes;
- Discord and Instagram contact information.

The page states that the A&R team reviews submissions and normally responds within several days. If the form confirmation is not received, it explicitly authorizes `demo@bassdarkness.com` as a fallback.

`demo@bassdarkness.com` is first-party published, syntactically valid, domain-aligned and explicitly authorized for that fallback purpose. The form remains the canonical route. No mailbox probing was performed.

Activity is current. The official homepage lists **SPARK** dated **3 July 2026**, **GLITCH (RXMALIAN REMIX)** dated **19 June 2026**, and the forthcoming **Fade Away** dated **24 July 2026**.

Manual review is required for exact catalogue fit, finished-track status, private-link access, social contact fields, rights and samples, Content ID, AI provenance, privacy, hidden form controls, fallback-email use and any post-selection agreement.

- a streaming link with download access, such as a private SoundCloud link;
- no email attachments;

Activity is current. The first-party homepage was updated on **10 June 2026**, the official SoundCloud published tracks from the **Mindfield EP** on **6 March 2026**, and **V: Black Venom Cycle** was released by Boomslang Recordings on **13 February 2026**.

Manual review is required for neurofunk/DnB fit, streaming/download permissions, the external Google Form, rights and samples, Content ID, AI provenance, privacy, platform terms, anti-spam controls and any post-selection agreement.

## 3. Indie Global 365

Official route: `https://indieglobal365.com/music-submission/`.

Indie Global 365 publishes a free first-party airplay upload form for independent Hip-Hop, R&B and Caribbean music, including Reggae and Soca. It asks for:

- artist name, email and song title;
- up to two MP3 songs;
- one image per song;
- 192kbps MP3 files;
- a stated maximum of 10MB per song, while the upload widget displays a 25MB overall limit;
- artist-and-title file naming;
- positive, uplifting, motivational, conscious or feel-good content;
- acceptance of the linked Terms & Conditions.

The current submission page says the form is the only route. An older first-party information page instead publishes `indieglobal365@gmail.com` as the only route, while the linked terms describe station-specific or CD delivery. These conflicts are material. The terms also require performance/streaming-rights representations involving ASCAP/BMI, SoundExchange or direct rights grants.

The airplay route is explicitly free. A separate artist-membership plan is advertised at a one-time **USD 15** fee, but membership is not required for free submission.

`indieglobal365@gmail.com` is first-party published and syntactically valid, but it is a free-mail address and conflicts with the current form-only instruction. It was retained as a legacy contact and not queued separately.

Activity was verified on **19 July 2026** through the active current-song/live-radio site and open free submission form. The platform states that it was established in 2024.

Manual and legal review is required for genre and positive-content fit, the Afro-Pop eligibility inconsistency, upload limits, image requirements, the mandatory terms, conflicting routes, rights and broadcast/streaming representations, Content ID, AI provenance, privacy, optional membership and final upload.
