## Verification notes

- KEXP official Contact and Submission Guidelines pages list `md@kexp.org` for music rotation consideration and ask for streaming/download WAV links, focus tracks, release dates, FCC-clean radio edits, lyric sheet, credits and bio/one-sheet. The official route states there is no spin guarantee, no physical submissions through this channel, and no pay-to-play.
- The Line of Best Fit official Contact page welcomes music from new and unsigned artists and links to a public Google Form. The form requests one track link only, artist/contact fields, genre, short bio and optional newsletter preference.
- Music Xray public source material identifies the service as an active A&R/song-to-opportunity platform where artists submit songs to industry professionals; public source material describes free membership but paid guaranteed-listen/response submissions.
- ReverbNation public source material identifies the service as an active BandLab-owned independent-music website and social/artist-services platform with optional registration, artist tools, promotion, distribution and fan-management workflows.
- Broadjam public source material identifies the service as an independent-musician community with contests, reviews, radio-play submissions and licensing opportunities for film, TV, ads and games; direct official-site parsing was access-limited in this run, so it remains manual.
