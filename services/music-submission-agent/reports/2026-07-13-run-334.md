## Verification and enrichment

### KEXP

- Authorized route: KEXP's official contact page directs music for rotation consideration to Music Director Chris Sanley and the Music Department at `md@kexp.org`.
- Delivery requirements: the official guidelines request streaming and WAV download links and prohibit attachments. They also request artist names and pronouns, release title and label where applicable, pronunciation guidance, focus tracks, release dates, clean versions for FCC-restricted content, a lyric sheet, credits, a bio or one-sheet and relevant Pacific Northwest or Bay Area dates.
- Scope: KEXP states that its rotation includes singles and full albums or EPs and represents an eclectic, diverse range of new music for a global community.
- Activity: the official website displayed current new-music reviews, podcasts and public in-studio sessions scheduled for July 22–30, 2026.
- Cost and access: the digital email route has no stated account, login, CAPTCHA or submission fee. KEXP explicitly states that airplay cannot be purchased and that spins or responses are not guaranteed.
- Physical route: KEXP says not to send physical submissions to the music department.
- Business-email validation: `md@kexp.org` has valid syntax, matches the official domain and is explicitly published for music consideration.
- Manual boundary: a human must select the appropriate MarcsMusic release, verify rights and release status, prepare the requested links and metadata, approve personal data and send the email manually.

### KUTX

- Authorized route: KUTX's official Submit Your Music page directs on-air rotation submissions to Music Director Rick McNulty at `music@kutx.org`.
- Subject and materials: the requested subject is `Music Submission – [Your Artist Name]`. The email should contain a streaming link, a downloadable WAV link preferably through Dropbox or Google Drive, a concise bio, location, release notes, tour information and collaborations. MP3 attachments are prohibited.
- Eligibility and quality: KUTX asks for final mastered tracks rather than rough demos, favours new or upcoming releases, requires clean radio versions where FCC rules apply and asks senders of multiple tracks to identify their top pick.
- Genre fit: the official page lists separate specialist programmes, including Soundfounder for electronic and experimental music. Current July 2026 programming included electronic and beat music from Austin and around the world, Latin music, hip-hop and general new-release programming.
- Locality: Austin and Texas artists may mark locality in the subject, but the published rotation route is not stated as local-only.
- Cost and access: no account, login, CAPTCHA, submission fee or mandatory payment is stated for the core email route. Paid event sponsorship and invitation-only session routes are separate and were not treated as music-submission alternatives.
- Business-email validation: `music@kutx.org` has valid syntax, matches the official domain and is explicitly published for music consideration.
- Manual boundary: a human must confirm programme fit and release status, verify rights, prepare the approved stream and WAV links, approve the pitch and personal data and send the email manually.
