## Research notes

- Fresh On The Net publishes an official weekly Send Us A Track route, currently shows its inbox closed, limits submissions to one track per artist per week, requires public worldwide availability and excludes AI-generated tracks.
- Obscure Sound publishes an official Contact/Submissions page with SubmitHub, SubmitHub Marketplace, MusoSoup and direct email routes; email was redacted by the fetch layer and was not decoded.
- The Other Side Reviews publishes an official Contact route, recommends MusoSoup to guarantee a listen and also exposes a direct contact route; the contact was redacted and was not decoded.
- Under the Radar Magazine publishes a Submissions department route, but contact values are JavaScript-protected and were not decoded.
- The Line of Best Fit welcomes music from new and unsigned artists and links to a public Google Form with required artist, genre, track link and track title fields.
