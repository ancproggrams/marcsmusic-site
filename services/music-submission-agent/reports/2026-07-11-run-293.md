## Verification and enrichment

- CJSW: the official 2026 Jotform accepts EPs and albums only, not singles. The full release must contain at least three songs and be uploaded as MP3 files in a ZIP. The form requests release metadata, genre, location, biography, RIYL information, artwork, an optional one-sheet and permission to share the music with programmers and retain it in the station library. Its anti-spam field must remain empty.
- CKUT: the official route accepts 320-kbps MP3 submissions at `music@ckut.ca` and physical CDs, cassettes and vinyl. Singles are rejected; except for 7-inch vinyl, releases need at least three distinct tracks and remixes do not count. CKUT says recordings arrive from artists and labels around the world.
- PBS FM: the official form explicitly welcomes local and international acts and asks for artist details, biography, social links, a stream and a required free high-quality download. Spotify and Deezer links are rejected. The form contains both a blank anti-spam field and a CAPTCHA. CDs and vinyl are also accepted with a one-page press release or biography.
- RTRFM: the official form includes an Overseas option and requests contact data, an uploaded biography, social links, gigs, a stream and a free download link. The page recommends WAV or high-quality MP3 and forbids attaching audio to email. A hidden validation field must remain unchanged. Protected Music Team and Music Director email links are shown but were not decoded.
- fbi.radio: the official digital route is `music@fbiradio.com`. A compliant email includes a clear subject, short biography, press image or gig details, a stream and a free 320-kbps MP3 or WAV download. General playlist servicing is auditioned on Wednesdays, and physical CDs are no longer accepted.
