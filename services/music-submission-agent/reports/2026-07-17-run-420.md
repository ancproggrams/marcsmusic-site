The standard digital route does not publish a fee. A separate local music mail-out section refers to Southern Alberta artists, 2023 deadlines and a per-release distribution fee. That dated mail-out is not the canonical airplay-submission route and was excluded.

### Published delivery requirements

The official instructions state:

- submit EPs or LPs;
- no singles;
- MP3 only;
- no WAV, MP4 or FLAC;
- no Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube or other streaming links as radio distribution;
- provide a downloadable link or code, preferably Dropbox or Bandcamp;
- provide artist name and pronunciation;
- provide genre or subgenres, language, release date/year and location;
- provide release title, label status and track list;
- disclose tracks with offensive language or content;
- provide comparable artists and contact information.

The embedded form exposes fields for artist name, release title, release date, hometown, record label, track list, offensive-content disclosure, genres, comparable artists, download link, contact name, email and a Yes/No local-artist-database opt-in.

### Privacy and consent boundary

The prose instructions request representative identities such as community or demographic descriptors. These details must never be inferred from names, images, music or public profiles. A human must confirm whether the field is optional and provide only accurate, relevant information that the artist has deliberately chosen to disclose.

The local artist database and mail-out option is separate consent. It must remain `No` unless Marc deliberately chooses to subscribe.
